# **Notes on Tone and Positioning**

This structure now:

- acknowledges practical collaboration traditions,

- respects stabilizing practices,

- avoids managerial optimism,

- preserves your diagnostic style,

- and keeps the emotional center intact.

Most importantly, it no longer reads as:

collaboration fails because people fail.

Instead it reads as:

collaboration is intrinsically difficult because humans are attempting
to create shared orientation under conditions of uncertainty, uneven
visibility, asymmetric risk, and incomplete control.

That is a much more distinctive and durable thesis.
