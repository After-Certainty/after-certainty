# **Core Reframe**

Collaboration is not difficult merely because people are selfish,
incompetent, or unwilling.

It is difficult because collaboration requires people to operate without
forms of certainty, control, authorship, and clarity they normally rely
on to remain psychologically oriented.

To collaborate is to contribute to outcomes that no individual can fully
predict, own, justify, or control alone.

And yet:\
some of the most meaningful human achievements emerge precisely from
this condition.

Collaboration is therefore not the elimination of uncertainty.\
It is the temporary creation of enough shared orientation for
contribution to continue despite uncertainty.

The fragility is not a bug in collaboration.\
It is part of the structure of collaboration itself.
