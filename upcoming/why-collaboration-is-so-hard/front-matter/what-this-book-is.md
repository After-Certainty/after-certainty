# **What This Book Is**

This book is not:

- a teamwork playbook,

- a leadership manual,

- a productivity framework,

- or a moral argument that people simply need to “collaborate better.”

It is:

- a lens for seeing collaboration,

- a vocabulary for diagnosing instability,

- a description of what collaborative practices stabilize,

- and an examination of why collaboration remains difficult even among
  competent people acting in good faith.

The aim is not to prescribe virtue.\
It is to increase clarity.
