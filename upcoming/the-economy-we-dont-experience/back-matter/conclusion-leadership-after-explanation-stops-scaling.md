# **Conclusion**

## **Leadership After Explanation Stops Scaling**

- Why there may be no return to cleanly scalable economic explanation

- Interpretive breakdown as the deeper democratic threat

- Why misinformation is not the only — or deepest — problem

- Leadership virtues under compression:

  - humility

  - memory

  - restraint

- Why avoided disasters cannot justify themselves politically

- The distinction between persuasion and preserving legitimacy

- What kind of durability remains possible after certainty fractures

- Final claim:

  - leadership is no longer about achieving agreement

  - it is about preserving meaning long enough for disagreement to
    remain bearable
