## Chapter title

Chapter 6 — The Athlete Who Said No

## Governing question

Who has authority to refuse the burden?

## Purpose in the book’s progression

**Conceptual role: Agency and situated knowledge.** After showing how institutions moralize carried risk, ask who may refuse and on what epistemic grounds.

## Ordinary opening image

A competition continuing without the athlete everyone expected to see; absence as a public act.

## Central mechanism

Presumptive authority for the person who carries the most direct consequence of error, without claiming that athlete judgment is infallible.

## Primary case or case structure

Simone Biles at the Tokyo Olympics.

## Supporting cases

- Naomi Osaka
- Andrew Luck
- athletes refusing to play through concussions or injuries
- players leaving before audiences believed they should

## Key distinctions

**Institution / team knowledge:** commitments made; effects on teammates; competitive consequences; collective preparation; symbolic and institutional stakes.

**Athlete knowledge:** pain; disorientation; bodily control; fear; movement felt internally; direct consequence if the judgment is wrong.

## Necessary complication or counterexample

Do not replace institutional authority with an equally absolute claim that the athlete always knows best.

**Working principle:** The athlete’s judgment should not be treated as infallible, but it deserves presumptive authority because the athlete possesses unique knowledge and carries the most direct consequence of error.

## Research questions

- What did Biles and surrounding institutions say in real time vs later?
- How were Osaka and Luck framed contemporaneously regarding obligation and refusal?
- Which supporting injury-refusal cases are documented without relying on rumor?

## Evidence risks

Moderate — living athletes, mental health reporting norms, and retrospective canonization require careful dating and scoped claims. Avoid mind-reading.

## Possible return image

The competition continues without the athlete everyone expected to see. The absence becomes one of the event’s most visible acts.

## Approved compression lines

- The audience can see the size of the moment. Only the athlete can feel the condition of the body entering it.
