## Chapter title

Chapter 7 — When a Gesture Becomes Another Message

## Governing question

What does the event become after it enters public memory?

## Purpose in the book’s progression

**Conceptual role: Meaning, signal, compression, and retrospective interpretation.** Close the life-of-a-result sequence by showing how public memory remakes the event without making every interpretation equally grounded.

## Ordinary opening image

A photograph of kneeling that outlives the game; argument already elsewhere.

## Central mechanism

Speakers do not retain complete control over meaning after a signal enters public life. Loss of control is not evidence-free chaos.

## Primary case or case structure

Colin Kaepernick — precise chronology before any narrative.

## Supporting cases

- Muhammad Ali
- Tommie Smith and John Carlos
- Allen Iverson’s “practice” press conference as a smaller example of one repeated signal replacing surrounding context

## Key distinctions

- Intended meaning vs later public compressions
- Grounded interpretation vs evidence-free attribution
- Signal without total speaker control vs “any meaning goes”

## Necessary complication or counterexample

Do not turn the chapter into a verdict on the protest itself or assume that intended meaning is the only valid meaning.

## Research questions (chronology first)

1. The original concern
2. The original form of protest
3. Consultation and change in form
4. Kaepernick’s direct explanation
5. NFL and team responses
6. Media framing
7. Opposing public compressions
8. Supporting public compressions
9. Material consequences
10. Later reinterpretation and partial canonization

Also: how Ali / Smith–Carlos / Iverson illustrate signal capture without equating their cases.

## Evidence risks

**High.** Sequence errors, attributed motives, and unsupported claims about “what people thought” are acute risks. Build dated source log before prose. No invented quotations.

## Possible return image

The athlete remains kneeling in the photograph while the argument moves far beyond the field.

## Approved compression lines

- Meaning leaves the speaker, but it does not enter a world without evidence.
