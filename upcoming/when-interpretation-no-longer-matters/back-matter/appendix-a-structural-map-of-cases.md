# **Appendix A**

## **A Structural Map of the Cases**

## Why This Appendix Exists

This book deliberately avoids explanation through belief, motive, or
character. As a result, it demands sustained attention to structure.
This appendix provides a way to re-orient without reopening those
explanatory frames.

Use it as:

- a reference while reading

- a reset point when returning to the book

- a way to distinguish cases without ranking them

The work of the book remains diagnostic. This map simply makes that
diagnosis easier to hold.

For term definitions, see [Glossary](glossary.md).
