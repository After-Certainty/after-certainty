# **Conclusion**

## **After Interpretation**

It allows judgment to stop masquerading as intervention. And it allows
responsibility to be exercised without illusion.

The work, then, is not to restore interpretation everywhere.

It is to recognize where it still matters, where it has been displaced,
and where its absence defines the conditions under which authority now
operates.

That recognition does not solve the problem of authority.

It clarifies it.
