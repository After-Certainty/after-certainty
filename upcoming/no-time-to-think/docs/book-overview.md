## Short premise

Speed is not inherently careless. But thought is not simply an individual virtue people can summon on command. Judgment depends on conditions. *No Time to Think* examines what happens when production accelerates faster than the system’s capacity to understand, verify, integrate, maintain, and care for what it produces.

## Core question

What social and institutional conditions must exist before thinking is actually possible?

## Governing claim

> A faster step does not necessarily create a faster system. It often relocates the constraint.

Judgment does not merely require intelligent people. It requires conditions in which intelligence has time to encounter consequence.

## Book-level ordinary object

A green “Merge” button at the bottom of a software pull request. The scarce resource is no longer production; it is the human attention required to decide whether the change should exist.

## Sentence if finished

Every organization decides where judgment will live. If it never makes that decision consciously, speed will make it accidentally.
