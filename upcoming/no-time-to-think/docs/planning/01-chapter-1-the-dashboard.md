## Chapter title

Chapter 1 — The Dashboard

## Governing question

What do productivity metrics teach an organization to pursue when generation becomes cheap?

## Ordinary opening image

A productivity dashboard with arrows pointing upward.

## Case / terrain

Research on AI coding assistants continued to find real productivity gains alongside substantial variation by setting and disagreement about what should count as productivity. Some 2026 studies argued that immediate output measures are insufficient without considering comprehension, technical expertise, ownership, maintainability, and learning.

## Conceptual movement

Metrics do not merely observe work. They teach an organization what to pursue. When the easiest measurements are commits, tickets, documents, messages, or time saved, the organization may accelerate production while degrading context and capability.

## Questions to preserve

- Which gains appear immediately, and which costs arrive later?
- How should ownership, comprehension, and maintainability appear in a productivity account?
- What happens when leaders promise gains before agreeing on what productivity means?
- How does measurement convert possibility into pressure?
