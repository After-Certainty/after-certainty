# No Time to Think — Research Plan

Structured roadmap for research. Not full chapter research. **No chapter prose should be drafted from this document alone.**

## Evidence standards

- Prefer primary or institutional sources for specific factual claims.
- Verify memorable anecdotes before using them.
- Distinguish documented facts from interpretation.
- Reverify every 2026 event before publication; studies, regulatory actions, and company outcomes may change.
- Treat historical parallels as structural comparisons, not claims that technologies or harms are identical.
- Write current events for the mechanism they reveal, not for the durability of a company claim, study headline, or regulatory outcome.

## Guardrails from outline

- Do not collapse into “AI is bad” or “companies move too fast.”
- Preserve examples where speed, automation, and standardization improve life.
- Distinguish a moved bottleneck from a quality failure.
- Avoid claiming that stable near-term metrics prove long-term safety—or that they prove hidden harm.
- Follow work downstream into review, integration, training, maintenance, support, and care.
- Ask who absorbs the work that disappeared from the dashboard.
- Treat time as an institutional resource, not merely an individual habit.

## Evidence layers

For every central case, separate:

1. **What happened** (documented events)
2. **How it was interpreted at the time**
3. **How the meaning changed later**
4. **What the pattern reveals** (author analysis—clearly marked)
