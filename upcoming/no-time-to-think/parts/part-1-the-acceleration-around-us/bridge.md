# **Part I**

## **The Acceleration Around Us**

The present makes the shifted bottleneck visible.

Production becomes cheaper. Organizations interpret new capacity as an obligation to produce more. Work piles up downstream in review, integration, coordination, maintenance, training, and care. Dashboards measure what became countable. Fragility often lives in what the dashboard never asked to see.

Human judgment is easy to treat as delay precisely when it becomes the scarce control point.
