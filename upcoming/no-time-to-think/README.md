## Premise

Speed is not inherently careless. Urgency can be real. Automation can remove toil. But judgment depends on conditions: time, attention, consequence contact, stable context, permission to pause, and institutional slack for a second look. *No Time to Think* examines what happens when production accelerates faster than the system’s capacity to understand, verify, integrate, maintain, and care for what it produces.

## Governing claim

> A faster step does not necessarily create a faster system. It often relocates the constraint.

Judgment does not merely require intelligent people. It requires conditions in which intelligence has time to encounter consequence.

## Companion title

*The Case That Does Not Fit* asks whether an institution can reconsider a category that failed a particular case. *No Time to Think* asks whether the institution still possesses the systemic capacity—time, attention, context, review, and stop authority—to reconsider anything at all.
