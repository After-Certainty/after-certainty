# **Conclusion**

## **Uncertainty as a Discipline**

Closing posture:

- Uncertainty is not a failure state

- It is a condition that must be practiced

- Absolutes are tempting; discipline is harder

- The future depends less on what we predict than on how we reason when
  predictions fail

Final note to the reader:

This book does not ask you to abandon conviction.

It asks you to grow it up.
