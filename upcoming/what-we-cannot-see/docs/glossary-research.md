# What We Cannot See — Glossary (Research)

Term candidates for planning. Manuscript glossary lives in [`back-matter/glossary.md`](../back-matter/glossary.md) when drafting begins.

## Core terms

### Perspective

A situated way of noticing and interpreting—necessarily partial because finite.

### Partial perspective

A perspective that reveals some features and obscures others; not a defective perspective.

### Bias

How finite minds allocate limited attention. In this book: adaptive allocation, not primarily moral failure.

**Guardrail:** Distinguish from colloquial "bias = prejudice only."

### Attention budget

Metaphor for finite selective intake—what we notice costs what we do not.

### Judgment

The responsible use of limited perspectives when deciding under uncertainty.

**Not:** neutrality, debiasing, or certainty-seeking.

### Integration

Constructing a larger model that explains why multiple partial perspectives each noticed something important.

**Not:** compromise, averaging, false equivalence.

### Neutrality (myth of)

The idea that a perspective-free view is achievable—rejected in this book.

### Uncertainty

Condition of decision, not failure of character or effort.

## Bias families

See [`bias-taxonomy.md`](bias-taxonomy.md): Attention, Memory, Interpretation, Identity, Trust, Temporal, Incentive, Structural, Technological.

## Integration domains

Collaboration, science, engineering, medicine, democracy, organizational learning, AI—each a venue for integration, not synonymous with it.
