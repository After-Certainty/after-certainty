## What this book is not

- A debiasing handbook — unbiased is not a state finite minds occupy
- An argument that all views are equally accurate, or that every disagreement is equally valid
- A culture-war manuscript — assumes people adapt to limited information
- A trust guide, diversity guide, or collaboration manual — touches adjacent ground but steps back to foundational epistemics
