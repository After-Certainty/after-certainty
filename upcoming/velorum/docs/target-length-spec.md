**Do not confuse the two bars:**

- **Calibration bar** = how chapters should *read* during alignment passes (consequences over mechanics, cut thesis closers).
- **Target length spec** = how much *story mass* each act should carry at publication scale (~100k words).

A chapter can meet the calibration bar and still be **under** its act target until a later expansion pass adds scene depth without padding.

---

## Design principle: middle expands, ending compresses

Velorum is **not** a flat ~3,500 w/ch novel. Acts have different jobs; word budget follows **narrative function**, not even division by chapter count.

| Pattern | Why |
|---------|-----|
| **Acts II–III longest** | Reader must believe the bond might be *worth it* before the break takes it away; Kestrel Gate / Lyra / public collapse need room |
| **Act IV tightens** | Consequences and confrontation; less place-braid, more forward pressure |
| **Act V shortest** | Payoff, revelation, resolution — reader already knows bond, city, philosophy, stakes |

**Common mistake to avoid:** 3,500-word setup chapters *and* 3,500-word resolution chapters — endings feel slow when the finale does not compress.

## How to use this spec

### Expansion passes

1. Pick act → compare **current total** to **target range**.
2. Identify chapters **under** their act average with **thin beats** in **act-chapter-index**.
3. Expand **scene mass** (arrival, aftermath, relationship pressure, place) — not lore lectures or duplicate bond proofs.
4. **Act V:** expand only until act total approaches **~10k**; do not inflate finale toward Act III density.

### Compression passes

1. If an act exceeds its **acceptable range**, cut via **revision/07-scene-compression** and calibration checklist (duplicate proofs, thesis closers).
2. **Act IV–V:** prefer compression over expansion unless a mandatory beat is missing.

### Agents

- **Agent 01** (expansion): use when a chapter is **under ~1,200 words** *or* **under its act planning target** with missing beats — see **[agents/initial-drafting/01-chapter-expansion-depth-pass.md](./agents/initial-drafting/01-chapter-expansion-depth-pass.md)**.
- **Calibration agents (03–09):** apply regardless of length; length follows in a separate pass.

## Act-by-act editorial notes

### Act I — The Choice (~20k)

Meeting Riven and Cael, Greyhaven world, curse, forced decision. Readers need **orientation** — favor place-first and road dynamic over rushing the summit.

### Act II — The Gift (~25k)

Curse appears **beneficial**. Shared Load, Road to Kestrel Gate, Magistrate — reader must genuinely believe *maybe this is worth it* before Act III takes things away.

### Act III — The Break (~27k)

**Emotional core.** Lyra, No Privacy, Attack, Aftermath (in current index: **14–20** continues the fracture). Identity fractures, relationships strain, curse becomes dangerous. **Longest act at full scale.**

### Act IV — The Truth (~18k)

Halverin exit, Seeker, Fractured Lands, Velorum truth. Confrontation and consequences — **tightens** after the middle expansion.

### Act V — The Legend (~10k)

Payoff without re-explaining bond or philosophy. **Short, sharp** chapters; acceleration through the end.
