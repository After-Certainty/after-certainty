# **Chapter 4**

## **Targets Without Judgment**

Domain: Climate policy & corporate responsibility

Central question:

What does it mean to act morally when harm is global, delayed, and
uncertain?

Structure

1.  Original moral aim: harm reduction

2.  Why judgment paralyzes coordination

3.  Incentive substitution: targets, offsets, reporting frameworks

4.  Moral residue: displacement, accounting without clarity

5.  Why seriousness replaces responsibility

Example anchors:

- Net-zero commitments

- Carbon offset markets

- ESG reporting
