## Structure

### Fourteen chapters — right length?

**Proposal:** Keep 14. Part II (six practice chapters) is the book's core; compressing would thin comparative depth.

**Alternative:** Merge Ch 7–8 (memory + conflict) or Ch 8–9 (conflict + suffering) — not recommended without author approval.

### One dominant historical anchor per chapter?

**Proposal:** Yes for Part II — one scene or historical episode carries the chapter; other traditions enter comparatively without becoming a survey.

### Comparative table in body, appendix, or planning-only?

**Proposal:** Planning-only ([`comparative-map.md`](comparative-map.md)). Ch 12 may include a **shortened** reader-facing table (4–5 rows) if prose requires it; full table stays in docs.
