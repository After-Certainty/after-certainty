# When Accountability No Longer Expires — Book Rules

## Purpose

Architectural constraints for **When Accountability No Longer Expires** (*How Legitimate Authority Learns to Survive Harm*).

## Book scope and structure

### Target length

- Full essay book (~70–100k words at completion; current draft ~12.3k)
- 4 parts, 12 chapters, 2 bridges, interlude, appendix

### Markdown file structure

- Bridges required in Part III and Part IV (`bridge.md` per part).
- Interlude lives in `front-matter/` per `index.md` reading order.

## Core invariant (non-negotiable)

> Legitimate authority can learn to survive harm without renewing moral standing—adaptation, correction, and warning can substitute for consequence until legitimacy feels permanent from the inside.

## Thematic arc by part

- **Part I — Permanence of Legitimacy:** Authority without expiration; sacralization and inheritance.
- **Part II — Harm After Accountability:** Harm without villains; distribution of suffering; moral drift.
- **Part III — Learning Without Resolution:** Adaptation, correction, wisdom-from-inside, warning vs learning, stable equilibrium.
- **Part IV — Beyond Any One Institution:** Cross-domain pattern; non-inevitability.

## Tone and positioning

### This book must not be

- Institutional dunking or partisan exposure
- Blame-the-leader moralism
- Reform checklist advocacy

### This book must be

- Structural and cross-domain
- Sympathetic to inside-view experience without excusing harm
- Clear on distinction between accountability and blame (see appendix)

## Chapter construction

Scene or institutional opening → structural analysis → cross-domain parallel → return to invariant → optional pull-quote.

## Bridges

- Part III bridge must set up learning-without-resolution arc.
- Part IV bridge must transition from single-institution cases to pattern.

## Citation and glossary

- Citation pass during Phase 4 for structural pivots.
- No standalone glossary yet.
