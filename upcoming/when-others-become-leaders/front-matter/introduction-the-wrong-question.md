# Introduction — The Wrong Question

Most leadership books ask: how do I become a leader?

This book asks: what kind of leader leaves behind more leaders?

Some influence accumulates. It creates dependence, expands the leader’s centrality, and leaves a vacuum when the leader exits.

Other influence circulates. It increases the capacity of others to act, care, organize, and judge without waiting for instructions from the center. It survives through practices, institutions, relationships, and norms carried by people who were once treated as audience.

This introduction is intentionally brief. The chapters do not begin by announcing a framework and then searching for examples. They begin in ordinary life and let patterns emerge slowly, through evidence, conflict, and consequence.
