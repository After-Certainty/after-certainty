# What This Book Is

Most leadership books ask how a person becomes a leader. This book asks a different question: what kind of leader leaves behind more leaders?

Across nine chapters, each anchored in an ordinary object or social scene, the book studies influence that **circulates** through people, practices, and institutions instead of accumulating around one figure. The chapters are not biographies and not a ranking of exemplary lives. Each figure is treated as evidence for a broader human pattern, including tensions, contradictions, and limitations.
