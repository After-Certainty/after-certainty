# When Others Become Leaders — Bibliography Guide

Working bibliography for Phase 1 citation integrity. Export list: [`../back-matter/bibliography.md`](../back-matter/bibliography.md).

## Citation discipline

- Footnotes anchor **structural pivots** and **specific factual claims**, not interpretive synthesis.
- Prefer primary texts, institutional records, and standard scholarly monographs.
- Distinguish gospel narrative from historical reconstruction in Ch. 1.
- Contemporary reporting (Swift/Eras) cited to named outlets; figures may shift slightly as reporting updates.

**Target density:** ~50–70 footnote markers across the manuscript; most chapters carry 3–14 notes at verified pivots.
