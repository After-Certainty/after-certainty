## Chapter title

Chapter 7 — The Doctor’s Note

## Governing question

What changed when accommodation became a right rather than a favor?

## Ordinary opening image

A short note requesting that an ordinary requirement be modified.

## Case / terrain

The movement from charitable exceptions toward disability rights and enforceable accommodation. Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973 prohibited disability discrimination in federally funded programs, and the Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990 extended a broader civil-rights framework across employment, public services, public accommodations, and telecommunications.

## Conceptual movement

Accommodation changed the moral meaning of an exception. The different arrangement was no longer understood merely as kindness granted to an unusual person. It could be part of what equal participation required.

## Questions to preserve

- What changed when accommodation became a right rather than a favor?
- How does “reasonable accommodation” preserve judgment while constraining denial?
- When does documentation protect legitimacy, and when does it force people to repeatedly prove their vulnerability?

## Return image

The note does not ask the institution to abandon its purpose. It asks the institution to distinguish its purpose from its default method.
