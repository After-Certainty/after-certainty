title: "Leave a Little Room"
slug: leave-a-little-room

---

A1
You heard the sigh before the sentence,
I saw the shadow cross your face.
And in the hush between us, darling,
we put a story in its place.


So leave a little room for maybe,
leave a little room for me.
The heart is quick to close a doorway
before the eyes can learn to see.


A2
You know the words I've used before you,
I know the ones that made you leave.
Tonight they wear old clothes around them,
and we believe what we believe.


So leave a little room for silence,
not the kind that turns away—
just enough to keep one question
from becoming yesterday.


B
We bring every old goodbye
to every new goodnight.
A half-remembered hurt
can make a whisper sound just right


for the story
we were ready to believe.


But darling, stay a moment
before you leave.


A3
Let me be more than what you noticed,
more than what you feared I meant.
Some meanings move while we are talking;
some arrive when words are spent.


So leave a little room between us—
not for distance, not for doubt.
Just enough for what I meant, dear,
to find its own way out.


Tag
Leave a little room, darling,
before you call it true.
