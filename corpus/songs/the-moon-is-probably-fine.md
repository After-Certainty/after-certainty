[Verse 1]
The moon looked wrong at half past three
A little low, a little green
I stood outside in stocking feet
Like someone ought to intervene


The neighbor’s television glowed
A dog was barking down the block
I checked the sky again and thought
Well, maybe moons just do that stuff


[Chorus]
The moon is probably fine
It’s been doing this a long, long time
Turning slowly in the dark
Without consulting anyone


The moon is probably fine
And I am probably fine
And everything we cannot fix tonight
Can keep revolving for a while


[Verse 2]
I read somewhere it’s moving off
An inch or two each passing year
Which seems dramatic till you learn
How long an inch can disappear


The kitchen clock said 3:14
The refrigerator changed its tone
For half a second I was sure
We were the last two things awake


[Chorus]
The moon is probably fine
It’s been doing this a long, long time
Pulling oceans through the dark
Without explaining what it means


The moon is probably fine
And you are probably fine
And every little catastrophe
Doesn’t need an answer tonight


[Instrumental — Mellotron choir, warped organ, tape flutter]


[Bridge]
Maybe somewhere something broke
Maybe somewhere something bloomed
Maybe someone changed their mind

Maybe all the satellites

Look at that

[Final Chorus]
The moon is probably fine
Still hanging crooked in the sky
Doing whatever moons have done
Before we ever gave them names


The moon is probably fine
And we are probably fine
Not certain
Not exactly


But fine enough
To leave the curtains open


Fine enough
To let the night keep going


Fine enough
For now
