[Verse 1]


They drew a line across the wall
and called the matter closed
White paint dried beneath the lamps
where no one ever goes


The level slept inside its case
the numbers all agreed
But something underneath the white
was moving with the heat


[Refrain]


The grain remains
The grain remains


[Instrumental]


[Verse 2]


There’s a shadow where the picture hung
a key without a door
A name beneath another name
beneath another more


Someone filled the little holes
and swept away the dust
But every winter finds the place
that summer learned to trust


[Refrain]


The grain remains
The grain remains


[Instrumental — guitar]


[Verse 3]


The crack appeared three rooms away
thin as a strand of hair
No one heard the timber turn
No one saw it there


They measured from the painted edge
and found the measure true
The wall was square
The line was straight
The crack went somewhere new


[Bridge]


Under the paint
under the name
under the number
under the same


Something remembers
the shape of the rain


Something remembers


[Long instrumental passage]


[Final Verse]


Morning found the picture straight
the hallway clean and still
Light came through the eastern glass
and climbed across the sill


No alarms
No warning lights
No reason to explain


Only a corner lifting white


and underneath—


the grain


[Outro]


The grain remains


The grain remains


The grain remains


For now.
