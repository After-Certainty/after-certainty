---
title: "Somebody, Specifically"
slug: somebody-specifically
languages: ["en"]
---

[Verse 1]


From up on the hill
the whole city glows


A thousand little windows
all looking much the same


Come a little closer
one light stays on late


Someone’s waiting for a phone call
someone’s whispering a name


[Chorus]


Somebody, specifically
is awake behind that light


Missing someone
holding someone
trying to make it through the night


From far away we blur together
close enough, we don’t


Somebody, specifically
is living where you won’t


[Verse 2]


A crowd becomes a movement
a neighborhood, a shade


But every door you pass
has a thousand things unsaid


Someone laughs too loudly
someone hasn’t slept


Someone keeps a promise
no one else remembers yet


[Bridge]


Distance makes us simple


Come closer


We’re not.


[Final Chorus]


Somebody, specifically
is awake behind that light


Not an example
not a symbol
just a life


From far away we blur together
close enough, we don’t


Somebody, specifically


Someone you could know.
