---
title: "The Blue Cup"
slug: the-blue-cup
languages: ["en"]
---

[Verse 1]
You bought that ugly blue cup
at a roadside place in June
With a painted yellow rooster
and a crack shaped like the moon
I said, “Darling, it’s atrocious”
you said, “That’s why it came with me”
Now it sits beside the window
like some minor deity


[Verse 2]
We were younger in the kitchen
we were louder in those days
You could turn a missing teaspoon
into seventeen complaints
I could make a little kingdom
out of every wounded pride
Then we’d sleep like two republics
with a border down the side


[Chorus]
Oh, the things that get remembered
aren’t the things we thought they’d be
Not the roses, not the speeches
not the grand photography
Just a rooster on a coffee cup
a coat left on a chair
And somebody saying, “Take mine”
when the cold gets in the air


[Verse 3]
Once in November, somewhere southward,
we got lost beyond the lake
You blamed the folded paper map
I blamed every turn you’d make
Then the rain came down in buckets
and the engine made a sound
You laughed until you scared me
then we pushed the damn thing out


[Verse 4]
Later underneath a service light
with grease across your sleeve
You said, “I know you’re mad at me”
I said, “I haven’t tried to leave”
And neither one was sorry
not in any useful way
But you gave me half your sandwich
and I gave you yesterday


[Chorus]
Oh, the things that get remembered
aren’t the things we thought they’d be
Not the roses, not the speeches
not the grand photography
Just your hand across the dashboard
when the tires start to slide
And the funny little bargains
that keep two fools side by side


[Bridge]
There were years I thought I knew you
like a house I’d memorized
Every staircase, every window
every place the daylight hides
Then you’d say some strange small sentence
and the walls would all move back
And I’d find another hallway
I had somehow never mapped


[Verse 5]
Now the cup has lost its handle
and the rooster’s nearly gone
You still drink from it on Sundays
like there’s nothing going on
I could buy you something better
I have threatened to for years
But I think I’d miss that rooster
if he finally disappeared


[Final Chorus]
So the things that get remembered
aren’t the things we thought they’d be
They’re the scraps we kept returning to
without a guarantee
A blue cup by the window
a wet coat on the stair
And somebody still beside you
when you turn and find them there


[Outro]
You bought that ugly blue cup
at a roadside place in June
There’s a crack across the rooster now
and another through the moon
