---
title: "I Believe You Tonight"
slug: i-believe-you-tonight
languages: ["en"]

[Verse 1]


You tell me the room is empty
I still look behind the door
You say there’s nothing coming
I’ve heard those words before


I know how doubt gets dressed up
How fear can sound polite
But your hand is on the table
And I believe you tonight


[Pre-Chorus]


Not forever
Not because
I found the answer
Or proved what was


Just for now
Let it be
You don’t have to promise
Anything to me


[Chorus]


I believe you tonight
Not because I know you’re right
Not because I’ve seen the light


I can leave tomorrow open
I can leave the future wide
I don’t need eternal truth
To believe you tonight


[Verse 2]


You tell me you were frightened
You tell me why you stayed
There are pieces of the story
That neither one can save


I could cross-examine every word
Until the morning light
Or let the silence hold us
And believe you tonight


[Bridge — sparse]


Maybe faith is only temporary
Maybe trust can have an hour
Maybe certainty is something
We keep mistaking for power


Maybe I don’t need to own you
Maybe you don’t need to prove
Every shadow in your history
Before I let you move


Closer


[Final Chorus]


I believe you tonight
Without a contract
Without a sign


Without forever
Without a why


Let tomorrow ask its questions
Let tomorrow change my mind
There are truths that only need
To live a little while


I believe you tonight


[Outro — whispered]


Tomorrow
I may ask again


Tonight
I believe you
