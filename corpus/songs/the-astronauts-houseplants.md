title: "The Astronaut’s Houseplants"

languages: ["en"]

[Verse 1]
There’s a thunderstorm over India
and a blue light blinking red
There’s a continent beneath me
turning slowly toward the bed


Mission control says I’m drifting
seven centimeters east
I say, “Did anybody call my neighbor?
She still has my spare key.”


[Pre-Chorus]
Because the fern was looking yellow
when I left on Tuesday night
And the little one beside it
never did like too much light


[Chorus]
I can see the whole world from here
every ocean, every cloud
I can watch the cities flicker
when the sun is going down


I can see where all the borders are
or where we say they ought to be
But I can’t see my houseplants
Can somebody check for me?


[Instrumental Break]


[Verse 2]
At 04:13 this morning
Houston lost me for a while
So I floated by the window
watching lightning on the Nile


And I thought about the spider plant
that kept making little clones
Dropping roots into the empty air
like it didn’t know it was alone


Maybe somebody has watered it
Maybe somebody forgot
Maybe life is mostly reaching
for whatever light we’ve got


[Bridge]
The Earth is very beautiful
when nobody’s talking back
Just weather moving over it
and daylight on the black


No arguments
No headlines
No reasons to be right


Just one ridiculous green planet
turning quietly through night


And somewhere in an upstairs window
if the radiator’s low
there are four small leaves unfolding


I would really like to know


[Final Chorus]
I can see the whole world from here
but the whole world’s not enough
There are things that disappear from view
the farther you get up


So when you call tomorrow morning
you don’t need to tell me much


Just say the fern is still alive
and someone turned it toward the sun


[Outro]
Mission control says I’m drifting
seven centimeters east


The plants are fine.


The plants are fine.


...


Okay.


Good.
