---
title: "What I Thought You Meant"
slug: what-i-thought-you-meant
languages: ["en"]
---

[Intro]


You didn’t say it
I felt it move
In the dark between me and you


[Verse 1]


You turned away when I touched your shoulder
Then leaned back into my hand
Your breath caught somewhere near my collar
And I thought I understood


Your eyes said stay
Your mouth said nothing
I made a promise out of that


Funny how close I was
To what I thought you meant


[Pre-Chorus]


Maybe I heard an old goodbye
In the way you said my name
Maybe I was reading every shiver
Like it had something to explain


[Chorus]


Tell me what you meant
Not what I thought you meant
Come a little closer
Let me hear it from your lips


Your body’s talking softly
But I don’t want to pretend
I know the difference
Between wanting me
And what I thought you meant


[Verse 2]


You said, “I’m tired”
With your leg against mine
Your fingers tracing circles on my skin


I called your silence hesitation
Then you pulled me closer in


Maybe I was looking for an ending
While you were asking me to stay


[Bridge]


Maybe I was right
Maybe I was wrong
Maybe I don’t need to know
Before the night is gone


Don’t give me certainty
Just tell me what is true
Or whisper it so close
I have to hear it from you


[Final Chorus]


Tell me what you meant
Not what I thought you meant
Come a little closer
Let me feel the words begin


I was so sure I understood
I forgot to let you in


Stay right here
Say it again
Let me learn the difference
Between you
And what I thought you meant


[Outro]


Your mouth said nothing
Your body moved


Come closer


Tell me
