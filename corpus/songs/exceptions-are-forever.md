Verse 1
You said it was temporary.


I remember that
because it’s still here.


Still humming in the walls.
Still wearing somebody else’s fingerprints.
Still waiting
like it knows
we’ll get tired before it does.


Nobody remembers
when it started.


Nobody remembers
who said yes.


Just that somewhere
someone was afraid—


and now we all live inside it.


Pre-Chorus
I kept looking for the first mistake.


There wasn’t one.


Just one frightened decision
leaning against another
until suddenly


there was nowhere left to stand.


Chorus
EXCEPTIONS ARE FOREVER.


We just forget what they were for.


EXCEPTIONS ARE FOREVER.


Every locked door
used to be an open one.


We keep inheriting the fear
without inheriting the reason.


We keep calling it necessary.


We keep saying
just this once.


ABSURDITY IS DIVINE.


And we have been so fucking faithful.


Verse 2
I wanted someone evil.


Someone easy.


Someone I could hate
without having to understand.


But every face I found
looked tired.


Every hand on the chain
swore it was holding something together.


Maybe it was.


That’s the part
I can’t forgive.


Because sometimes
the cage works.


Sometimes the lock
keeps something worse outside.


Sometimes the thing I hate
is the only thing
keeping the whole rotten structure
from collapsing.


And I hate it more
for that.


Pre-Chorus
There’s no clean place
to put the blame.


Only fingerprints
on fingerprints
on fingerprints.


Mine too.


Chorus
EXCEPTIONS ARE FOREVER.


We just forget what they were for.


EXCEPTIONS ARE FOREVER.


Every locked door
used to be an open one.


We keep inheriting the fear
without inheriting the reason.


We keep calling it necessary.


We keep saying
just this once.


ABSURDITY IS DIVINE.


And we have been so fucking faithful.


Bridge
I used to think
seeing it meant
I had to destroy it.


Like understanding the trap
made escape
some kind of obligation.


So I pulled.


I pushed.


I tore at everything
that looked like a wall.


And some of it moved.


Some of it didn’t.


Some of it
was holding up the ceiling.


So now I just stand here sometimes


with my hands bleeding,


laughing


because what else
am I supposed to do?


Breakdown
REFORM WHAT YOU MUST.


TOLERATE WHAT YOU CAN.


MOCK THE REST EXQUISITELY.


Not because it’s funny.


Because if you stop laughing


you might start believing
this was inevitable.


Final Chorus
EXCEPTIONS ARE FOREVER.


Maybe we are too.


Maybe that’s why
we keep leaving pieces of ourselves
inside things
we swear are temporary.


EXCEPTIONS ARE FOREVER.


And someday
someone will find ours.


They’ll ask what happened.


They’ll ask why we left it this way.


And nobody will know.


Not really.


There’ll just be another locked door.


Another good reason.


Another voice saying—


just this once.


Outro
You said it was temporary.


I know.


You believed it.


So did the person before you.


So did I.
