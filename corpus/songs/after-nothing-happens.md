---
title: "After Nothing Happens"
slug: after-nothing-happens
languages: ["en"]
---

[Verse 1]
The blue light from the microwave
makes the kitchen look underwater
You’re standing there in one sock
reading the same sentence twice


I’m somewhere else again
you notice
but you don’t say it


The radiator kicks
someone upstairs drags a chair
three inches across the floor


You say my name
from the other room


not loudly


just enough


[Refrain]
After nothing happens
you’re still there


After nothing happens
I come back


[Verse 2]
There’s a cup on the windowsill
with a crack through the handle
We said we’d throw it out


months ago


You know when not to touch me
which is different
from not touching me


Sometimes you leave water
beside the bed
without mentioning it


Sometimes I find it
hours later


warm


The hallway light leaks
under the door


Neither of us gets up


[Refrain]
After nothing happens
you’re still there


After nothing happens
I come back


Not all the way


enough to be seen


[Verse 3]
We’ve said the wrong thing correctly
more than once


We’ve apologized
and meant it
and somehow that wasn’t enough


There are things repair can’t erase
There are things erasure would ruin


So we leave the scar where it is


stop asking it
to become a lesson


You move my shoes
out of the doorway


I put your book
back where you left it


The room changes shape
by almost nothing


[Bridge]
I thought there would be a moment


some clean dividing line


before
after


hurt
healed


alone
not alone


But nothing announced itself


No weather
No sudden understanding


Just your coat over my chair


my hand finding the small of your back
when we pass in the hallway


both of us tired


both of us
making room


[Final Refrain]
After nothing happens
you’re still there


After nothing happens
I come back


You forget
I remember


I break something
you keep the pieces nearby


You go quiet
I stop trying to fill it


Tuesday becomes Wednesday
without asking us


and sometime after midnight
your foot finds mine
under the sheet


not forgiveness


not forever


just checking


without waking


whether I’m still there


I am


for now
