[Intro — spoken]


Ladies and gentlemen
You know your parts


The halo enters from the left
The monster from the right


[Verse 1]


He walks in gold
We know the cue


Anything ugly
stays out of view


Don’t ask too much
Don’t spoil the show


We love him dearly
Isn’t that enough to know?


[Chorus]


Applause is an alibi
Clap your hands and close your eyes


If everybody loves him
how bad could he be?


And booing is a verdict
Before the evidence arrives


If everybody hates her
what else could she be?


[Verse 2]


Bring her out
Dim the light


Every mistake
proves we were right


Every kindness
gets explained away


Once you’ve made a monster
the person fades away


[Cabaret Break — spoken]


Polish the halo
Sharpen the horns


Saint
Monster


Hero
Fraud


Much easier


[Bridge]


The saint can still do damage
And the monster can still bleed


You can love without exemption
You can judge without erasure


[Final Chorus]


Applause is not an alibi
Booing is not proof


Love them if you love them
Just leave room for truth


Condemn what needs condemning
But leave the costume on the floor


A person can be guilty
And still be something more


[Outro — spoken]


The halo was cardboard


The horns were too


You may now distrust the lighting.
