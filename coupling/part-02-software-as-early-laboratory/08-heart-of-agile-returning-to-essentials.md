# 8. Heart of Agile: Returning to Essentials

## Draft Intent

- Show why simplification appears later in method evolution.
- Frame Heart of Agile as a re-centering move after framework accretion.
- Connect HoA basics to cohesion/coupling recovery.

## Planned Throughline

- Teams adopt process layers to manage scale, then lose clarity under accumulation.
- Heart of Agile (Collaborate, Deliver, Reflect, Improve) recovers executable basics.
- The return to basics improves cohesion discipline and prepares cleaner measurement in Chapter 9.

## Planned Section Arc

- `## Why a Return to Essentials Was Needed`
  - Process accretion and coordination drag
  - Framework compliance vs learning quality
- `## The Four Verbs of Heart of Agile`
  - Collaborate, Deliver, Reflect, Improve as loop discipline
  - Behavioral clarity over methodology sprawl
- `## Cohesion Effects at Team Scale`
  - Ownership legibility, role clarity, and local adaptation
  - How HoA can reduce handoff-induced ambiguity
- `## Limits and Misuse Cases`
  - "Back to basics" as slogan vs structural practice
  - Risk of simplification without consequence visibility
- `## Cross-Domain Parallels`
  - Healthcare: huddle-deliver-reflect-improve loops in care teams
  - Public administration: pilot-review-adjust cycles in service delivery
  - Education: lesson-study cycles with collaborative refinement
- `## Transition to Chapter 9`
  - Move from behavioral recentering to measurement discipline (DORA)

## Citation and Evidence Plan

- Alistair Cockburn (*Heart of Agile*) and Agile practice literature
- One critique source on simplification misuse or framework drift
- One cross-domain source each for healthcare/public administration/education loop design

## Key Risk to Manage While Drafting

- Avoid treating Heart of Agile as an anti-method slogan; keep focus on structural loop discipline and consequence proximity.
