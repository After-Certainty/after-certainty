# 9. DORA: Measuring Coupling

## Draft Intent

- Show how DORA converted method claims into measurable outcomes.
- Clarify what delivery metrics reveal about consequence-return quality.
- Distinguish diagnostic metrics from governance theater.

## Planned Throughline

- DORA metrics offer observable signals for coupling quality between decision and consequence.
- Measurement helps only when metrics feed redesign-capable boundaries.
- Metric systems can drift into reporting optimization when detached from learning loops.

## Planned Section Arc

- `## Why DORA Changed the Conversation`
  - From method allegiance to measurable outcomes
  - Speed-plus-stability as structural signal
- `## What the Four Metrics Actually Track`
  - Lead time for changes
  - Deployment frequency
  - Change failure rate
  - Time to restore service
- `## Metrics as Coupling Diagnostics`
  - What high/low patterns imply about boundary quality
  - Why trend context matters more than point scores
- `## Failure Modes of Metric Governance`
  - Scorecard theater, target gaming, and proxy optimization
  - Goodhart-like distortions under incentive pressure
- `## Cross-Domain Parallels`
  - Healthcare quality indicators and delayed corrective action
  - Public service KPI systems vs citizen-facing outcomes
- `## Transition to Chapter 10`
  - Move from measurement discipline to shift-left temporal compression

## Citation and Evidence Plan

- DORA research reports and supporting delivery-performance studies
- One metric-distortion/Goodhart-style critique anchor
- One empirical source on metric gaming under management pressure
- One cross-domain source each for healthcare/public-sector metric governance
