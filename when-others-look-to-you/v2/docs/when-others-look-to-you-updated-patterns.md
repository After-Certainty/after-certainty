# **Leadership Patterns --- Glossary**

Quick reference for canonical titles. For **Pattern Block** production,
**positive-only** renewing bodies, placement, and inline anchors, see
`book-rules.md` and `pattern-integration-guide.md`.

**v2 manuscript:** Chapter pull quotes use **insight lines** (analytical, third
person)—not the conversational **catalog taglines** below, which remain the
shared Appendix B phrases for cross-edition reference and integration notes.

## **Forming**

Attention Finds a Focus

In moments of uncertainty, people begin orienting toward one person for direction.

*Catalog tagline:* "Attention finds a focus—then direction follows."

*v2 pull-quote insight:*

> Influence begins as permission others give, not as authority someone posts.

Examples Accumulate

What people see others do---especially under pressure---quickly defines what they believe is acceptable.

*Catalog tagline:* "Careful—examples accumulate faster than announcements."

*v2 pull-quote insight:*

> Under pressure, people learn from what they watch—not from what was announced.

Leadership Coalesces

Repeated reliance on one person concentrates decision-making around them.

*Catalog tagline:* "We've made you the one we default to on this."

*v2 pull-quote insight:*

> Repetition makes one person the default path before the org chart moves.

## **Adjusting**

Dissent is Welcomed

Problems are surfaced early so reality can reach decision-makers in time.

*Catalog tagline:* "Dissent is welcomed here—say it early."

*v2 pull-quote insight:*

> Early signals stay cheap; late signals stay expensive.

Feedback Drives Change

The group adjusts direction in response to new information or emerging risk.

*Catalog tagline:* "We're going to change course based on what we just learned."

*v2 pull-quote insight:*

> A plan holds only while it still fits what is happening now.

Leaders Feel the Consequences

Those making decisions remain connected to the consequences of those decisions.

*Catalog tagline:* "We own the outcome on this---no passing it off."

*v2 pull-quote insight:*

> When decision and consequence stay in the same loop, drift cannot hide for long.

## **Eroding**

Disagreement is Suppressed

Dissent fades, and decisions go unchallenged even when risks are visible.

*Catalog tagline:* "I'm noticing no one pushes back anymore."

*v2 pull-quote insight:*

> The warning is usually silence, not a fight.

Learning Collapses

Information from the ground no longer matches what decision-makers hear or believe in time.

*Catalog tagline:* "Learning collapses when the ground story never reaches the room."

*v2 pull-quote insight:*

> The story at the center and the story on the ground can diverge while both still sound fine.

Exceptions are Forever

Temporary workarounds and shortcuts become permanent parts of how the group operates.

*Catalog tagline:* "Let's be careful---exceptions are forever."

*v2 pull-quote insight:*

> What was meant to be temporary outlasts its emergency when nothing removes it.

## **Circulating**

Leadership Reproduces Itself

Leadership behaviors and norms are imitated, shaping how future leadership forms.

*Catalog tagline:* "Leadership reproduces itself—people copy what gets rewarded."

*v2 pull-quote insight:*

> People copy what got rewarded, not what got written down.
