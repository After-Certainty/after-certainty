# **Acknowledgements**

This book did not come from a single moment. It came from many small
ones, repeated over time.

To my wife, Brittany—

Thank you for your constant encouragement and belief. You have not only
supported this work, you have lived it. You’ve taught me these dynamics
in ways I could not have learned on my own, and you’ve given me the
space to try, fail, and try again.

To my children—

You’ve helped me see what **Leaders Feel the Consequences** can mean in a
family. You respond to what I do far more than what I say, and through
you I’ve learned that **Examples Accumulate** whether I intend it or
not.

To my parents—

You’ve shown me, over time, that **Feedback Drives Change** and that
**Leadership Reproduces Itself**, not through instruction, but through how
you’ve lived. Those lessons have stayed with me longer than anything that
could have been said directly.

To Alistair Cockburn—

Thank you for teaching me how to see and speak in patterns. Your
influence is present throughout this book, not just in what is written,
but in how it is shaped.

To Anderson Shen, Herb Collins, Jeremy Smith, Dee Gray, Daniel Bean,
Tyler Chapman, and Justin Kenlon—

Your examples make this real. The ideas in this book are not abstract to
me because I’ve seen them lived out through you.

And to the many moments—small and easy to miss—where **Attention Finds a
Focus**, something happened, and someone learned from it.

This book is an attempt to notice those moments more clearly.
