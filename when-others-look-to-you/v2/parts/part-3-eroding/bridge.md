# **Bridge — From Renewing to Eroding**

When the loop weakens, the problems show up first as silence—not as
catastrophe. **Part III**—**Eroding**—follows what happens when pushback
fades, signals arrive incomplete, and temporary exceptions harden into
structure.

**Disagreement is Suppressed.** **Learning Collapse**—the ground story
stops reaching the room in time. **Exceptions are Forever**—not because
anyone decides, but because nothing removes them.
