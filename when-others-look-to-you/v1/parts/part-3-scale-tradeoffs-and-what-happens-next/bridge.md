# **Bridge — From Structure to Scale Pressure**

What follows keeps the same lenses under harder conditions: larger reach, more
distance from consequence, and pressure that cycles faster.

At small scale, [**Correction**](../../back-matter/glossary.md#correction) is visible and personal. At larger scale,
information from the ground travels farther, arrives later, and warps more on the
way. That makes [**vitality**](../../back-matter/glossary.md#vitality) harder to sustain, not impossible.

Scale also gives **Leadership Reproduces Itself** more room to run. What gets
rewarded or punished travels faster than written rules. People who come after
learn from example before they learn from policy.

The model still applies—the [**vibrant group**](../../back-matter/glossary.md#vibrant-group) and [**decaying group**](../../back-matter/glossary.md#decaying-group),
[**renewal**](../../back-matter/glossary.md#renewal) and [**erosion**](../../back-matter/glossary.md#erosion). Scale and pressure change the risks, not the labels.

At scale, selective followership also changes shape. Open dissent can turn into
people going their own way quietly, local workarounds, and paperwork that looks
aligned while day-to-day reality is not aligned.

The questions ahead take up scale and drift, tradeoffs under pressure, and what
judgment can still do when repair is only partial and time is short.
