# **Introduction — When Attention Gathers**

> A leader is someone others look to when deciding what to do next.

Leadership begins wherever attention concentrates. It does not require
title, rank, or intention. The moment others look to someone for
direction under uncertainty, influence multiplies.

This chapter establishes leadership as attention. It introduces the
central tension of the book: when others look to you, authority can
either renew itself or begin to harden.

No technical language yet. Universal tone.
