# **Chapter 7 — Authority Circulation**

Renewal requires permeability.

Authority circulation is defined as the degree to which influence can
move back toward power without punishment.

The chapter examines:

- Real vs symbolic consultation

- Mutual vs unilateral permeability

- Replication of leadership posture

- Temporary contraction vs permanent insulation

- Open selective dissent vs covert counter-signaling as circulation
  indicators

- Whether refusal remains corrigible (inside the system) or becomes
  silent exit (outside the system)

Circulation is the structural mechanism of vitality.
