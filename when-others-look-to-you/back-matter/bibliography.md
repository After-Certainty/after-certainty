# **Bibliography**

- Agamben, Giorgio. *State of Exception*. Translated by Kevin Attell.
  Chicago: University of Chicago Press, 2005.

- Argyris, Chris. *Overcoming Organizational Defenses*. Boston: Allyn and
  Bacon, 1990.

- Argyris, Chris, and Donald A. Schon. *Organizational Learning II:

- Arendt, Hannah. "What Is Authority?" In *Between Past and Future*.

- Beck, Ulrich. *Risk Society: Towards a New Modernity*. London: Sage,

- Cockburn, Alistair. "How to Step Up Stepping Up: Promoting Guest

- Edmondson, Amy C. *The Fearless Organization: Creating Psychological
  Safety in the Workplace for Learning, Innovation, and Growth*. Hoboken,
  NJ: Wiley, 2018.

- Foucault, Michel. *Discipline and Punish: The Birth of the Prison*.
  New York: Vintage Books, 1977.

- Hirschman, Albert O. *Exit, Voice, and Loyalty*. Cambridge, MA: Harvard

- Janis, Irving L. *Groupthink: Psychological Studies of Policy Decisions

- Perrow, Charles. *Normal Accidents: Living with High-Risk Technologies*.

- Scott, James C. *Domination and the Arts of Resistance: Hidden

- Staw, Barry M. "Knee-Deep in the Big Muddy: A Study of Escalating

- Vaughan, Diane. *The Challenger Launch Decision: Risky Technology,

  1996.

- Weber, Max. *Economy and Society: An Outline of Interpretive Sociology*.

  California Press, 1978.

- Weick, Karl E. *The Social Psychology of Organizing*. 2nd ed. Reading,
  MA: Addison-Wesley, 1979.

- Weick, Karl E., and Kathleen M. Sutcliffe. *Managing the Unexpected:

  2001.

- Weick, Karl E., Kathleen M. Sutcliffe, and David Obstfeld. "Organizing
