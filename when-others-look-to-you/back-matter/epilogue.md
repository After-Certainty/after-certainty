# **Epilogue**

This epilogue should address boundary conditions in plain language:
what happens when renewal thresholds are crossed and authority has already
traveled farther than accountability.

Focus:

- Distinguish "slowing damage" from "restoring vitality"
- Name what can still be preserved when full reversal is no longer
  possible
- Reinforce human-scale practice as the durable site of renewal
- Avoid fatalism and avoid false optimism

Tone target:

- Sobering but practical
- Honest about limits, clear about agency
- Reader-facing close that invites practice rather than certainty
