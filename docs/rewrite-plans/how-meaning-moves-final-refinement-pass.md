## 4. Reachability classification

- **Not** an eleventh Appendix A pattern.
- **Culminating relational condition** — whether later correction still has somewhere to land.
- **Connection** = ongoing relationship/consequence.
- **Contact** = staying near what was said and unclear.
- **Reachability** = what Contact, Connection, and Restraint sometimes preserve after compression hardens.

Heading: `### **Culminating Principle: Reachability**`
