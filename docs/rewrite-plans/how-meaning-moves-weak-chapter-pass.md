## 5. Chapter 4 new scene summary

Unnamed couple from Strand B appear as parents at an elementary-school library meeting after pickup. Twelve parents debate a modest drop-off-lane safety plan (paint, cones, volunteer). They share the same concern—kids not getting hurt—but diverge on tempo: he wants to stop delaying after a near-miss; she wants time to hear back-gate families. The room votes; plan passes quickly. Analysis enters during live exchange (tempo-as-character, caricature, facts under compression) rather than after a generic disagreement essay.
