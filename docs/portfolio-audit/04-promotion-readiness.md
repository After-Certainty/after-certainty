Most upcoming titles sit at **~8–13k words**—essay-length vs `book-rules` full-book bands (~50–90k). Promotion can proceed as **essay editions** if author accepts; otherwise expansion is a **pre-promotion** or **post-soft-launch** decision—not a metadata fix.

| Strategy | When |
|----------|------|
| **Essay promotion** | Phase 4 + author gate; position explicitly as concentrated argument |
| **Defer promotion** | Until Part I author approves expansion (Tier C books) |
| **Split volume** | Not recommended without author request—hurts portfolio coherence |
