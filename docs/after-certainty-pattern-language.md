# The After Certainty Pattern Language

Cross-corpus pattern language for the After Certainty portfolio. It **extends** the existing WOLTY and book-local pattern languages; it does not replace them.

Canonical data: `semantic/forces/`, `semantic/patterns/` (with `patternRole` / `organizingForce` / `realityDynamic`), and typed edges in `semantic/relationships.yml`.

## The master pattern

**Reality Answers Back** (`reality-answers-back`)

Human systems necessarily simplify reality, but reality eventually re-enters through consequences, exceptions, dissent, failure, and lived experience. Responsible systems preserve paths through which those answers can be noticed early enough to revise understanding and action.

## The four forces

| Force | Slug | Role |
|-------|------|------|
| Perception | `perception` | How we see — notice, simplify, interpret, and construct models |
| Power | `power` | How influence works — attention, participation, responsibility |
| Time | `time` | How systems persist and change — commitment, speed, inherited structure |
| Contact | `contact` | How systems remain connected to reality — consequences, bad news, particulars |

These are first-class `force-*` entities, not ordinary patterns and not the glossary concept `contact` (dialogue contact in *How Meaning Moves*).

## The twelve supporting patterns

Each supporting pattern belongs to exactly one force and **expresses** the master pattern. `realityDynamic` marks whether it tends to **obscure** reality or act as a **corrective** opening.

**Perception:** Compression Loses the Person · Understanding Circulates · Results Conceal the Game  
**Power:** Authority Follows Attention · Participation Creates Legitimacy · Responsibility Follows Leverage  
**Time:** Structures Outlive Reasons · Speed Relocates Judgment · Commitment Leaves Room  
**Contact:** Distance Conceals Consequence · Bad News Reaches Power · Care Preserves Particulars

## The cycle

1. Perception shapes Power.
2. Power creates and stabilizes structures through Time.
3. Time can thin Contact.
4. Contact with consequences renews Perception.
5. Reality Answers Back operates across the full cycle.

This is a recurring system with feedback loops, not a one-way ladder.

## Relation to WOLTY

WOLTY patterns (Attention Finds a Focus, Dissent Is Welcomed, Exceptions Are Forever, and others) remain the leadership/organizational pattern language. Typed edges connect them to After Certainty patterns where the dynamics meet (for example Attention Finds a Focus **precedes** Authority Follows Attention; Disagreement Is Suppressed **weakens** Bad News Reaches Power). Portfolio books may participate in both languages.
