# **Closing Reflection**

We began with a simple shift: certainty is not the only way to move
through complexity.

Along the way, we looked at ordinary settings - commutes, grocery lines,
conversations, debugging sessions - and saw the same pattern repeat. What
first looked like noise became easier to read once we stayed with the
question a little longer.

The world is not a puzzle you solve once. It is a landscape you keep
learning.

You will not see everything at once. No one does. But you can keep moving
with attention.

When something does not fit, notice it.

Ask why.

Test a guess.

Learn from what happens.

That is enough to keep finding better paths.
