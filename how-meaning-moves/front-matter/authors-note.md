# **Author’s Note**

Most books on communication begin with skill: how to speak better, how to listen better, how to persuade more effectively. This one does not. It begins with how meaning forms.

By the time people start replying to each other, interpretation is already underway. Signal has already landed. Compression has already started. Restraint is already being tested. That sequence appears in families, teams, institutions, and intimate relationships. The setting changes, but the pattern remains.

I wrote this book because that pattern is common and easy to miss. Communication failures are often explained too late, after words are judged and positions are fixed. I wanted to look earlier, at what moves before understanding and why that movement so often outruns care.

I kept the language plain on purpose. The goal is not to sound impressive. The goal is to make the structure easier to see.

For transparency: I used an AI language model as a drafting and revision aid, much like an editor or analytical assistant. It helped with structure, phrasing, and coherence checks. It did not determine the book’s claims, values, or conclusions. All judgments and final decisions are mine.
