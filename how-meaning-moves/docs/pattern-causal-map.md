# Pattern causal map (reference — not shown on pattern-groups figure)

Appendix A lists **Related Patterns** for each entry—conceptual neighbors, not a single official causal DAG. This note proposes a **directional reading** consistent with:

- cluster order (Formation → Completion → Movement → Resolution → Reinforcement),
- phenomenology in Part I (signal → compression → early meaning → gaps),
- and where the book describes **acceleration**, **hardening**, and **loops**.

Use this note when you need a **causal** reading; `pattern-groups.svg` only shows **group membership** (stacked rectangles). Revise causal claims here first.

---

## 1. Primary spine (one exchange, early-to-late)

Rough **micro-temporal** order inside a single interaction—patterns are not strictly discrete steps, but this is the usual flow the chapters imply:

| Step | Pattern | Rationale |
|------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | **Attention finds a signal** | First cue “decides the channel” (Appendix A; Ch 1). |
| 2 | **Meaning forms early** | Interpretation starts before language is fully processed; follows signal selection. |
| 3 | **Meaning outruns the words** | Language under-specifies; inference fills structure—often **after** a partial read exists. |
| 4 | **Gaps invite completion** | Open meaning does not stay open; blanks fill (linked from meaning forming early). |
| 5 | **Intent gets assigned** | Motive inferred once impact lands; tight coupling to gap-filling in the appendix. |

Spine edges (forward):

1. Attention finds a signal → Meaning forms early  
2. Meaning forms early → Meaning outruns the words *(language arrives “after” a frame is already forming—also argue parallel; diagram may use parallel branch from step 2)*  
3. Meaning forms early → Gaps invite completion  
4. Meaning outruns the words → Gaps invite completion *(inferring missing structure)*  
5. Gaps invite completion → Intent gets assigned  

*Optional refinement:* Some drafts draw **Attention → Meaning outruns** as a second arrow (appendix relates them). That captures “signal loads the channel before wording catches up.” If the diagram gets crowded, keep **Attention → Meaning forms early → …** as the main line and add Meaning outruns as a merge into Gaps.

## 2. Pressure and drift (two kinds of “movement”)

**Under pressure** (same exchange or escalating stakes):

- **Meaning forms early** → **Meaning shifts under pressure** (appendix: related patterns).
- **Meaning shifts under pressure** → **Meaning gets distorted** (appendix: related; nuance traded for speed, correction can’t catch up).

**Across time** (returns, history, repeated encounters):

- **Meaning outruns the words** → **Meaning drifts over time** (appendix relates drift to outrunning).
- **Meaning drifts over time** → **Meaning reinforces itself** (appendix relates these two).

So **Meaning shifts under pressure** and **Meaning drifts over time** are **siblings** under Movement—different timescales—not a strict A→B order between them. The diagram can show both receiving arrows from earlier patterns and both feeding forward.

Forward edges:

- Meaning forms early → Meaning shifts under pressure  
- Meaning shifts under pressure → Meaning gets distorted  
- Meaning outruns the words → Meaning drifts over time  
- Meaning drifts over time → Meaning reinforces itself  

---

## 3. Resolution (counterforce vs hardening)

**Meaning gets distorted** is linked from **Intent gets assigned** (appendix). Add:

- Intent gets assigned → Meaning gets distorted *(when motive feels settled, correction feels personal)*  
- Meaning shifts under pressure → Meaning gets distorted *(already above)*  

**Contact keeps the read open** is partly **interruptive**: it presumes return, lower reactivity, more context—so it **competes with** distortion rather than always sitting “after” it. Causal arrows that match the appendix:

- Meaning outruns the words → Contact keeps the read open *(interpretation still tied to signal/history; contact revisits)*  
- Meaning gets distorted → Contact keeps the read open *(misleading if read as “distortion causes contact”—better: show Contact as a **return path** from distortion risk)*  

For a **single DAG**, a practical compromise:

- Draw **Contact keeps the read open** with an incoming arrow from **Meaning gets distorted** labeled conceptually as “response path” *or* incoming from **Meaning outruns the words** only, and use caption in intro that contact is **optional**, not inevitable.

Simplest diagram-friendly rule:

- **Meaning gets distorted** ← Intent gets assigned  
- **Meaning gets distorted** ← Meaning shifts under pressure  
- **Contact keeps the read open** ← Meaning outruns the words *(appendix relates contact to outrunning and to distortion—here we choose one clean inbound to avoid implying distortion always precedes contact)*  

If that feels too thin, add a second inbound to Contact from **Meaning gets distorted** and mention in the figure note that relationship is **non-linear**.

**Minimal Resolution edges for v1 diagram:**

- Meaning outruns the words → Contact keeps the read open  
- Intent gets assigned → Meaning gets distorted  

---

## 4. Reinforcement (loops)

Appendix **Meaning reinforces itself** relates to **Meaning shifts under pressure** and **Meaning gets distorted**.

Forward into reinforcement:

- Meaning gets distorted → Meaning reinforces itself  
- Meaning drifts over time → Meaning reinforces itself  

Feedback **out** of reinforcement (loops):

- Meaning reinforces itself → Meaning shifts under pressure *(next encounter; “prepared to see again”)*  
- Meaning reinforces itself → Meaning forms early *(interpretive frame loads early—same appendix spirit as Ch 9 / Ch 11)*  

Optional third loop:

- Meaning reinforces itself → Attention finds a signal *(selective attention—“what stands out” is primed)*

## 5. Consolidated edge list (causal reference)

Directed edges (draft causal reading):

| From | To |
|------|-----|
| Attention finds a signal | Meaning forms early |
| Meaning forms early | Meaning outruns the words |
| Meaning forms early | Gaps invite completion |
| Meaning outruns the words | Gaps invite completion |
| Gaps invite completion | Intent gets assigned |
| Meaning forms early | Meaning shifts under pressure |
| Meaning outruns the words | Meaning drifts over time |
| Meaning shifts under pressure | Meaning gets distorted |
| Intent gets assigned | Meaning gets distorted |
| Meaning outruns the words | Contact keeps the read open |
| Meaning gets distorted | Meaning reinforces itself |
| Meaning drifts over time | Meaning reinforces itself |
| Meaning reinforces itself | Meaning shifts under pressure |
| Meaning reinforces itself | Meaning forms early |

**Multi-out:** Meaning forms early → three targets (Meaning outruns the words, Gaps invite completion, Meaning shifts under pressure).  
**Multi-in:** Meaning gets distorted ← Intent gets assigned, Meaning shifts under pressure.  
**Multi-in:** Meaning reinforces itself ← Meaning gets distorted, Meaning drifts over time.

---

## 6. What we are not claiming

- Not every formation pattern fires in every conversation in lockstep.  
- **Movement** patterns are not sequential with each other without context (pressure vs drift).  
- **Contact** is not guaranteed after distortion; contact names **relational possibility**, not fate.
