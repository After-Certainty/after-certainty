# Part IV — Love in Human Life

We have watched love move and noticed what helps it endure. Now the harder work begins—not in theory, but on Tuesdays. Romance, family, friendship, community: the same patterns, lived in different rooms, with different centers of gravity. Not four kinds of love—four places where love learns to live. The boundaries between them are often porous; a friendship becomes family, a romance deepens into friendship, a street slowly turns strangers into neighbors. Not as a formula applied from above, but as terrain.
