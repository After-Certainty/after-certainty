# **Bibliography**

This book draws primarily from sociology, philosophy, organizational theory, and epistemology. Works cited in the notes appear below in alphabetical order by author.

- Carreyrou, John. *Bad Blood: Secrets and Lies in a Silicon Valley Startup*. New York: Knopf, 2018.

- Cole, Robert E. "What Really Happened to Toyota?" *MIT Sloan Management Review* 52, no. 4 (2011): 29–35.

- Dewey, John. *Experience and Education*. New York: Macmillan, 1938.

- Douglas, Karen M., et al. "Understanding Conspiracy Theories." In *Political Psychology* 40, suppl. 1 (2019): 3–35.

- Druckman, James N., et al. "A Framework for the Study of Misinformation and Health." *Annual Review of Public Health* 44 (2023): 373–394.

- Freeh, Sporkin, & Sullivan LLC. *Report of the Special Investigative Counsel Regarding the Actions of the Pennsylvania State University Related to the Child Sexual Abuse Committed by Gerald A. Sandusky*. July 12, 2012.

- Fricker, Miranda. *Epistemic Injustice: Power and the Ethics of Knowing*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2007.

- Grady, Peter. *Flying Blind: The 737 MAX Tragedy and the Fall of Boeing*. New York: Anchor Books, 2022.

- Isaacson, Walter. *Steve Jobs*. New York: Simon & Schuster, 2011.

- Keep, William W., and Peter J. Vander Nat. "Multilevel Marketing and Pyramid Schemes in the United States: An Historical Analysis." *Journal of Historical Research in Marketing* 6, no. 2 (2014): 188–210.

- Lowenstein, Roger. *Buffett: The Making of an American Capitalist*. New York: Random House, 1995.

- Luhmann, Niklas. *Trust and Power: Two Works*. Translated by Howard Davis, John Raffan, and Kathryn Rooney. Chichester, UK: John Wiley & Sons, 1979.

- Merton, Robert K. "The Normative Structure of Science." In *The Sociology of Science*, edited by Norman W. Storer, 267–278. Chicago: University of Chicago Press, 1973. First published 1942.

- O'Neill, Onora. *A Question of Trust*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2002.

- Ostrom, Elinor. *Governing the Commons: The Evolution of Institutions for Collective Action*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1990.

- Raine, Susan. "Reinventing the self: NXIVM's promises, secrets, and lies." *International Journal for the Study of New Religions* 2 (2021). DOI: 10.54208/0002/005.

- Raymond, Eric S. *The Cathedral and the Bazaar*. Sebastopol, CA: O'Reilly Media, 1999.

- Reason, James. *Managing the Risks of Organizational Accidents*. Aldershot, UK: Ashgate, 1997.

- Vaughan, Diane. *The Challenger Launch Decision: Risky Technology, Culture, and Deviance at NASA*. Chicago: University of Chicago Press, 1996.

- Weber, Max. *Economy and Society: An Outline of Interpretive Sociology*. Edited by Guenther Roth and Claus Wittich. Berkeley: University of California Press, 1978.

- Wiedeman, Reeves. *The Cult of We: WeWork, Adam Neumann, and the Great Startup Delusion*. New York: Little, Brown, 2021.
