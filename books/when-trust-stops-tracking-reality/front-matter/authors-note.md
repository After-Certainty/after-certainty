# **Author's Note**

The recurring story of Calder Family Health is fictional. The organization, its employees, and the events described throughout this book are fictional.

I created that story to explore patterns that appear across many real organizations, institutions, communities, and relationships. The examples discussed throughout the book draw from historical events, academic research, public case studies, and everyday human experience. Calder itself is not intended to represent any single organization. Instead, it serves as a common narrative through which the patterns explored in each chapter can be observed more clearly.

The purpose of the story is not to provide a case study—it is to provide a lens.

The patterns are real.

The story is not.
