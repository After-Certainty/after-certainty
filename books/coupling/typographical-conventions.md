# Typographical Conventions

This section explains how visual cues are used throughout the book to support cross-domain reading.

## Bold Terms

For sections that appear after Typographical Conventions in reading order, glossary terms appear in **bold** only at their first occurrence in the manuscript.

Example:

- **Invariant**
- **Cohesion**
- **Coupling**

This signals that the term is domain-relevant and can be looked up in the glossary.

## Glossary References

Key terms are defined in `glossary.md` in back matter so readers from different domains can align on shared definitions.

## Section Headings

Clear sub-headings are used to mark conceptual transitions and scene transitions.

Headings are functional navigation cues, not decorative labels.

## Emphasis and Tone

Emphasis is used sparingly and only to improve clarity.

The book avoids typographic overstatement (for example, excessive capitalization, repeated emphasis marks, or decorative formatting).
