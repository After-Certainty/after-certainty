# Bridge - Into Part III

Transition from software history to AI-era acceleration.

## Why AI Comes Next

Part II treated software as a laboratory where consequence pathways are visible and measurable. Part III tests the same invariant under faster generation cycles, larger context surfaces, and weaker default ownership of output.

The coordination grammar remains the same at medium visibility: acceleration without coherence maintenance, partial information at model and tool boundaries, and guardrails as constraint architecture that makes consequence visible before failure propagates.

## Focus

- Test the invariant under faster generation cycles
- Expose where speed outpaces ownership and consequence
- Introduce guardrails as an emergent constraint architecture
- Connect AI-era entropy (low cohesion modules, accidental tight coupling) to machine-speed structural drift
