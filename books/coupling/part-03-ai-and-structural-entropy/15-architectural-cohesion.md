# 15. Architectural Cohesion

## Draft Intent

- Argue that AI does not remove responsibility; it amplifies structural flaws when boundaries are unclear.
- Extend hexagonal and bounded-context logic to human–model–tool systems.

## Planned Throughline

- Bounded contexts: clear ownership domains for decisions, data, and model use
- Interfaces: explicit contracts between teams, services, and tool actions
- Composition roots: where integration is deliberately owned
- Clear ownership domains: who absorbs consequence and who may redesign
