# 12. The Frictionless Illusion

## Draft Intent

- Treat AI-assisted acceleration as a coordination stress test: output rises while ownership cohesion and consequence coupling often weaken.
- Avoid hype framing; keep diagnostic tone on structure, not tools.

## Planned Throughline

- Vibe coding and accept-all culture as severed feedback: generation without redesign-capable ownership
- Acceleration without coherence maintenance: many actors (human, model, tool) changing assumptions faster than integration can reconcile
- Partial information: context windows, retrieval gaps, and tacit team knowledge not carried into generated artifacts
