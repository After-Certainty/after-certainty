# 22. Designing for High Cohesion

## Draft Intent

- Translate cohesion diagnostics into design constraints: clear domains, role integrity, ownership visibility—reducing reliance on coordination substitutes.

## Planned Throughline

- Clear domains: explicit boundaries that survive scale pressure
- Role integrity: authority and obligation aligned under stress
- Ownership visibility: failures traceable to redesign-capable actors
- Guardrails: constraints that support cohesion without dissolving judgment (link Ch 14)
