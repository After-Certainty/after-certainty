# 21. The Oscillation

## Draft Intent

- Describe reform → acceleration → entropy → stabilization as a structural oscillation in how systems re-price coordination cost—not a cycle where "freedom" or "structure" wins permanently.

## Planned Throughline

- Reform: attempt to restore cohesion/coupling after visible harm
- Acceleration: throughput gains that can thin coherence maintenance
- Entropy: coordination substitutes multiply; stale representations accumulate
- Stabilization: procedural or technical freeze until the next shock
