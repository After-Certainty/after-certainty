# Bridge - Into Part V

Synthesize prior diagnostics into design-oriented constraints.

## What the Interlude Established

*Coherence Under Scale* named the pattern beneath cohesion and coupling: scaled systems pay synchronization cost to maintain a workable shared picture across actors who hold partial information and change on different clocks. Coordination substitutes for cohesion when that cost is paid in queues and rituals rather than in consequence return.

Part V does not introduce a new theory. It asks how design re-prices coordination cost—how to preserve learning without pretending scale away.

## Focus

- Explain oscillation as a structural pattern (reform, acceleration, entropy, stabilization)
- Offer design principles without prescriptive policy writing
- Treat Ch 22–23 as responses to coordination pressure: cohesion design and intentional coupling design
- Treat Ch 24–25 as limits: over-coupling fragility and necessary distance under complexity
