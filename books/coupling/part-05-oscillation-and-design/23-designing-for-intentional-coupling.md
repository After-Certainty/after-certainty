# 23. Designing for Intentional Coupling

## Draft Intent

- Design responses to weak coupling: proximal feedback, measured recovery, early detection, transparent consequence chains—re-pricing coordination cost toward learning.

## Planned Throughline

- Proximal feedback: shorten delayed propagation where redesign is possible
- Measured recovery: DORA-style diagnostics generalized as coherence signals (not software-only)
- Early detection: shift-left logic as temporal coordination discipline
- Transparent consequence chains: make return paths legible across boundaries
