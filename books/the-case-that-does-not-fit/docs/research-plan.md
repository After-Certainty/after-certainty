# The Case That Does Not Fit — Research Plan

Structured roadmap for research. Not full chapter research. **No chapter prose should be drafted from this document alone.**

## Evidence standards

- Prefer primary or institutional sources for specific factual claims.
- Verify memorable anecdotes before using them.
- Distinguish documented facts from interpretation.
- Reverify every 2026 event before publication; legal and factual outcomes may change.
- Write live events around decision situations whose tension survives regardless of eventual ruling, settlement, or policy reversal.
- Treat historical parallels as comparisons, not claims that present events are morally identical.

## Guardrails from outline

- Do not make the book a disguised argument for one preferred political outcome.
- Treat balance as structural rather than partisan.
- Preserve the strongest reason for the general rule before showing what it misses.
- Distinguish an exception from a precedent.
- Distinguish local judgment from unaccountable discretion.
- Ask who can appeal, who can leave, who can wait, and who must accept the first answer.

## Evidence layers

For every central case, separate:

1. **What happened** (documented events)
2. **How it was interpreted at the time**
3. **How the meaning changed later**
4. **What the pattern reveals** (author analysis—clearly marked)
