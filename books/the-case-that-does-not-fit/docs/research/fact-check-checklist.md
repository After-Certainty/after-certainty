# Fact-check checklist — The Case That Does Not Fit

Use before drafting or citing any 2026 current-event anchor or historical claim.

## Current-event anchors

- [ ] Claim still accurate as of draft date (outcome may have changed)
- [ ] Primary or institutional source located (not only secondary summary)
- [ ] Mechanism for the chapter still holds if the live outcome reverses
- [ ] Dates, numbers, and proper names verified
- [ ] Distinguishes study finding / agency plan / news report / interpretation

## Historical claims

- [ ] Primary or standard institutional history consulted
- [ ] Parallel is structural, not identity of harm
- [ ] Contested interpretations noted where relevant

## Chapter gate

- [ ] Durability / opposite-outcome test recorded and still holds
- [ ] Strongest reason for the general rule preserved before showing its limit
