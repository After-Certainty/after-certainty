## Case / terrain

The answer is not to replace checkboxes with an infinitely long blank page. Institutions still need categories, deadlines, jurisdictions, standards, and repeatable processes. Nor is the answer to romanticize local discretion.

## Conceptual movement

The deeper argument is for institutions capable of reconsideration: institutions that can recognize when a category has failed without abandoning the protection that rules provide.
