# After Certainty — Editorial Passes

Focused editing workflow for the grounding branch. Run one pass at a time; complete across the target scope before starting the next.

## Pass order

1. **Grounding** — Draft and place 5–8 vignettes; wire into argument flow *(complete)*
2. **Asymmetry** — Vary openings, length, delayed reframe *(complete)*
3. **Cohesion** — Vignettes must not smuggle new moral certainty; preserve revisability *(complete)*
4. **Feedback pass 2** — Author drafts incorporated file-by-file *(complete)*
5. **Essay discovery** — Delay thesis at opening; preserve compression at ending; see `docs/agents/01-essay-discovery-revision.md` *(complete)*
6. **Curiosity expansion** — Expand intellectual wandering between interesting questions and their answers; see `docs/agents/02-curiosity-expansion.md` *(complete)*
7. **Recognition preservation** — Protect recognitions and pattern prominence; compress over-expansion; see `docs/agents/03-recognition-preservation.md` *(complete)*
8. **Experience deepening v2** — Transform abstract argument into lived recognition before patterns land; see `docs/agents/04-experience-deepening-v2.md` *(complete)*
9. **Terrain & voice diversity** — Enrich examples, domains, inquiry pivots, and texture without changing structure or arguments; see `docs/agents/05-terrain-voice-diversity.md` *(complete)*
10. **Terrain thematic deepening** — Theme-native terrain per chapter map; discover don't decorate; see `docs/agents/06-terrain-thematic-deepening.md` *(complete)*
11. **Echo pass** — Resolve within-book and cluster repetition; assign ownership; see `docs/agents/07-echo-pass.md` *(complete)*
12. **Terrain & variety** — Late polish: passing terrain, rhetorical jazz, Ch 7–9 de-abstraction; see `docs/agents/08-terrain-variety.md` *(complete)*
13. **Trust the reader** — Reduce scaffolding; trust scenes; preserve pattern labels; see `docs/agents/09-trust-the-reader.md` *(complete)*
14. **Memorable terrain & quiet discovery** — Terrain diversity, memorability, quiet discovery; see `docs/agents/10-memorable-terrain-quiet-discovery.md`
15. **Export** — `make export-docx DIR=books/after-certainty`

## Pass 1 — Grounding

**Goal:** Add recognition anchors so readers inhabit institutions, speech, and responsibility—not only analyze them.

Check for:

- Each vignette connects to the chapter stabilizer/distortion
- Scene inside `::: {custom-style="Vignette Block"}`; heading outside
- 150–400 words; varied length and texture across chapters
- Analysis stays outside vignette blocks
- No bold or glossary terms inside scene text

## Pass 2 — Asymmetry

**Goal:** Break predictable chapter rhythm without losing calm moral posture.

Check for:

- At least one chapter opens with a concrete scene (Ch 5)
- At least one chapter is shorter/sharper (Ch 1)
- At least one section holds tension before reframing (Ch 6)
- Ch 2 and Ch 9: minimal structural change beyond vignette placement

## Pass 3 — Cohesion

**Goal:** Preserve intellectual discipline from beta feedback.

Check for:

- Vignettes show restraint, proportionality, incompletion—not new prescriptions
- Core invariant unchanged in every chapter
- Strong hinge lines in Ch 2 and Ch 9 preserved in meaning

## Pass 5 — Essay discovery

**Goal:** Arguments feel discovered rather than announced—without changing claims, structure, or conclusions.

Check for:

- Openings begin with observation, scene, or situation before naming the thesis (3–4 paragraphs, not pages)
- Existing vignettes moved earlier when they carry the insight
- Abstractions earned through observations, not eliminated
- Bold pattern compressions at chapter endings preserved (`**Pattern Name.**`)
- Surgical pass only (~20% more discovery); no literary wandering or memoir drift

Spec: [`docs/agents/01-essay-discovery-revision.md`](agents/01-essay-discovery-revision.md)

## Pass 6 — Curiosity expansion

**Goal:** Expand intellectual wandering between interesting questions and their answers without changing the underlying argument.

Check for:

- Interesting questions no longer answered within 1–3 paragraphs
- Investigation before conclusion (obvious answer rejected, implications followed)
- Writer appears curious—investigating, not lecturing
- Patterns earned through wandering (`**Pattern Name.**`)
- Localized expansion at question-and-answer choke points (~200–500 words per expansion)

Spec: [`docs/agents/02-curiosity-expansion.md`](agents/02-curiosity-expansion.md)

## Pass 7 — Recognition preservation

**Goal:** Exploration deepens recognitions without burying pattern language.

Check for:

- Recognition clearer and deeper than before Agent 02
- Pattern feels discovered and remains memorable
- Over-expansion cut (30% compression test)
- No repetition through wandering
- Kevin's voice preserved — not literary essayist drift

Spec: [`docs/agents/03-recognition-preservation.md`](agents/03-recognition-preservation.md)

## Pass 8 — Experience deepening v2

**Goal:** Transform abstract argument into lived recognition—the reader recognizes an experience, forms an explanation, discovers its limits, then meets the chapter insight.

Check for:

- Three-beat engine: "I've seen that" → "I know what's happening" → "Wait—that explanation isn't quite enough"
- Experience described before pattern or explanation names it
- Reasonable explanation allowed to breathe before reversal
- Solnit test: reader could recognize experience without formal pattern label
- No new theories, citations, pattern labels, or arguments added
- Bold pattern compressions at chapter endings preserved

Spec: [`docs/agents/04-experience-deepening-v2.md`](agents/04-experience-deepening-v2.md)

## Pass 9 — Terrain & voice diversity

**Goal:** Enrich texture—examples, metaphors, domains, inquiry pivots, observational detail—without rewriting structure, arguments, or pattern language.

Check for:

- One or two additional non-organizational examples per chapter where natural
- Inquiry pivots varied ("Perhaps.", "At first...", etc.) without losing rhythm
- Recurring imagery (dashboard, report, metrics) varied in surface form
- Declarations grounded in small observations where possible
- Chapter structure, conclusions, named patterns, and footnotes unchanged
- Unit length within ±10% of pre-pass count

Spec: [`docs/agents/05-terrain-voice-diversity.md`](agents/05-terrain-voice-diversity.md)

## Pass 10 — Terrain thematic deepening

**Goal:** Place each unit in theme-native terrain—caregiving, ecology, geology, navigation, community, craft—where the pattern is discovered, not decorated.

Check for:

- Chapter map action followed (deepen / replace / keep / do not touch)
- One primary terrain family per chapter
- Ornamental Agent 05 beats replaced where mapped
- Author-locked anchors intact
- Net length within ±5% per unit

Spec: [`docs/agents/06-terrain-thematic-deepening.md`](agents/06-terrain-thematic-deepening.md)

## Pass 11 — Echo pass

**Goal:** Resolve cross-unit repetition and cluster overlap; conclusion points rather than re-argues.

Check for:

- Verbatim intro/conclusion duplicates varied
- Within-chapter repeated beats compressed
- Bridges hand off without retelling cases
- Intentional anchors preserved (Ch 5, 6 trip, 8 dashboard, 9 fifth meeting)
- Cluster siblings not re-argued at chapter length

Spec: [`docs/agents/07-echo-pass.md`](agents/07-echo-pass.md)

## Pass 12 — Terrain & variety

**Goal:** Late polish after author read-through—no developmental rewrite.

Check for:

- One passing natural-world observation per chapter (weather, geology, forestry, gardening, navigation)
- Rhetorical pivots varied (Perhaps, conclusion seems obvious, story is incomplete, etc.)
- Part III re-grounded where abstraction drifts
- Top-tier units (intro, Ch 1–2, Ch 5, conclusion) minimally touched
- Ch 5 untouched

Spec: [`docs/agents/08-terrain-variety.md`](agents/08-terrain-variety.md)

## Pass 13 — Trust the reader

**Goal:** Reduce visible explanatory scaffolding; increase reader discovery.

Check for:

- Post-scene explanation trimmed where scene earned the insight
- Inquiry scaffolds varied or removed where accumulation carries the turn
- Explanations-of-explanations cut
- Pattern labels preserved; reader arrives before label
- Ch 5 untouched

Spec: [`docs/agents/09-trust-the-reader.md`](agents/09-trust-the-reader.md)

## Pass 14 — Memorable terrain & quiet discovery

**Goal:** More memorable terrain; less visible scaffolding; scenes carry weight; patterns feel earned.

Spec: [`docs/agents/10-memorable-terrain-quiet-discovery.md`](agents/10-memorable-terrain-quiet-discovery.md)
