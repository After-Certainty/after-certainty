# After Certainty — Beta Reader Feedback (2026)

Tracking doc for the `after-certainty/editorial-grounding` branch. Source: manuscript read-through feedback (May 2026).

## Overall assessment

- One of the clearest and most emotionally mature books in the portfolio
- Strong bridge/capstone into the broader series
- Biggest risk: **over-coherence** — uniform abstraction density and chapter rhythm
- Single biggest improvement: **5–8 short grounded vignettes** (150–400 words) as recognition anchors

## What is working (preserve)

- [x] Consistent emotional posture: serious moral reflection under epistemic/institutional instability
- [x] Three-part arc: dismantling → practicing → enduring
- [x] Prose clarity (Feynman-simple target)
- [x] Avoids "secret prescription" trap (revisability, proportionality, restraint)
- [x] **Ch 2** — emotional center; keep hinge lines on explanation as moral substitute
- [x] **Ch 9** — sharpest portfolio insight; keep "interpretation begins as care…"
- [x] **Conclusion** — restrained; no manifesto crescendo
