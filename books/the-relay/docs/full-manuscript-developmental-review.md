## Headline

The book no longer feels like an experiment. It feels like a **real novel that found its subject**.

The problems have changed: not essay-disguised-as-fiction, but **fiction strong enough to hide the best ideas**; not missing clarity, but missing **scale, strangeness, and uneven escalation**.

**North star for expansion:** Add life around ideas—scenes, character moments, sensory experience—not analysis or bulk word count.

**Reader test (every added passage):** Emotionally alive? Reveals character? Deepens mystery? Makes the world feel bigger? If yes, keep—even if messy for an agent checklist.

## Expansion priorities

### 1. Uneven chapter gravity

Readers must feel escalation. **Gravity chapters** (~4,000–4,500 words): **6, 9, 10, 11, 13, 14, 19, 22, 25**. Standard chapters ~3,200–3,500.

### 2. Wonder before cleverness — wonder ≠ description

Wonder = **character + scale colliding** through what the POV notices.

- *Weak:* "The gate was ten kilometers wide."
- *Strong:* "Cai had spent twenty years maintaining a structure so large he had never seen all of it."

Anchor locations: Drift Markets scale, fringe flight, Archive Moon interiors, Pilgrim Road, First Gate approach, corridor transit, reflected cities.

### 3. Antagonist faces (not villains)

Conflict: people doing what makes sense **locally** create failure **globally**.

| Type | Belief | Example face |
|------|--------|----------------|
| **Vendor-sufficiency** | Green readings = safety; expedited clearance is care | Clearance rep, mirror-tech lawyer |
| **Measurement-sufficiency** | Clusters are actionable; decimals justify delay | Jun-adjacent bureau aide |
| **Border-sufficiency** | Jurisdiction lines protect; pamphlets are prudence | Drift pamphlet runner, separatist kiosk |

### 4. Character voice divergence

**Test:** Remove POV name from three paragraphs—still identifiable?

| POV | Prose leans toward |
|-----|-------------------|
| **Lena** | Concrete, practical, people-first—routes, bodies, waiting, obligations |
| **Jun** | Patterns, compression, classification |
| **Toma** | Records, evidence, time, citation |
| **Cai** | Physical systems, wear, maintenance, seals |
| **Nadi** | Costs, margins, adaptation, fuel burn |
| **Sera** | Stories, communities, memory, unhurried pace |
| **Veya** | Dry precision, institutional grammar, reframes without sermon |

**Gap:** Lena and Jun sometimes share systems-thinker cadence—sharpen in expansion.

### 5. Cai / Toma weight

Reader pull toward **fixing** (Cai) and **uncovering** (Toma). Prioritize Ch. 13, 19, 22 expansion.

### 6. Pipeline serves story

Agents refine; they do not sand alive scenes. See [agents/README.md](./agents/README.md).
