## Wrong theory arc — separatist sabotage

**Rule:** Explains ~80% of early evidence. Fails when maintenance/coordination truth emerges.

| Phase | Evidence for sabotage | Counter-evidence (don't foreground yet) |
|-------|----------------------|----------------------------------------|
| I | Silence + border grievance threads; no reason code | Marks at sites; keeper language in threads (weak) |
| II | Reroute patterns; jurisdictional fees; Drift panic | Nadi's price lead time; fringe corridors viable |
| III | Archive gaps misread as **deliberate purge** | Toma's slope — gradual, budget ink, vendor consolidation |
| IV | Collapses | Cai: seals fine; rosters thinned; marks = proof of work |
| V | Echoes only | Communities rebuild without needing villain |

**Jun's role:** Models sabotage cluster expertly; professional confidence peaks Act I–II; strains Act II–III when price/narrative lead times invert; humbled Act IV–V.

## Act III misread — purge vs erosion

**Setup (Toma Ch. 7 probe):** Roster attrition, budget strike-throughs, vendor consolidation — **slope not event**.

**Misread (Ch. 14):** Lena + others read page nine list as coordinated purge of relay guilds. Fear wants faces.

**Reframe (Ch. 15, Veya):** Same data = institutions forgetting to notice weakness. *"Most don't fail because of their weaknesses. They fail because they stop noticing them."*

**Payoff:** Gate closures are symptom. Disease is social — eroded memory, invisible maintenance, meaning faster than facts.
