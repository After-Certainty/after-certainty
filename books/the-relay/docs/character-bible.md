# Character bible — The Relay

**Related:** [project-spec.md](./project-spec.md) · [civilization-bible.md](./civilization-bible.md) · [voice-spec.md](./voice-spec.md)

Each entry: job, **independent goal**, sees/misses, voice/friction with Lena, anti-caricature notes.

---

## Lena — Courier

**Job:** Interstellar courier; carries manifests, messages, small cargo between worlds.

**Independent goal:** Keep routes open; stay in contact with Arin.

**Sees:** Breadth — movement, patterns across civilizations, many worlds.

**Misses:** Any single domain deeply; reluctance to act when patterns demand it.

**Arc:** Observer → participant.

**Voice:** Clear, reflective, visual; notices what others ignore; rarely names themes aloud.

**Friction:** Impatient with theory; drawn to people who see differently.

**Does not solve alone** — assembles fragments from others.

---

## Arin — Lena's sibling

**Job:** Mirror-maintenance coordinator on **Orinreach Basin** (one world, southern district) — seasonal work on reflective navigation aids tourists misuse.

**Independent goal:** Keep Orinreach Basin's mirror corridors safe and honest through storm season.

**Complementary to Lena:**

| Lena | Arin |
|------|------|
| Breadth | Depth |
| Moves | Stays |
| Many worlds | One world intimately |

Lena looks for patterns across civilizations. Arin reminds her real people live inside those patterns.

**Sees:** Local weather, community rhythms, what reflection navigation actually requires.

**Misses:** How gate politics elsewhere reshape his weather funding.

**Agency:** Sends fragmented, thoughtful messages; solves problems on his side. **Not helpless. Not rescue bait.**

**Sample message (pre-suspension):**

> Storms over the southern mirrors again. You'd laugh if you saw the tourists trying to navigate by reflection.

**Voice:** Dry warmth; specific; short sentences; grounded in place.

---

## Nadi — Market Pilot

**Job:** Captain, small independent freight vessel (*Quick Margin*).

**Independent goal:** Keep her business alive.

**Sees:** Incentives, adaptation, ground truth — route shifts, prices, shortages before experts.

**Misses:** Larger context; the why.

**Voice:** Consequence-first; impatient with rumors; numbers and routes.

**Friction with Lena:** Nadi doesn't care why; Lena can't stop asking.

---

## Jun — Consensus Analyst

**Job:** Studies public **belief** flows — rumors, narratives, confidence, cohesion (civilization-specific profession).

**Independent goal:** Build first predictive model of civilization-scale belief formation; prove his profession matters.

**Personal arc:** Model starts **failing during the crisis** — forced to see people aren't merely data.

**Sees:** Meaning, narratives, social coordination.

**Misses:** Individual experience; people become data points.

**Secondary:** Graduate assistant **Corin** (Ch. 6). Not **Pell** (Henn's apprentice, Sera Ch. 4).

**Key line:** *"People don't wait for certainty. They build with whatever certainty they can find."*

**Voice:** Pattern language; curious; can seem cold until he's wrong.

---

## Toma — Station Archivist

**Job:** Preserves records on Archive Moon — centuries of institutional memory.

**Independent goal:** Preserve records before they're lost.

**Sees:** Long-term patterns, institutional memory, slow decay.

**Misses:** Immediate human needs, urgency.

**Behavior:** Responds to urgent questions with historical examples — frustrating, often right (institutions forget the same lessons).

**Chapter shape:** Detective, not exposition — query → investigate → surprise → revise → report.

**Form of attention:** Memory (long spans; patience as doubt).

**Colleague:** Veya (language stacks) — dry mutual knowledge on page.

**Voice:** Measured; citation habits; gentle stubbornness.

---

## Sera — Pilgrim

**Job:** Travels Pilgrim Road — collects stories, helps communities, listens.

**Independent goal:** Help communities adapt and stay connected.

**Differentiation:** First person Lena notices **oddly unconcerned** by gate disruption — not because she knows something; years watching communities adapt to uncertainty.

**Sees:** Values, belonging, motivation — people move on meaning, not evidence alone.

**Misses:** Technical mechanisms.

**Not mystical on the page** — deeply practical.

**Seed schedule:** Act I brief encounter → II crossing → III story heard → IV substantial → V payoff.

**Voice:** Warm; questions more than statements; unhurried.

---

## Cai — Maintenance Keeper

**Job:** Inspects old infrastructure everyone assumes works.

**Independent goal:** Maintain neglected systems; find small problems before large ones.

**Sees:** Fragility, dependencies, hidden work.

**Misses:** Politics.

**Leaves maintenance marks** — tiny symbols at inspection sites.

**Voice:** Quiet; specific; uncomfortable with praise.

---

## Veya — Historian of Failures

**Job:** Studies collapse — failed colonies, dead institutions, vanished cultures.

**Independent goal:** Preserve **lost languages** and extinct cultural records.

**Loves:** Lost languages — collapse study is **preservation**, not doom.

**Sees:** Warning signs, recurring patterns.

**Misses:** Hope.

**Key line:** *"Most don't fail because of their weaknesses. They fail because they stop noticing them."*

**Voice:** Precise; unexpectedly tender about language; dry humor.
