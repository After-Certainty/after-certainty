# Civilization bible — The Relay

**Author-facing.** Enough consistency for exploratory chapters and full draft. Not reader-facing exposition.

**Related:** [iconic-locations.md](./iconic-locations.md) · [project-spec.md](./project-spec.md)

---

## Scale

**Settled human space:** ~400 worlds and major stations linked by the gate network. Not a galactic empire — a **mesh** of places people actually live, trade, and visit.

**Naming (places):** Gates — astronomical/temporal (*Vesper Gate*). Settlements — geographical (*Orinreach Basin*, *Lake Orin* district names). See [iconic-locations.md](./iconic-locations.md). **Do not use Velorum place names** (e.g. Kestrel) in this book.

**Gate travel:** Common for couriers, traders, officials, and anyone with reason. Most people on any given world **never use a gate in a given year** — but everyone knows someone who has. Like international flight: normal, not daily.

**Age:** Gates operating ~700 years in current form. Civilization **feels** ancient; institutional memory **is** patchy.

---

## Communication

- **Quantum relays:** Near-instant text/data between linked nodes. Fast, cheap, ubiquitous for written messages.
- **Courier packets:** Physical or high-trust digital bundles for places relays don't reach cleanly or when signatures matter.
- **Pilgrim carry:** Stories, warnings, and context still move person-to-person on the Pilgrim Road — slower, differently weighted.

Lena's job exists because relays carry **information**, not always **judgment** or **trust**.

---

## Economy and jobs

**Prestigious:** Relay administration (declining), gate security (over-funded), consensus analysis (new), venture charter law, orbital design.

**Ignored until failure:** Maintenance keepers, mirror crews, archive junior staff, relay guild successors (where they still exist), pilgrim support infrastructure.

**Common:** Freight, local fabrication, habitat services, education, mirror tourism (on curved habitats).

People **incorrectly believe** the gate network is self-maintaining — "the gates have always worked."

---

## Education (children)

- **Upside-down geography** on curved habitats (Ribbon, some orbitals): districts overhead are real places with names.
- **Gate etiquette** taught young: don't block transit corridors, respect suspension notices.
- **History:** Heroic gate-builders as myth; maintenance as boring footnote — **wrong emphasis**.

---

## First Gate myths

- Built by "the First Pair" (no consensus on who).
- **Myth:** gates were gift, self-sustaining, morally pure.
- **Reality (story truth):** always maintained by people and institutions; coordination mattered as much as machinery.

People **incorrectly believe:** someone at the center still coordinates everything.

---

## Gate suspension (story present)

- Rare but precedented; explanations usually follow within days.
- **Current crisis:** explanation absent; behavior shifts anyway.

---

## Wrong popular beliefs

| Belief | Truth (story) |
|--------|----------------|
| Gates are autonomous | Maintained by people and institutions |
| Central authority still coordinates | Coordination institutions eroded |
| Sabotage explains everything | Symptom; erosion is social |
| Maintenance is engineering only | Relationships and oversight mattered |
| Pilgrims are backward | Carry trust and meaning relays don't |

## Everyday texture

- Food, dialect, and fashion vary by world; Drift Markets blend all three.
- Most people have **local** primary loyalty; gate mesh is **practical**, not ideological unity.
- Uncertainty produces **narratives faster than facts** — Jun's profession exists because this is measurable.
