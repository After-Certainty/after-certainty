# Symbol Migration Plan — The Relay

**Purpose:** Replace the retired maintenance-mark symbolic system with architecture grounded in the relay act (receive · carry · pass) and the move/stay axis.

**Mode:** Cut · replace · merge — not one-for-one repaint.

---

## Core

**The Relay** — how responsibility moves through people.

The title is a verb disguised as a noun. Supporting images answer: *what is being relayed, and by whom?*

---

## Axis

**Move / Stay** — the two ways people participate in a civilization.

- Lena learns because she **leaves**.
- Arin learns because he **stays**.
- Neither could solve the mystery alone.

**Crystallization targets:** Ch. 3 · Ch. 11 · Ch. 25 · Ch. 27

## Validation rules

1. **First-encounter** — Symbol understandable on first read. Gate scenes: character before monument.
2. **Collapse** — If removed from architecture, does the system collapse? (Storm glass: no → texture only.)
3. **Cut vs replace** — Default cut. Replace only when scene still needs an image.
4. **Person-before-monument** — Hands in frame for compass, light handoff, gate scenes.
5. **Transfer** — Reader sees the pass, not hears about carrying.
6. **Compass scope** — Beat about Lena and Arin, or cut the stamp.
7. **POV relay** *(soft)* — Most POV chapters show receive · carry · pass. Don't manufacture handoffs.
8. **Reader memory** — After a chapter, would a reader remember a **person/action** or a **symbol/framework**? Revise if the latter.
