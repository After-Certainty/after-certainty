# Act I — pacing & stakes audit

**Date:** 2026-06-17 · **Scope:** Ch. 1–5 (~17,124 words) · **Question:** Does each chapter change Lena's *situation*, not just her *angle list*?

**Related:** [act-1-developmental-review.md](./act-1-developmental-review.md) · [mystery-beat-sheet.md](./mystery-beat-sheet.md) · [act-2-editorial-brief.md](./act-2-editorial-brief.md)

## Executive summary

**Verdict:** Act I pacing is **good enough to ship with light trims**, not a structural rewrite. The arc (observe → investigate → commit) lands. **COMMITTED timing is correct** — early in Ch. 5 with the rest of the chapter spent on cost and departure.

**Main drag:** Three patterns repeat across chapters:

1. **Second Jun scene** (Ch. 2 lip + Ch. 4 briefing) — same function, similar beats.
2. **Keeper/work-language beats** stacked in Ch. 3 — same insight three times before the handoff pays.
3. **Ch. 2 afternoon courier montage** — procedural texture, zero situation delta.

**Information vs situation:** Strong through Ch. 3 (Arin thread + paid handoff + stamp). **Ch. 4 is the softest chapter** — mostly confirms what Ch. 2–3 already filed. Ch. 5 restores momentum immediately.

**Editor note on suspense type:** Act I hook is intellectual (*What happened to Vesper?*) before physical (*Is Arin in danger?*). That is intentional for social SF; emotional stake grows via sibling relay, not danger beats. Audit does not recommend forcing danger — only tightening **cost** and **irreversibility** where scenes only add angles.

## COMMITTED timing

### What works

- **Commit at ~line 45 of Ch. 5** (~15% into chapter) — reader gets **post-commit** pages: clerk strips, Dael, lake bookend, departure hall, Drift crossing, Nadi seed. Decision is not the last beat; **consequence and motion** are.
- **Math-before-commit opening** (renewal vs commitment vs wait) earns the keystroke. Three-column desk scene is the right emotional runway.
- **Ch. 4 ends on TRACKING / not yet** — creates one-chapter tension before Ch. 5. Acceptable if Ch. 4 is trimmed; slightly long as written.

### Risks (minor)

| Risk | Severity | Note |
|------|----------|------|
| Reader feels "still not leaving" through all of Ch. 4 | Medium | Ch. 4 adds no ledger change; only files more evidence |
| Priority handoff (Ch. 3) vs COMMITTED (Ch. 5) | Low | Two costs — intentional: handoff = emotional spend; commit = route spend. Could confuse if reader thinks handoff *was* commitment |
| Arin *Travel safe* before commit | Low | Two-word reply lands after COMMITTED in ms order — actually **after** commit (Ch. 5 ~line 161). Good: brother acknowledges move once she's already committed |

### Recommendation on timing

**Do not move COMMITTED earlier** (e.g. into Ch. 4). Ch. 5 structure is the strongest Act I engineering. If tightening, **trim Ch. 4 accumulation**, not Ch. 5 decision placement.
