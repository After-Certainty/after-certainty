# Synopsis — The Relay

**Living plot spine.** When this and [project-spec.md](./project-spec.md) diverge, reconcile here first.

---

## ACT I — THE ABSENCE

Lena, courier on the Ribbon, notices a transit bulletin: one gate suspended, no explanation. Inconvenience first — delays, grumbling. Explanations usually arrive faster than solutions; this time, silence.

Different worlds produce different stories: sabotage, hidden war, control, collapse preparation. She encounters **Jun**, consensus analyst, fascinated by narratives forming around absence. **Sera** crosses her path — oddly unconcerned, mid-errand, own story visible.

**Arin's** last message arrived days before suspension. Lena worries about his route home. **Maintenance marks** catch her eye; most ignore them.

Wrong theory seeds: **separatist sabotage**.

---

## ACT II — THE CASCADE

More routes unreliable — behavior changes without panic. **Nadi** shows trade maps reorganizing; she knows something is wrong before experts. **Jun** tracks belief cascades; his model strains. **Sera** again.

Lena cannot confirm Arin is safe. Sabotage narrative explains most of what she sees.

---

## ACT III — THE FRAGMENTS

Lena follows records to **Toma** on Archive Moon. Missing institutions — relay guilds, oversight networks. Archive evidence **misreads** as deliberate purge (conspiracy) before **Veya** reframes: civilizations fail when they stop noticing weaknesses. Gate closures are symptom.

Maintenance marks everywhere. Stories of **Sera's** community work reach Lena.

---

## ACT IV — THE KEEPER

**Cai** reveals gates mostly function — coordination institutions eroded through assumption, not conspiracy. Maintenance marks = caretaker evidence. **Sera** — substantial interaction. Wrong theory collapses.

---

## ACT V — THE RELAY

No central savior. Communities rebuild human relays: archivists, pilgrims, pilots, keepers, analysts. **Sera's** earned insight: responsibility moves person to person; the true relay was always human.

Lena and **Arin** reconnect within human scale. Adaptation without certainty returning.
