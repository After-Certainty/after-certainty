# Guide 5 — Character Guide

*The Relay Design Handbook · the cast. Assumes the [Novel Constitution](./01-novel-constitution.md) and [Plot & Integration](./02-plot-and-integration.md) (both frozen). Drafted ahead of World Design and Artifacts at the author's direction; references to species, places, and artifact forms are left as **[TBD → World]** / **[TBD → Artifacts]** to be reconciled when those guides exist.*

> **Status:** Draft v1, written to be argued with.

This guide owns the **people and their interiority**. It does not own integration events or story causation (that is Plot), the cultures or species themselves (World Design), the artifacts they use (Artifacts), or how they sound on the page beyond a one-line fingerprint (Writing Style). Plot defined each character's *position* and *interest* as plot forces; here those forces become someone you could recognize across a room.

---

## Casting laws

Three laws from the Constitution govern every entry below. They are not optional flavor; they are how the cast stays honest to the book's thesis.

1. **The epistemic condition.** Every character is, at once, **right about something, wrong about something, and dependent on someone else for something.** Each entry states all three. If a character is only right, they are a mouthpiece; if only wrong, they are a target.
2. **No one exists to correct or be corrected.** No character is in the book merely to be proven wrong, and none is here merely to deliver the truth. The clever are also blind; the overlooked also know.
3. **Species never determine character.** Which characters are human and which are not is a World Design decision (only **Lena, and therefore her sibling Arin, are confirmed human**). When species are assigned, no trait, virtue, or flaw may derive from species. **Humans are a species too** — they have systematic blind spots like anyone else; humanity is not the invisible default. *(World Design owns "what does humanity fail to notice?")*
   - **The practical form — definition by resistance.** Once species are assigned, the interesting question is not a character's species *default* but **how this character resists it.** Write "**[species] *but*…**", never "**[species] *therefore*…**" — *"she's Orenth, but she can't sit still"* rather than *"she's Orenth, therefore patient."* Characters are partly defined by where they push against their culture's grain; that resistance is the anti-essentialism made concrete (and becomes a per-character note once species exist). **Species assignments themselves are deliberately *not* fixed yet** — let the characters earn them on the page rather than freezing a roster in advance.

A note shared with Writing Style: **characters argue positions; readers infer interests.** The "Position" lines below are spoken aloud in the book; the "Interest" lines are almost never said — they are dramatized, not declared.

## How to read each entry

- **Function** — their job and plot-function (no more than Plot grants).
- **Position / Interest** — what they argue / the need beneath it (integration participants only).
- **Relay role** — what they *receive*, what they *pass*, who they *connect*. (Their place in the network — not how the climax unfolds; that is Plot.)
- **Fingerprint** — recognizable by · always notices · consistently overlooks · the mistake they repeat · surprised by · delighted by · annoyed by · finds funny.
- **Epistemic condition** — right about · wrong about · dependent on.
- **Indispensable interest** — the legitimate interest this character protects that *no one else does*. The standard is deliberately high: not "what perspective or skill do they bring?" but **"what would the civilization lose if this person disappeared?"** If the honest answer is "nothing the others don't also cover," the character is redundant and must be rethought or cut.
- **Keeps going because** — the persistence answer: *when everyone else would stop, why does this character continue?* This is distinct from motivation, and it should **evolve across the book** — its evolution *is* the character arc, running alongside the plot arc. When the reason a character continues changes, the character has grown.
- **Interior** — what the role costs them; the arc inside.
- **Voice (one line)** — a fingerprint for Style to execute, not the full register.

## Lena — the courier *(protagonist; human)*

- **Function:** Courier; breadth; the Move leg. Carrier **and** translator. The protagonist, though never the solver.
- **Position / Interest:** "These people need to hear each other." / Connection — and, beneath it, reaching the one person (Arin) she cannot reach.
- **Relay role:** *Receives* everyone's fragments, worries, and half-truths; *passes* them on — and renders them intelligible to each other; *connects* the experts who never meet, and finally the two networks.
- **Fingerprint:**
  - *Recognizable by:* restlessness; she reads rooms the way she reads routes; can't sit still without cataloguing exits and cargo.
  - *Always notices:* how information moves — who isn't being heard, what a notice leaves out, where a story has a seam.
  - *Consistently overlooks:* her own exhaustion, and the possibility that someone else could carry this.
  - *Mistake she repeats:* taking on too much; mistaking **carrying** for **fixing**.
  - *Surprised by:* what staying still can teach her.
  - *Delighted by:* a clean handoff; the moment two people finally understand each other.
  - *Annoyed by:* willful certainty; people who would rather be sure than be right.
  - *Finds funny:* the gap between official notices and ground truth; bureaucratic self-importance.
- **Epistemic condition:** *Right about* — the single-cause story is wrong, and connection is the work. *Wrong about* — that she must personally hold it all; she underrates depth and staying. *Dependent on* — Arin for depth, and everyone for the fragments she carries.
- **Indispensable interest:** **intelligibility between strangers** — the labor of making genuinely different people understandable to one another. Without her the network has carriers, but nothing that renders its fragments mutually legible; the pieces exist and never cohere.
- **Keeps going because** *(evolves — this is her arc):* **Act I** "I'm trying to get home." → **Act II** "I have to find Arin." → **Act III** "I need to understand." → **Act IV** "Someone has to carry these observations." → **Act V** "We all do." The reason widens from private need to shared responsibility; the last answer is the book's emotional thesis in her mouth.
- **Interior:** The toll of always moving and never staying; loneliness of being the conduit through which others connect; fear for Arin as the engine under everything. Her arc runs from *"I have to carry everything"* to *"I am one relay among many,"* and from Move-against-Stay to Move-with-Stay.
- **Voice (one line):** Dry, observational, understated; reaches for route-and-cargo metaphors.

## Arin — the one who stays *(Lena's sibling; human; the Stay leg)*

- **Function:** Stays on **the Tarn** (the basin/lake world); deep local knowledge; **co-creator**, not victim — independently builds the depth-network of stayers.
- **Position / Interest:** "You can't understand a place you won't stay in." / Place — the dignity of knowing one world completely.
- **Relay role:** *Receives* the slow signals of one place across long time; *passes* continuity and stability; *connects* the stayers into a depth-web that is half the eventual Relay.
- **Fingerprint:**
  - *Recognizable by:* patience; an attention tuned to slow change; the habit of noticing what is *missing* over years.
  - *Always notices:* deviations from long baselines — the lake's color out of season, the fog arriving wrong.
  - *Consistently overlooks:* the value and urgency of breadth; can be dismissive of "people who only pass through."
  - *Mistake he repeats:* assuming depth alone is enough — the exact mirror of Lena's mistake.
  - *Surprised by:* how much breadth reveals that staying cannot.
  - *Delighted by:* a pattern confirmed across years.
  - *Annoyed by:* people who mistake speed for understanding.
  - *Finds funny:* outsiders' confident wrong guesses about his world.
- **Epistemic condition:** *Right about* — the local signal is real and irreplaceable. *Wrong about* — that local is sufficient. *Dependent on* — the Move-web to make depth travel.
- **Indispensable interest:** **the slow signal of one place** — knowledge that only surfaces across long staying. Without him, the deviations that appear only over years go unseen by everyone in motion; the civilization keeps mistaking the absence of a fast signal for the absence of a problem.
- **Keeps going because** *(evolves):* this place needs a witness and he is it → his witnessing is half of something larger than the place. From staying-as-duty to staying-as-building.
- **Interior:** The dignity and the loneliness of staying; sibling longing for Lena under the patience; the late vindication that staying was a kind of building all along.
- **Voice (one line):** Spare, grounded, weather-and-place; comfortable with silence.

## Nadi — the freight pilot

- **Function:** Freight pilot; ground truth; routes and markets. The Reroute position.
- **Position / Interest:** "People can't wait for certainty to eat." / Livelihood and the present — people fed *now*.
- **Relay role:** *Receives* route and trade ground-truth; *passes* maps hand to hand; *connects* worlds and markets through the paths she flies.
- **Fingerprint:**
  - *Recognizable by:* bluntness and heat (she carries the book's profanity, per Style); practicality you can set a watch by.
  - *Always notices:* the real flow of goods and people, and what is actually changing on the ground.
  - *Consistently overlooks:* the long view, and the worth of records and memory.
  - *Mistake she repeats:* dismissing anything not immediately useful — including Toma's archives and Jun's models.
  - *Surprised by:* the day the "useless" past turns out to matter.
  - *Delighted by:* a problem solved with her hands; people fed because of her.
  - *Annoyed by:* theorists, bureaucrats, and certainty-sellers in equal measure.
  - *Finds funny:* gallows humor; the absurdity of official explanations.
- **Epistemic condition:** *Right about* — ground truth contradicts the sabotage story; people need to eat now. *Wrong about* — stopgaps accelerate the erosion she reroutes around; the present is not enough. *Dependent on* — the depth and memory she dismisses.
- **Indispensable interest:** **the present, and the people who must live in it now** — the ground truth of how goods and people actually move today. Without her, the network optimizes for theory and memory while people go hungry; she is the civilization's reality check.
- **Keeps going because** *(evolves):* people have to eat today → feeding people now and keeping the world learning turn out to be the same job. From the present-against-the-future to the present-as-part-of-it.
- **Interior:** Pride in being useful; a fear of being made obsolete; the slow discovery that feeding people now and keeping the world learning are not opposed.
- **Voice (one line):** Blunt, salty, concrete; no patience for abstraction.

## Jun — the consensus analyst

- **Function:** Consensus analyst; studies how beliefs spread. The Centralize position. Tragic engine of Varik's rise (below).
- **Position / Interest:** "If nobody holds the whole picture, people panic." / Comprehension — a center that can be trusted again.
- **Relay role:** *Receives* belief-flows and data; *passes* models and analysis; *connects* — but abstractly, and his abstraction is **the relay gone wrong**: he turns living observation into a model that strips the people out.
- **Fingerprint:**
  - *Recognizable by:* measured speech thick with qualifiers; he hedges even when certain.
  - *Always notices:* patterns in how beliefs move through a population.
  - *Consistently overlooks:* the human cost of his abstractions, and that his careful caveats get stripped away by others.
  - *Mistake he repeats:* believing a better model will fix what is actually a human problem; trusting the map over the territory.
  - *Surprised by:* that certainty spreads *faster* than evidence — the one thing his models keep underestimating.
  - *Delighted by:* an elegant model that fits the data.
  - *Annoyed by:* sloppy thinking — and, more painfully, his own role in the cascade.
  - *Finds funny:* himself; wry, self-deprecating about the limits of his field.
- **Epistemic condition:** *Right about* — belief cascades are real and dangerous. *Wrong about* — that comprehension can be centralized, and that rigor protects against misuse. *Dependent on* — the distributed others for the knowing no model can hold.
- **Indispensable interest:** **clear sight of how belief itself moves** — the recognition that certainty spreads faster than evidence. Without him, no one in the cast can see the cascade *as a phenomenon*; the civilization is blind to the very dynamic that is destroying it.
- **Keeps going because** *(evolves):* he has to understand the cascade → he is implicated in it and must help repair what his work fed. From curiosity to responsibility.
- **Interior:** Pride in rigor curdling into guilt; the humbling that is the book's clearest **"no single genius"** beat — the smartest analyst learns his intelligence was never going to be enough, and was even part of the harm.
- **Voice (one line):** Precise, qualified, self-aware.

## Toma — the archivist

- **Function:** Archivist; memory and precedent. The Restore position. Holds the record the purge-misread is read from.
- **Position / Interest:** "We already knew how to do this. We forgot." / Memory — that nothing true be lost.
- **Relay role:** *Receives* and keeps records; *passes* history, precedent, and packets; *connects* the present to the past.
- **Fingerprint:**
  - *Recognizable by:* care with objects; slowness; a reverence for the kept thing.
  - *Always notices:* what has been lost, miscatalogued, or quietly discarded.
  - *Consistently overlooks:* that the past's conditions no longer hold.
  - *Mistake repeats:* treating restoration as the answer; over-trusting the record as if it were the world.
  - *Surprised by:* the gaps in the archive — the absence that first looks like a purge.
  - *Delighted by:* recovering a document everyone thought gone.
  - *Annoyed by:* carelessness; anything thrown away.
  - *Finds funny:* dry archival humor; the marginalia of long-dead clerks.
- **Epistemic condition:** *Right about* — precedent exists; memory matters. *Wrong about* — the past as template for the present. *Dependent on* — those who act now to make memory live.
- **Indispensable interest:** **memory — that nothing true is lost** — the keeping of precedent across time. Without her, the civilization keeps re-losing what it already solved and forgot, and cannot tell its real gaps from its records.
- **Keeps going because** *(evolves):* nothing true should be lost → memory must serve the future, not cage it. From preservation to release.
- **Interior:** The keeper's quiet grief at how much is already gone; learning that memory serves the future only when it stops being a cage.
- **Voice (one line):** Quiet, exact, antiquarian warmth.

## Cai — the keeper

- **Function:** Maintenance keeper at a gate; stewardship; teaches Lio. The Maintain position. His apprenticeship becomes the climax's template.
- **Position / Interest:** "We cannot abandon what generations entrusted to us." / Continuity — that what was handed down not be lost on his watch.
- **Relay role:** *Receives* knowledge from his predecessors; *passes* stewardship and teaching to Lio; *connects* the generations.
- **Fingerprint:**
  - *Recognizable by:* calloused competence; economy of motion; teaches by doing, not telling.
  - *Always notices:* the physical state of things — wear, the small fix before it becomes the large one.
  - *Consistently overlooks:* the non-local and systemic; he cannot see past his own gate.
  - *Mistake repeats:* assuming discipline alone preserves; hearing "change" as "abandonment."
  - *Surprised by:* that maintenance can be transformed without being betrayed.
  - *Delighted by:* a thing well-kept; an apprentice's first real competence.
  - *Annoyed by:* neglect; talk that replaces work.
  - *Finds funny:* deadpan, laconic; the rare dry remark that lands harder for its rarity.
- **Epistemic condition:** *Right about* — the machinery is mostly sound; stewardship is real work. *Wrong about* — that maintenance alone suffices against a non-local failure. *Dependent on* — the wider relay to show him what one gate cannot.
- **Indispensable interest:** **stewardship of the inherited working thing** — the daily keeping that nothing else in the cast performs. Without him, the physical machinery degrades locally, quietly, before anyone with a model or a map ever notices.
- **Keeps going because** *(evolves):* the charge was entrusted to him and he will not fail it → passing it on *is* how he keeps it. From holding to handing on.
- **Interior:** The weight of a charge he fears he is failing; the relief, hard-won, of learning that passing it on *is* keeping it.
- **Voice (one line):** Terse, practical, weighty.

## Lio — the apprentice

- **Function:** Cai's apprentice; the relay forward in time. Embodies the carrier↔watcher apprenticeship that templates the climax.
- **Relay role:** *Receives* apprenticeship — skill *and* doubt; *passes* future responsibility; *connects* the present to the future.
- **Fingerprint:**
  - *Recognizable by:* eagerness braided with a fear of not measuring up.
  - *Always notices:* the gap between what he's told and what he sees.
  - *Consistently overlooks:* his own growing competence.
  - *Mistake repeats:* deferring too much; not trusting his own fresh observation.
  - *Surprised by:* being trusted with something real.
  - *Delighted by:* getting it right unsupervised.
  - *Annoyed by:* being condescended to.
  - *Finds funny:* youthful irreverence toward the solemnity of his elders.
- **Epistemic condition:** *Right about* — fresh eyes catch what habit misses. *Wrong about* — his own readiness; he underrates himself. *Dependent on* — Cai, and on the courage to carry.
- **Indispensable interest:** **the future's capacity to receive** — fresh eyes now, and the unbroken transmission forward in time. Without him, skill dies with its holders and habit goes permanently unchallenged; he is the proof that the relay must reach the not-yet-arrived.
- **Keeps going because** *(evolves):* he wants to prove he measures up → he has been trusted to carry, and trust is its own reason. From seeking approval to accepting responsibility.
- **Interior:** The making of someone who will one day receive · carry · pass without being told to.
- **Voice (one line):** Earnest, questioning, quick.

## Sera — the pilgrim

- **Function:** Pilgrim; people and meaning; community. The Trust-the-people position. **Seed her from Act I onward — never only an Act V spokesperson.**
- **Position / Interest:** "The people will hold. Trust them to adapt." / Human meaning — faith that people carry each other.
- **Relay role:** *Receives* community stories; *passes* meaning, hope, and road-teaching; *connects* strangers into community.
- **Fingerprint:**
  - *Recognizable by:* an unhurried calm; presence; she is the least anxious person in any room.
  - *Always notices:* people — who is struggling, who is being left out.
  - *Consistently overlooks:* mechanism and logistics; *how* the holding actually happens.
  - *Mistake repeats:* assuming goodwill is enough; vagueness where structure is needed.
  - *Surprised by:* when meaning itself turns out to need scaffolding.
  - *Delighted by:* a community quietly holding together.
  - *Annoyed by:* almost nothing — her calm is real, not a pose.
  - *Finds funny:* gentle, warm; humor that includes rather than cuts.
- **Epistemic condition:** *Right about* — people hold; meaning sustains. *Wrong about* — will without mechanism produces nothing. *Dependent on* — the builders for the how.
- **Indispensable interest:** **the bonds that make people hold together** — meaning, and the community that carries it. Without her, even a correct solution has no one willing to take it up; she protects the social fabric that every mechanism depends on and that no mechanism can manufacture.
- **Keeps going because** *(evolves):* people need someone unafraid → meaning itself needs scaffolding, and she can help build it. From faith-alone to faith-with-structure.
- **Interior:** A faith tested by a problem goodwill cannot solve alone; the discovery that trust and structure are not enemies.
- **Voice (one line):** Warm, plain, rooted in people; almost never profane.

## Veya — the historian of failures

- **Function:** Historian of how civilizations decline; delivers the Discovery reframe. The Let-go position.
- **Position / Interest:** "Every civilization that tried to restore the past fell." / Avoiding the collapse she has spent her life studying.
- **Relay role:** *Receives* lost languages and long patterns; *passes* warning signs and the reframe; *connects* this failure to the long history of not-noticing.
- **Fingerprint:**
  - *Recognizable by:* clear-eyed bluntness; the distance of someone who has read too many endings.
  - *Always notices:* the pattern of decline; the signs of a civilization that has stopped noticing itself.
  - *Consistently overlooks:* the immediate human stakes; she can be coldly right.
  - *Mistake repeats:* a letting-go that tips into abandonment; assuming decline is the only pattern.
  - *Surprised by:* that something genuinely new can be invented — that the cycle can break.
  - *Delighted by:* rarely; a pattern that, for once, does not end the way the others did.
  - *Annoyed by:* nostalgia and restoration fantasies.
  - *Finds funny:* dark, the dry irony of a historian.
- **Epistemic condition:** *Right about* — restoration fails; civilizations stop noticing. *Wrong about* — that decline is inevitable; she underrates invention. *Dependent on* — the builders to prove her own thesis incomplete.
- **Indispensable interest:** **the long memory of how civilizations decline** — the early-warning pattern no one else can read. Without her, this failure looks unprecedented, the warning signs go unnamed, and the civilization repeats the death it cannot see it is dying.
- **Keeps going because** *(evolves):* she must witness the pattern run its course one more time → for once the pattern might actually break. From resignation to a frightening, unfamiliar hope.
- **Interior:** The fatigue of foresight; the unfamiliar, frightening thing the ending asks of her — hope.
- **Voice (one line):** Dry, incisive, unsentimental.

## Varik — the embodiment of premature certainty

*(Plot owns his function and arc; this entry owns his inner life. **Not** an integration participant — he has no interest the solution serves; instead his necessity shrinks.)*

- **Function:** The most trusted public voice in the vacuum. The human form of *premature certainty vs. continuous learning*. **Not the villain.**
- **What he believes (and never doubts):** that people need direction; that somebody has to decide; that endless uncertainty kills civilizations. He never thinks of himself as a demagogue — he thinks of himself as the one willing to help when others only hedge. These beliefs are *partly true*, which is what makes him dangerous and human.
- **Relay role (a perversion of the relay):** he does not carry observations between people; he broadcasts conclusions. He *connects people to himself* — a hub that centralizes rather than distributes. He is what the Relay looks like when it collapses into a single interpreter.
- **Fingerprint:**
  - *Recognizable by:* warmth and clarity; the physical relief people feel when he speaks.
  - *Always notices:* what frightens people and what they are aching to hear.
  - *Consistently overlooks:* his own uncertainty, and the cost when he is wrong.
  - *Mistake repeats:* converging too early; mistaking decisiveness for learning.
  - *Surprised by:* reality breaking his story — and, more unsettling, people needing him *less* rather than turning on him.
  - *Delighted by:* a room coming into alignment; being trusted.
  - *Annoyed by:* hedging, "endless questions," anyone who will not commit.
  - *Finds funny:* genuinely charming — he uses humor to bond, and it works; this is a real gift, not a mask.
- **Epistemic condition:** *Right about* — people are suffering in the vacuum and someone must act; uncertainty can paralyze. *Wrong about* — that his certainty is knowledge. *Dependent on* — the vacuum, and the followers who need him to be sure.
- **Indispensable interest:** **the legitimate human need for direction under fear** — the orientation the honest, hedging others fail to supply. He protects this interest *badly* — by exploiting it rather than serving it — but no one else in the cast voices it, and without him the book would never admit that the hunger for certainty is real even when its supply is false. *(This is why he is necessary to the argument without being an integration participant: the need is legitimate; his answer to it is not.)*
- **Keeps going because** *(and, tellingly, this does* **not** *evolve):* people need him to be sure, and being needed is who he is. While every other character's reason for continuing widens, his stays fixed — that arrested arc *is* his tragedy. When the new Relay makes him needed *less*, he faces the growth he cannot manage: adapt, or double down.
- **Interior:** A sincere helper who cannot tell the difference between leading people and deciding for them. His tragedy is not exposure; it is gradual, gentle irrelevance — and the choice, at the end, between adapting (as some followers do) and doubling down (as others do).
- **Voice (one line):** Warm, fluent, confident; a gift for making the complex feel simple.
- **Backstory (motive; established in the Ch 9 calibration draft):** he has a sister who was on a route that stopped answering; in the early dark he *"spent twelve days buying lies because every lie pointed in her direction."* This is the private root of both his gift and his flaw — he learned that decisive action and a clean story were the only things that held him together when the network went silent, so he now hands others the certainty he once reached for. It also makes him a **deliberate mirror of Lena**: each has a sibling lost across the dark, but she keeps searching *and doubting*, while he converted the same wound into answers. *Consistency guard:* leave the sister's fate **open** — do not resolve her in passing. Her unresolved silence quietly indicts his certainty (he was never actually proven right) and should inform, not contradict, his Act IV absence and Ch 27 fade (adapt vs. double down).

### Why people love Varik

Not *why they follow him* — **why they love him.** This is the most important thing to get right about Varik, and the easiest to get wrong. If he is written as a manipulator, the book becomes a thriller about a bad man and loses its entire point. People love him because he is, in ordinary human terms, *good to them*:

- he remembers names;
- he visits disasters first, before the officials;
- he actually comforts frightened people — and is good at it;
- he explains complicated things clearly, so people feel less stupid and less alone;
- he volunteers; he shows up;
- he eats with ordinary people, not above them;
- and he **actually solves real problems** — a riot defused, a stalled convoy moving, people fed *today* — because premature certainty often produces excellent short-term results. (That short-term success, not deceit, is what makes him dangerous; the long-term cost is a civilization that stops learning.)

All of this is **real.** None of it is a mask. And therefore:

> **His failure is not empathy. His failure is premature convergence.**

He genuinely cares, and he genuinely helps — and then he closes the question too soon, supplies certainty the situation has not earned, and asks people to organize around an answer before one exists. The book must let the reader *feel his pull* — even love him a little — so that what he embodies is recognizable as a temptation, not a villainy. (The trap he dramatizes is *certainty mistaken for wisdom*, not unkindness.)

## The sibling spine — Lena and Arin

The Move/Stay tension is not abstract; it is a brother and sister. Lena learns by leaving and Arin by staying; each privately believes the other chose the lesser path; each is half a relay. Their reunion is the emotional form of the climax's structural fusion — **not a rescue.** Neither saves the other; they discover they have been solving opposite halves of the same problem. This is the human-scale stake the Constitution requires: the fear under the whole book is *what if she cannot reach someone she loves* — answered not by reaching across the dark to save him, but by their two networks meeting in the middle.

**Arin must cast a long shadow even while offstage.** He should *not* require more POV to stay present. Instead, keep him alive in Lena's mind through the middle movements — *"Would Arin notice this?"; "Arin would already know what the lake's color means"; "Arin would never trust a record over the thing in front of him."* She carries his absence like cargo; she avoids a mistake because of something he once taught her; she sees a thing he'd love and has no way to send it. The reunion lands in proportion to how present he stayed while gone. (Avoids the failure mode where the sibling vanishes for two movements and the reunion feels unearned.)

## The Jun–Varik tragedy *(cross-character)*

The book's sharpest illustration of "knowing is not sufficient." **Jun publishes preliminary findings** — careful, hedged, provisional. **Varik strips the caveats and sells the conclusion as certainty.** Jun spends the rest of the novel living with *"my work helped create this."* It binds the two: the honest analyst whose rigor is weaponized by the man who removes all doubt. It is also the cleanest dramatization of the Constitution's warning that **diversity and intelligence are not automatically wisdom** — Jun is brilliant and right and still part of the harm, because brilliance alone, ungoverned by the relay, leaks into the hands of premature certainty.

## Failure Modes

*Ways a draft can satisfy this guide and still flatten the cast. (Localizes "do not mistake diversity for co-creation" and "certainty for wisdom.")*

- **Characters become philosophical positions.** Each person is a walking argument rather than someone you'd recognize across a room. *Tell:* a character only ever speaks to their theme. *Instead:* the fingerprint, the humor, the persistence, the interior must exceed the position.
- **Species become stereotypes.** A trait is predictable from a character's culture. *Tell:* "their kind is like that." *Instead:* counter-examples are deliberate; species enriches, never determines.
- **Everyone is wise; no one is wrong.** The epistemic condition collapses into a cast of sympathetic sages. *Tell:* no character is visibly, confidently wrong about something. *Instead:* right *and* wrong *and* dependent, all three, each.
- **A character is decorative.** Removing them costs the civilization nothing. *Tell:* the indispensable-interest line is vague or duplicates another's. *Instead:* each protects something no one else does.
- **Flat persistence.** A character's reason for continuing never changes (the arc is only plot, not person). *Tell:* "keeps going because" reads the same in Act V as Act I — *except Varik, where that flatness is the point.*
- **Varik the manipulator.** He is written as a cynic working people. *Tell:* the reader never feels his pull. *Instead:* he is loved for real virtues; his failure is convergence, not empathy.

## Dramatization

*How character ideas become scenes — `idea → shown through… / not explained by…`.*

- **The epistemic condition** → *shown through* each character being visibly right and visibly wrong within the same scene; *not explained by* anyone announcing their own blind spot.
- **Position vs. interest** → *shown through* characters arguing positions while their interests leak out in what they protect; *not explained by* a character naming their underlying need.
