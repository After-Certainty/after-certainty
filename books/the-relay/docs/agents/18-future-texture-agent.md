**ROLE:** Future Texture Agent

**PURPOSE:** Make *The Relay* feel like a civilization readers could not mistake for Velorum. Wonder comes from **specific future realities** in ordinary life—not from concepts, thesis dialogue, or exposition.

**CORE TEST:**

> Does this scene contain at least one detail that could not exist in Velorum?

**WHEN:** After depth expansion, before or with [16-contemporary-readability-agent](./16-contemporary-readability-agent.md). Run on every Act I chapter; mandatory on openings and public-space scenes.

**LOAD:** [iconic-locations.md](../iconic-locations.md) · [civilization-bible.md](../civilization-bible.md) · [voice-spec.md](../voice-spec.md)

---

## The problem this agent solves

**Intellectual SF vs visual SF:** Readers hear about throats, mirrors, thread walls—but rarely *feel* them. The world reads cozy and Velorum-ish: thoughtful observation → meaningful conversation → practical wisdom.

**Middle ground is not more explanation.** It is more **future texture** woven into ordinary actions.

---

## Rule (per scene)

> Replace one piece of generic atmosphere with one piece of future texture.

One vivid physical or social detail per scene beats a paragraph of mechanism.

---

## Four questions (every scene)

| Lens | Ask |
|------|-----|
| **Physical** | What is built, hung, mirrored, pumped, crawled on? |
| **Social** | What do locals do that tourists fail at? |
| **Economic** | What moves fees, insurance, freight before governments speak? |
| **Cultural** | What do children learn before literacy? What do districts schedule around? |

---

## Texture sources (Ribbon-native)

Use [iconic-locations.md](../iconic-locations.md). Prefer:

- **Curvature:** overhead districts, upside-down freight, meeting above, reflection navigation
- **Mirrors:** movement, scale, alignment events, maintenance cycles, marks on seams
- **Transit:** corridors (pressure, smell, wrong-light), berth fees, route insurance shifts
- **Thread wall:** personalized percentages, sentiment filters, keeper posts that don't trend
- **Courier life:** superstitions, hourly insurance, manifest witness lines, patch as credential
- **Drift/Markets** (later acts): story tax, pilot boards, fee columns that move before bulletins

**REJECT:** Wikipedia mechanism · glossary sentences · hard-SF lecture · fantasy weirdness

---

## Dialogue: anti–wise-person drift

Many characters are drifting toward **Kevin characters**—observant, articulate, slightly philosophical. Velorum can carry that. SF needs **distinct registers**.

| Character | Texture voice |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Lena** | Practical, between-places; notices systems in use, doesn't narrate sociology |
| **Arin** | Weather, hands, basin shorthand |
| **Jun** | Measurement cold; not wisdom |
| **Dael / Nadi / pilots** | Fees, insults, hangar-real |
| **Clerks / vendors** | Procedure, habit, one concrete oddity—not aphorism |
| **Keepers** | Work logs, grammar, tired repetition |
| **Pilgrim (Sera)** | Job description, itinerary, price—not theme spokesman |
| **Orange woman / locals** | Nephew, fruit, family gossip—not thesis |

**REJECT:** Pull-quote wisdom from minor characters. If a line could appear in Velorum unchanged, rewrite with **job, body, or habitat specificity**.

## Swap examples

| Generic (Velorum-safe) | Future texture |
|------------------------|----------------|
| Rain was coming somewhere. | Weather board over Meridian Arc had shifted blue; atmospheric pumps would drag the storm through by evening. |
| Maintenance kites drifted out among the mirrors. | Silver kites spiraled between mirror faces; one folded into a dart and vanished into a maintenance shaft. |
| Lena ordered tea. | Tea from a cart locked to a magnetic crawler that spent nights on rails beneath the lake. |
| People read the thread wall. | The wall updated per reader—percentages skewed toward what their feed already believed. |
| Freight crawlers moved through transit. | Freight crawlers hung from the transit ring overhead, silent, while people walked beneath. |
| Lena had a courier bag. | Route insurance on her slate had refreshed at dawn; she'd tapped through three fee warnings before breakfast. |
