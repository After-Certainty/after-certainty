For seven hundred years, the gates have carried people between a thousand worlds, turning impossible journeys into ordinary departures. Lena has crossed them all her life as a courier — until the gate at Vesper shuts without warning, trapping her brother on the far side while the notices offer excuses instead of answers.

Then more gates fail. Circulation thins. Cities grow hungry and frightened, and people turn toward the nearest clear voice. Lena is not a soldier or a hero. She is stubborn enough to keep asking where an answer came from before the next gate closes, before certainty fills the silence, and before her brother is lost for good.
