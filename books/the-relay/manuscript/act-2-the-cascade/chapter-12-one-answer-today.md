# Chapter 12 — One Answer, Today

> STUB — to be drafted. POV: Lena · Location: a world where a route fails.

A route Lena cleared herself fails; the people on it don't come back. Varik says the thing she's most afraid of wanting to hear — *enough questions, enough waiting, one answer, today* — and, exhausted, for one moment she almost says yes. The lists surface again, and stay with her like a half-heard name.

Carries: the low point ("Lena almost believes him"); Arin seed #2; the mark it leaves — she nearly breaks. Withholds: the answer she can't yet imagine.
