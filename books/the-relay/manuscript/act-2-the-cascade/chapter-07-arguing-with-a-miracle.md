# Chapter 7 — Arguing With a Miracle

> STUB — to be drafted. POV: Lena · Location: a saved town.

On one world, acting on the sabotage theory saves a town — a route closed in time, and people who would have died don't. Lena tries to explain why the theory is wrong and sounds exactly like a crank arguing with a miracle. They stop listening; she is denied passage and has to barter her way out, and she starts to doubt her own judgment.

Carries: the sabotage story *works* (the reader feels the uncertainty); Lena denied passage (scene conflict); the mark it leaves — she begins to doubt herself. Withholds: why the theory is locally true and globally wrong.
