# Chapter 6 — Stranger and Larger

> STUB — to be drafted. POV: Lena · Location: several worlds (wonder beats).

The sabotage story spreads ahead of Lena as she crosses world after world. She passes through the weightless market — a vendor walking the air to reach her — and a world whose people navigate by sound. Hauling freight, she had stopped seeing how vast the thing she belonged to was; now she can't stop, and the bigger it looks, the more frightening it becomes that something this size could be coming apart with no one able to see all of it at once. (Wonder beats are environmental and cultural worldbuilding, not the two non-human species.)

Carries: wonder/scale; the "impossibly large" line. Withholds: the cause still invisible.
