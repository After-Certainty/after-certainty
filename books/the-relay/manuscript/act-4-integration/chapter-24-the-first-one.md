# Chapter 24 — The First One

> STUB — to be drafted. POV: Lena · Location: the dead gate.

Opens with the turn still warm in Lena — for the first time she is not carrying it alone (the bracket after Jun). Then the invention, almost embarrassingly small: they take an ordinary courier manifest and, below the cargo, each writes what they've seen go wrong, with the rule that whoever carries it next must add before passing it on — the courier manifest (Move) fused with the watcher list (Stay). Made in the open by several hands who were screaming at each other an hour before. The handoff — receive · carry · pass — and Cai teaching Lio become the template.

Carries: the open manifest (the climax; the fusion of two half-artifacts); apprenticeship template; the derivability test (*unforeseeable in advance, inevitable in hindsight*); Lena's warm aftermath (bracket close). Artifact: the first open manifest (living). Withholds: whether it will spread or die.
