# Chapter 23 — No Center

> STUB — to be drafted. POV: Jun (second Jun POV) · Location: the gatehouse.

The intellectual surrender, kept central but no longer the only one dramatized: Jun, who most wants a single mind that sees all, gives up the dream of the central model and accepts a method that produces sight *without* a center. From inside him the turn lands — the two halves are one broken thing; no one can hold it, and that is the point.

Carries: the centralize→distributed abandonment (intellectual); the two-halves recognition. Withholds: the object that makes it real.
