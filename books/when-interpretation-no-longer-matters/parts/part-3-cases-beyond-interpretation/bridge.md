# Part III

## Cases Beyond Interpretation

Part II named four modes. Part III asks whether they hold under pressure at scale.

These chapters are not interchangeable examples. Each case shows a distinct pattern:

- **Chapter 7 — Alignment:** Sun Myung Moon and the Unification Church—commitment before comprehension, interpretation downstream of belonging.
- **Chapter 8 — Identity saturation:** Hugo Chávez and chavismo—political authority absorbed into collective identity.
- **Chapter 9 — Total authority:** Adolf Hitler and the Nazi regime—public interpretation eliminated while private understanding persists.
- **Chapter 10 — Transitional drift:** Theranos and Elizabeth Holmes—interpretation narrows before it loses force.

Read comparatively. In each case ask:

1. What replaced interpretation here? 2. What still looked like interpretation but no longer mediated legitimacy? 3. What could not be repaired from inside the system's own language?

Part IV turns from cases to the lived residue: judgment, non-repair, and early recognition once the shift is underway.
