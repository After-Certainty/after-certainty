# Part I

## Where Interpretation Ends

Part I establishes the boundary the introduction names but does not yet demonstrate.

Readers often assume the failure is ignorance or bad faith. Part I tests a narrower claim: understanding can remain intact while interpretation stops coordinating legitimacy, consequence, or repair.

Chapter 1 marks that boundary through a case where assent preceded evaluation. Chapter 2 names the mechanism—**interpretive collapse**—not as the disappearance of thought, but as the loss of public consequence.

If Part I works, you should be able to say when explanation is still active but no longer institutionally operative.

Part II then examines what kinds of authority fill that gap.
