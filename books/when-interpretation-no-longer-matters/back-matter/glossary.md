# **Glossary**

Working terms used in this book. They are meant to be recognized in scenes, not memorized as a taxonomy.

**Alignment / sorting** — Communication that classifies audiences by loyalty or belonging rather than testing claims across a shared evaluative space.

**Anti-repair** — A condition in which criticism and interpretive activity remain intense—even mandatory—while losing the capacity to correct authority. The presence of criticism does not prove the existence of repair.

**Identity saturation** — Authority stabilized by identification with a leader, movement, or peoplehood so thoroughly that policy contradiction and factional disagreement do not dissolve legitimacy.

**Interpretation** — A shared public process by which meaning is evaluated, contested, and made consequential for legitimacy, accountability, and repair. Not merely private understanding or personal opinion.

**Interpretive leverage** — The capacity of understanding, reasons, or evidence to alter authority’s standing. Understanding can persist after leverage is gone.

**Narrative enclosure** — An interpretive system that remains active inside a bounded story while reclassifying external objections as persecution, testing, misunderstanding, fulfillment, or confirmation.

**Performative legitimacy** — Authority maintained through visible compliance and ritualized participation under threat, even when private belief is thin or absent.

**Prior assent** — Acceptance of an interpretive frame before—and as a condition for—evaluation of claims inside it. External disagreement may lack jurisdiction at the frame’s center without ending internal interpretive rigor.

**Repair** — Attempts to correct authority through transparency, accountability, dialogue, justification, or other means that presume interpretation can still change standing.

**Witness** — Judgment that records, refuses erasure, or remembers after the pathways that once connected recognition to consequence have been destroyed. Witness is not a substitute for repair; it is what judgment can still do when repair is gone.
