## Where analogies consistently break

| Comparison | Helps because… | Breaks because… |
|------------|----------------|-----------------|
| Peer review ↔ confession | Both invite correction from outside the self | Different authority, confidentiality, moral stakes, and ends |
| Meditation ↔ prayer | Both train attention | Different objects, communities, and accounts of what attention is *for* |
| Congregation ↔ research lab | Both extend perspective beyond the individual | Revelation, initiation, and membership differ from credentialing and falsification |
| Scripture ↔ scientific literature | Both preserve insight across generations | Different claims about inspiration, authority, and revision |
| Forgiveness ↔ postmortem | Both address harm and learning | Grace, justice, safety, and power asymmetry are not procedural equivalents |
| Sabbath ↔ digital detox | Both interrupt compulsive doing | Sabbath is covenant and gift, not optimization for Monday |
