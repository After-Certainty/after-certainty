# **Author's Note**

*Learning to See* is a work of comparative inquiry, not spiritual autobiography or apologetics. The opening scripture experiment, design-review scenes, and other personal passages are the author's unless otherwise noted. They are offered as doorways into questions about formation and perception, not as testimony intended to convert or to settle metaphysical disputes.

Comparative descriptions of religious, scientific, and organizational practices draw on primary texts, respected scholarship, and the sensitivity review documented in `docs/sensitivity-review.md`. Where traditions differ internally, the manuscript tries to name that diversity rather than speak for entire communities.

The author is a software engineer writing as a serious learner across epistemology, philosophy, and spiritual traditions—not as an ordained teacher or disciplinary expert in any single tradition surveyed here.
