## Purpose

Help the reader see why compression is necessary; how a careful economic release becomes a headline and then a partisan signal; why orientation differs from substitution; why averages, medians, and distributions can be accurate and still feel dishonest; why “adding more nuance” does not automatically restore trust.

## Opening Observation

A monthly economic report passes through several forms in a single day: technical release, headline, television segment, political verdict. Same underlying document; different social objects.

## Essay Movement

1. Observation — jobs or CPI release day metamorphosis
