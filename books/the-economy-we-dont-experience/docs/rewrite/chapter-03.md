## Purpose

Show where belief anchors when aggregates no longer settle the argument: receipts, rent renewals, insurance, medical bills, work schedules, childcare calendars, debt payments, household memory. Hold the tension that lived evidence is essential and incomplete—receipt cannot reveal the national pattern; national pattern cannot pay the receipt.

## Opening Observation

A nurse (or equivalent household witness) keeps photographs or a roll of receipt totals—not as a BLS rebuttal, but as a record that survives clips when the room debates whether inflation is “over.”

**Label:** nurse (and hardware-store / regional-banker) scenes are **composite / illustrative**.
