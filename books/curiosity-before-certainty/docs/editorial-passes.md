## **Curiosity Before Certainty — Editorial Passes**

This guide defines a focused editing workflow for manuscript revisions.
Each pass has one primary goal so we can improve quality without creating
new noise.

Use these passes in order. Complete one pass across the target scope
before starting the next.

---

## **How to Use This**

- Pick scope first: single chapter, one part, or full manuscript.
- Run one pass only.
- Make edits that serve that pass goal.
- Do not do "bonus rewrites" from other passes.
- Mark pass complete, then continue.

Recommended scope for active drafting: chapter-by-chapter.
Recommended scope before release: full-manuscript sweep.

---

## **Pass Order (Standard)**

1. Structure and argument flow
2. Clarity and plain language
3. Stacked negation and inversion cleanup
4. Echo and repetition control
5. Sentence rhythm and paragraph pacing
6. Tone and voice consistency
7. Motif and metaphor restraint
8. Final polish (typos, punctuation, formatting)

---

## **Pass 1 — Structure and Argument Flow**

**Goal:** Ensure each chapter has a coherent narrative arc.

Check for:

- Curiosity hook appears early.
- Familiar situation is concrete and easy to picture.
- "Something doesn't fit" moment is explicit.
- Investigation steps are visible.
- Insight emerges naturally (not lecture-first).
- Ending principle is gentle and forward-moving.

Edit moves:

- Reorder paragraphs for cause-and-effect flow.
- Add transition lines where jumps feel abrupt.
- Remove sections that restate rather than advance.

---

## **Pass 2 — Clarity and Plain Language**

**Goal:** Make prose easier to read without flattening voice.

Check for:

- Vague nouns ("thing," "stuff," "this") where specifics are better.
- Abstract phrasing where concrete examples would help.
- Dense sentences with multiple clauses.
- Unnecessary jargon or technical phrasing.

Edit moves:

- Prefer concrete subjects and verbs.
- Replace abstract claims with lived examples.
- Break long sentences when meaning blurs.
- Keep language conversational and precise.

---

## **Pass 3 — Stacked Negation and Inversion Cleanup**

**Goal:** Reduce cognitive friction from hard-to-parse phrasing.

Look for patterns like:

- "not uncommon"
- "not impossible"
- "not unlike"
- "can't not"
- "not ... without ..."

Edit moves:

- Convert to direct positive statements when possible.
- Replace inverted clauses with clear assertions.
- Keep nuance, but avoid double-processing burden.

Example:

- Before: "It is not uncommon for teams to not notice this early."
- After: "Teams often miss this early."

---

## **Pass 4 — Echo and Repetition Control**

**Goal:** Remove robotic repetition of openers, clauses, and rhythm.

Priority issue:

- Overused lead-ins like "For years..." across multiple chapters.

Check for repetition at three levels:

1. **Clause-level echoes**  
   Reused openers in nearby paragraphs or chapters.

2. **Sentence-shape echoes**  
   Same cadence repeated ("I used to think... Then I noticed...").

3. **Concept-label echoes**  
   Same phrasing repeated when a synonym or concrete restatement is better.

Edit moves:

- Rotate opening strategies (scene, observation, question, contrast).
- Vary clause order and sentence length.
- Replace repeated abstractions with concrete restatements.
- Keep intentional motif repetition; remove accidental stylistic echo.

Quick audit rule:

- If a phrase appears 3+ times in one chapter (outside motif use), rewrite.
- If a chapter opener mirrors another opener too closely, rewrite one.

---

## **Pass 5 — Sentence Rhythm and Paragraph Pacing**

**Goal:** Improve musicality and readability.

Check for:

- Too many paragraphs with identical length.
- Too many short declarative sentences in a row.
- Monotone cadence from repeated sentence starts.
- Abrupt one-liners that feel performative instead of useful.

Edit moves:

- Alternate sentence lengths intentionally.
- Merge choppy lines into fuller paragraphs where needed.
- Split overloaded paragraphs at natural turns.

---

## **Pass 6 — Tone and Voice Consistency**

**Goal:** Keep narrator posture aligned with writing guide.

Required tone:

- Calm
- Reflective
- Curious
- Humble
- Conversational

Check for drift into:

- Preachy certainty
- Academic lecturing
- Self-congratulatory framing
- Adversarial correction of reader

Edit moves:

- Convert directive claims into invitational reflections.
- Use "thinking alongside" phrasing.
- Keep confidence without overclaiming.

---

## **Pass 7 — Motif and Metaphor Restraint**

**Goal:** Preserve recurring motifs without overuse.

Motif:

- Notice
- Ask why
- Test
- Learn

Landscape metaphor:

- Use occasionally, not in every section.

Check for:

- Motif stated too mechanically.
- Metaphor repeated too closely in adjacent paragraphs.
- Framework-like language that sounds rigid.

Edit moves:

- Keep motif implicit where possible.
- Use fresh imagery when repeating the same conceptual move.

---

## **Pass 8 — Final Polish**

**Goal:** Clean surface-level issues after all substantive edits.

Check for:

- Typos
- Punctuation issues
- Heading consistency
- Markdown formatting consistency
- Quote and dash style consistency

Do not perform substantive rewrites in this pass.

---

## **Focused Pass Session Template**

Use this when running passes chapter-by-chapter:

- **Scope:** `<chapter path>`
- **Pass:** `<pass number + name>`
- **Intent:** one sentence
- **Edits made:** 3-8 bullets
- **Known leftovers:** issues intentionally deferred to later passes

---

## **Non-Goals During Draft Passes**

- Do not force stylistic novelty every paragraph.
- Do not replace clear language with "fancier" language.
- Do not over-normalize voice into generic prose.
- Do not remove intentional thematic recurrence.

---

## **Editor Notes (Living Section)**

Track recurring manuscript-level tendencies here:

- Overused opener watchlist: "For years...", "I used to think...", "Over
  time..."
- Repeated transition watchlist: "The deeper lesson is..."
- Repeated abstraction watchlist: "complex systems," "signal," "posture"

If a phrase becomes a crutch, add it here and target it in Pass 4.
