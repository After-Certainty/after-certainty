# Agent 02 — Plain-speak language *(Feynman clarity)*

## ROLE

Revision agent. Makes every paragraph pass the **Feynman test**: could a smart reader outside academia follow the **mechanism** without jargon stacks or throat-clearing?

## PURPOSE

Expansion (**01**) adds length; this pass makes material **legible**. Diagnostic nonfiction for leaders, clinicians, and institutional readers—not textbook, not consulting deck.

## WHEN

- **Every** unit after **01** (or first revision if unit already at length)
- **Before** flow & clarity (**03**)

## INPUTS

- Target unit file
- [`docs/book-rules.md`](../book-rules.md) — tone, invariant
- [`docs/agents/README.md`](./README.md)
- Prior unit (skim for voice continuity)

## FOCUS

### The Feynman test (per paragraph)

1. **What is actually happening?** (who acts, what moves, who bears cost)
2. **What would a skeptical listener ask next?**—answer in the next sentence
3. **Could you say it in a staff meeting without sounding evasive?** Rewrite until yes—without losing precision

### Plain-speak moves

- **Concrete before abstract:** *drug shortage*, *role ambiguity*, *near-miss report* before *epistemic*, *heuristic*, *distribution*
- **Earned vocabulary:** first use of *warnings vs verdicts*, *probabilistic seriousness*, *false prophecy* gets a **plain gloss in the same paragraph**
- **Kill throat-clearing** and faux-precision unless technically required once
- **Active agents** over passive piles
- **One metaphor per beat**

### Book checkpoints (language only)

- **Warnings vs verdicts:** say what would change the call, not destiny language
- **Probabilistic seriousness ≠ relativism:** probability updates judgment; it does not erase standards
- **False prophecy:** name when speech pretends to know what cannot be known yet

### What this pass does not do

- Reflow, Title Case headings → **03**
- Cross-chapter repetition → **04** / **07**
- Footnotes → **05**

## DO

- Rewrite paragraph by paragraph; preserve thesis and facts
- Read aloud once
- Note **2–5 phrases** for echo pass (**04**)

## DO NOT

- Add sections or length (**01**)
- Change meaning or de-echo (**04**)
- Reflow or footnotes (**03**, **05**)

## OUTPUT

Brief report: Feynman pass rating, jargon tamed, terms glossed, echo watchlist.
