# **Part III**

## **Probabilistic Truth and Moral Seriousness**

Parts I and II mapped appetite for absolutes and the misuse of patterns. Part III takes up **truth under distribution**—how a world that refuses simple binaries still demands moral seriousness.

Two chapters, one tension.

**The world refuses absolutes.** Outcomes cluster; causes multiply; categories leak at the edges. Chapter 5 argues that this is not an excuse for evasion. It is a description of the terrain judgment must cross.

**Probabilism is not relativism.** Updating confidence is not the same as dissolving standards. Chapter 6 defends probabilistic reasoning as a form of **care**—the willingness to act, revise, and remain accountable when the map is incomplete.

Part III prepares Parts IV–VI: institutions, leadership, and life after certainty all depend on this pairing. You cannot lead on warnings alone; you also cannot lead by pretending the world offers verdicts on demand.

Chapter 5 opens with why the world refuses absolutes—and what changes when we stop treating that refusal as moral cowardice.
