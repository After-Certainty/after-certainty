## Chapter title

Introduction — The Merge Button

## Governing question

What happens when production accelerates faster than the capacity to understand what was produced?

## Ordinary opening image

The green button beneath a pull request.

## Case / terrain

“AI Writes Faster Than Humans Can Review,” a July 2026 longitudinal study of a company pursuing a “2x” engineering mandate, followed 802 developers and 196,212 pull requests. Per-capita merged pull-request throughput eventually reached 2.09 times the pre-mandate baseline. Per-reviewer load roughly doubled, automated review overtook human review, and merge and revert rates remained stable during the study period.

## Conceptual movement

This is not a simple story of quality collapse. It is a story about a constraint moving. A faster step does not necessarily create a faster system. It can relocate the scarce work into review, integration, maintenance, or consequence.

## Questions to preserve

- When output doubles, which downstream capacity must also double?
- What does a stable revert rate prove, and what does it fail to reveal?
- When automated review overtakes human review, which decisions remain meaningfully owned?
- Is a bottleneck always waste, or can it be a safety boundary?
