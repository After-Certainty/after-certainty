# Part I

## The Need for Translation

When judgment can no longer coordinate action at scale, institutions still need language that survives distance, scrutiny, and audit. Part I follows that need into three domains where translation begins as a genuine solution.

Part I begins close to the objects through which judgment first becomes portable: a bed, a feed, a shelf. What cannot be said in the room must become something portable—a date on a whiteboard, a signal in a ranking model, a count on a CV. The chapters that follow do not ask whether translation should exist. They ask what happens when it becomes the only speech an institution can defend.
