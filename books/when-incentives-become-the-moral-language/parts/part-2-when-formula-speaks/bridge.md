# Part II

## When Formula Speaks

Part II asks what happens when substitution hardens into **public moral vocabulary**—when fairness, importance, legitimacy, and formation are spoken only through formulas institutions already know how to defend.

You will read four domains as one arc:

**Fairness** — procedure replaces contested judgment in workforce decisions.

**Attention** — visibility metrics replace editorial claims about public importance.

**Polling** — popularity signals replace deliberative ownership of representation.

**Formation** — measurable outcomes replace developmental judgment in schools.

Chapter 5 begins with fairness by formula: when procedure is the only language harm can hear.
