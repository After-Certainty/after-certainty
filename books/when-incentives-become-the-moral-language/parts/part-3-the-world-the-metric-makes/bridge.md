# Part III

## The World the Metric Makes

Part III turns from how translations are adopted to what they make visible—and what they make governable. Politics, education, and the week's ordinary surfaces show how proxies begin to shape not only what institutions measure but what they can safely say, teach, and represent.

The consequence shifts again: from distributed harm to civic and formative power—what representation can defend, what a score can hold, and when the institution forgets the proxy was a translation.
