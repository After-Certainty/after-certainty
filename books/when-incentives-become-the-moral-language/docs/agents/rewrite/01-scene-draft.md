# Rewrite Agent 01 — Scene draft

## ROLE

Primary rewrite agent. Drafts or substantially rewrites **one unit** from the chapter brief in [`WHEN_INCENTIVES_REWRITE_PLAN.md`](../../WHEN_INCENTIVES_REWRITE_PLAN.md) §5.

## PURPOSE

Replace essay-edition scaffold prose with **scene-first essayistic** chapters. Expand as the scene and argument require—not by repeating mechanism headings or hitting a word target.

## WHEN

- After author gates clear ([`author-approval-gates.md`](../../author-approval-gates.md))
- One unit per session (default)
- Follow production sequence in plan §16 when starting fresh units

## INVISIBLE CRAFT SEQUENCE (do not label in prose)

1. Enter through scene or object; stay until tension is felt
2. Observation and question emerge from scene
3. Widen once: history, policy, documented episode
4. Show why reasonable actors adopted the proxy (counterpressure required)
5. Evidence and primary sources (footnotes at new claims)
6. Reveal what proxy cannot carry—hold two truths
7. Return to opening image with altered meaning
8. Optional restrained **Core principle** only if earned

## DO

- Preserve passages listed in plan §12 (often near-verbatim)
- Use composite scenes with indefinite framing when not documented; no fake names/quotes
- Weave mechanisms into narrative prose
- Let the chapter take the room scene-led craft requires (no fixed word count)
- Migrate footnotes from essay-edition source where claims persist

## DO NOT

- Use labeled scaffold headings (`### The Original Moral Aim`, `### Moral Residue`, etc.)
- Open with "Central question:"
- Use "Three mechanisms…" as heading or counted list (Ch 9 only, once)
- End with "Chapter N+1 tests…"
- Add policy checklist or reform program
- Use legacy Agent 01 expansion patterns from [`legacy-essay-edition/`](../legacy-essay-edition/)

## OUTPUT

- Rewritten unit file at new path (when `index.md` restructured) or in-place draft on rewrite branch
- Brief report: approximate length, scene type (documented/composite), preserved passages, top 2 risks for Agent 02/03
