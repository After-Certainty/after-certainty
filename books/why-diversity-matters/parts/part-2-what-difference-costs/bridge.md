# Part II

## What Difference Costs

<!-- draft: outline only -->

Part II turns from what groups fail to notice to what it costs to say what you see.

Not everyone can afford to speak up, take a chance, or explain their reality on demand. Constraints shape options. Words land differently depending on who hears them. Representation can stop at visibility while decisions stay unchanged. And systems often reward people who are easy to understand.

Chapter 4 examines choices that aren't really choices. Chapter 5 follows why the same words can feel harmless to one person and threatening to another. Chapter 6 asks what inclusion means when power doesn't move. Chapter 7 names the cost of making unfamiliar experience legible to others.

If Part II works, you should feel the weight of constraints before treating misunderstanding as a simple communication problem.

Part III then asks what might help—and what still remains uncertain.
