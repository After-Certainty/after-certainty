# **Helping Shape This Book**

<!-- draft: outline only -->

This book will be a draft. I plan to have people help me shape it after setting up the initial version. The front matter that follows includes questions for conversations I hope to have along the way.

### **What I'm Looking For (Very Direct)**

I'm not looking for:

- People to speak for groups
- Perfect answers
- Agreement

I am looking for:

- People who live with risks others don't see
- People whose perspectives get filtered out
- People who can tell me where I'm missing something important

If this outline makes you think, "You're not seeing ___," that's exactly the conversation I want.

### **Why I'm Doing This This Way**

Most people who've read my earlier work come from backgrounds similar to mine. That's not a moral failure—but it does limit what I can see.

This book exists because I don't think good intentions are enough anymore.
