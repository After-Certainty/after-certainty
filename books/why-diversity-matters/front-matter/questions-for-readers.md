# **Questions for a Conversation About Perspective, Visibility, and Learning**

I'm working on a book exploring how people notice different things, how misunderstandings happen, and why diversity of experience may help us learn.

You don't need to prepare answers to all of these questions. In fact, one good story is often more valuable than ten short answers. If any of these questions remind you of an experience, a conversation, a relationship, a job, a family situation, a health issue, or a moment in your life that still sticks with you, that's exactly what I'm interested in discussing.

### **Stories About Being Seen (or Not Seen)**

- Tell me about a time people misunderstood something important about you.
- Tell me about a time someone made a reasonable assumption about you that turned out to be wrong.
- Tell me about a time you surprised people who thought they already understood you.

### **Stories About Seeing Something Others Missed**

- Tell me about a time you noticed a problem, risk, or opportunity before others did.
- Tell me about a time you struggled to convince people that something mattered.
- Tell me about a time you turned out to be right about something people initially dismissed.

### **Stories About Being Wrong**

- Tell me about a time you realized you had misunderstood someone.
- Tell me about a time you became convinced of something that later turned out to be incomplete or mistaken.
- Tell me about a time someone changed how you saw a situation.

### **Stories About Constraints**

- Tell me about a choice that looked easy from the outside but wasn't.
- Tell me about a risk that other people didn't fully understand you were carrying.
- Tell me about a situation where your options were more limited than others realized.

### **Stories About Influence**

- Tell me about a time you were included in a conversation but didn't feel influential.
- Tell me about a time someone listened to you but nothing changed.
- Tell me about a time your perspective unexpectedly changed a decision or outcome.

### **Stories About Communication**

- Tell me about a time you couldn't find the words to explain something important.
- Tell me about a time a conversation went wrong even though everyone involved had good intentions.
- Tell me about a time you realized two people were hearing the same words very differently.

### **Stories About Learning**

- Tell me about a time a person from a very different background changed how you think.
- Tell me about a time a group's shared assumptions turned out to be wrong.
- Tell me about a time you learned something important from someone you weren't expecting to learn it from.

### **One Final Question**

What is something people regularly misunderstand about your experience, your community, your work, your family situation, or your life?
