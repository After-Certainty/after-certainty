# **Introduction**

## **What This Book Is About**

<!-- draft: outline only -->

This book starts with a simple problem.

Many people care about diversity. Many organizations say they value it. And yet the same misunderstandings keep happening.

People feel talked over instead of heard. Important warnings are ignored. Systems include people without really changing how decisions get made.

This book asks why.

It argues that diversity isn't just about who shows up. It's about what gets noticed, what gets trusted, and what gets ignored—often without anyone meaning to.

### **The Basic Idea**

We all see the world from where we stand. Our jobs, money, health, safety, and past experiences shape what feels obvious or risky.

When too many people share the same blind spots, mistakes feel normal. Diversity matters because it helps catch those mistakes before they cause harm.
