# Active-agency and voice pass — completion report

## Agency improvements

Principal places where previously hidden actors were named:

- **Introduction:** interpretive habits / audiences / institutions distort
  judgments; people tolerate misunderstanding; groups excuse small harms;
  institutions and audiences continue granting authority; public memory
  and hallway retellings compress sequence.
- **Ch1:** listeners reconstruct; organizations/schools treat communication
  as transfer; people manage/accommodate/endure misunderstanding; media
  systems train audiences.
- **Ch2:** boards/publics/rooms evaluate leadership; groups allow
  authority to persist; the “deserved” couplet revised into ordinary prose
  naming groups.
- **Merkel:** English-language media, coalition partners, critics;
  governments/partners preserve stability and distribute costs;
  supporters/critics hear *Wir schaffen das*; journalists report
  tradeoffs as hesitation.
- **King:** civic memory elevates; King and organizers construct
  campaigns; churches/newspapers/presidents postpone legitimacy;
  resistant institutions deny justification; commemorators narrate denial.
- **Nooyi:** investors, analysts, journalists, PepsiCo’s board as readers;
  boards/markets treat care as dilution; analysts/markets grade on old
  columns.
- **Galileo:** Church authorities censure/suspend/warn (1616); Roman
  Inquisition finds/requires/sentences/bans; observable verbs for
  recognition and status (no interior mind-read).
- **Deng:** Chinese authorities restrict archives and inquiry; Party
  leaders encourage experimentation / reposition ideology; authorities
  suspend rights, close speech, break bodies (ordinary-room return).
- **Mandela:** National Party government releases/unbans; apartheid
  denied the franchise; TRC presents report; negotiators/GNU transfer
  power.
- **Washington:** delegates create the presidency and build the chair;
  Continental officers as coercion risk; federal authorities/settler
  expansion break treaties and seize land; schools/civic rituals convert
  sequence into virtue.
- **Ch10:** HUAC, studios, employers process witnesses; regents press
  faculty; loyalty-security culture displaces understanding; people and
  institutions grant/resist/postpone/reopen.
- **Conclusion:** schools/monuments/civic rituals remember essence;
  audiences and institutions read authority; later memory rebrands
  unfinished repair.

## Useful passives retained

- “a concern was noted” (Introduction) — reproduces evasive meeting
  language; agentlessness is the point.
- “Witnesses were sworn” (Ch10) — procedural theater of hollow inquiry.
- Receiver-focus after agency named: Galileo abjuration / house arrest
  sequence still keeps attention on the person under sentence once the
  Inquisition is the grammatical subject of the prior acts.
- Ordinary-room ledger passives in Nooyi (“written in the margin”) —
  the agentless spreadsheet *is* the chapter’s villain.
- Principal patterns kept in italic/bold form, including Ch1 meaning
  transit, Ch2 reconsideration cost, Mandela moral space, Ch10
  interpretation/allegiance boundary.
- Nooyi “functions as a defense of a measurement regime” — retained from
  source-fidelity pass (avoids unsupported teleology of personal intent).

## Voice improvements

- Abstract nouns → actions: distortion, tolerance, excuse, preservation,
  construction, refusal of openness, invention of presidency, displacement
  of understanding.
- Weak verbs → precise verbs: preserves, postponed, recoded, suppressed,
  repositioned, transferred, elevated, protects.
- Sentence openings: more actor-led openings in intro and leader
  chapter hinges without SVO monotony.
- Institutional responsibility clearer especially for 1616 censure,
  Tiananmen accounting, Convention presidency, HUAC processing.

## Guardrail confirmation

This pass did **not** change:

- title or subtitle;
- chapter titles or part structure;
- leader selection or historical sequence;
- governing principal pattern statements (retained wording);
- ordinary-object motifs (corridor, chair, sentence, spreadsheet,
  telescope, machine, letter, empty chair, attendance sheet);
- conclusion architecture (three movements + corridor return);
- Chapter 10 HUAC boundary case and italic boundary sentence;
- citation IDs/numbering and historical claim substance beyond naming
  supported actors;
- anti-hagiography / anti-cynicism discipline.

Source-fidelity improvements from the polishing pass remain intact
(King campaigns without unsupported mind-reading; Mandela restraint
ledger; Merkel managed uncertainty; Nooyi measurement-regime phrasing;
Galileo observable characterization; Deng official non-reckoning;
Washington civic-myth and slavery material).
