# When Authority Is Misread — Book Rules

## Structure

- 3 parts, front matter per house layout
- Part I: two foundation chapters + part bridge
- Part II: bridge (“Two Ways of Looking”) + seven leader chapters
- Part III: boundary chapter + conclusion
- One file per chapter under `parts/part-N-*/`
- Part bridges live beside chapters (`bridge.md`)

## Chapter movement

Each major chapter generally follows:

1. Ordinary observation
2. Historical widening
3. Philosophical tension
4. Pattern compression
5. Reframed return

Do not use worksheet headings (Signal / Compression / Constraint / Moral
Posture / Effectiveness / Legitimacy Persistence / Closing Reflection /
Synthesis) as the reader-facing structure. Keep those distinctions under
the prose.

## Citation and bibliography

- Use Pandoc footnotes (`[^id]` with chapter-scoped IDs such as `[^c1-source-interpretive-models-of]`).
- Prefer named primary sources and specific secondary works over field gestures.
- Maintain `back-matter/bibliography.md` for works cited by name in footnotes.
- Place a blank line before each `[^id]:` definition block (required for Pandoc/EPUB).

## Thematic arc

- **Part I — Before the Leader Becomes a Story:** ordinary rooms; sentence and chair; meaning and authority before history hardens
- **Part II — Leaders Becoming Legible:** seven cases discovered through distinct human problems
- **Part III — Looking Twice:** when interpretation stops; the second look as ordinary practice
