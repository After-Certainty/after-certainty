# Active-agency audit — *When Authority Is Misread*

Pre-edit audit for the active-agency and voice pass. Counts are approximate
reviews of genuine *be* + past-participle constructions (and close
institutional passives)—not every *is/was/were*. Linking verbs and
intentional middle voice are excluded unless they hide responsibility.

---

## Introduction

**Likely genuine passives worth reviewing:** ~9–12

**Hides actor:** “judgments… are distorted”; “Misunderstanding is
tolerated”; “Small harms are excused”; “Authority persists”; “leaders
were read”; “minutes are written” / “name is attached.”

**Retain:** “a concern was noted” (evasive meeting language); reception
framing where the book’s object is the condition of being misread.

**Agentless patterns:** interpretation / meaning / authority / judgment as
weather-like actors; compressed memory “does the work.”

**Most static:** thesis block from “Leaders are rarely misjudged…” through
“blind spots widen”; “Compressed memory… Looking twice…” stretch.
