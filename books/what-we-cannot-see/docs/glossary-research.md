# What We Cannot See — Glossary (Research)

Term candidates for planning. Manuscript glossary lives in [`back-matter/glossary.md`](../back-matter/glossary.md) when drafting begins.

## Core terms

### Perspective

A situated way of noticing and interpreting—necessarily partial because finite. **Primary ontology** of the book.

### Partial perspective

A perspective that reveals some features and obscures others; not a defective perspective.

### Bias

The selective work through which finite perspectives become possible. One manifestation of selective cognition—not primarily a moral failure, and **not eliminable from individuals**.

**Guardrail:** Distinguish from colloquial "bias = prejudice only." Distinguish from "bias can be removed with training."

### Selective cognition

The necessary filtering through which finite minds engage with the world. Bias is one important manifestation.

### Attention budget

Metaphor for finite selective intake—what we notice costs what we do not.

### Judgment

The responsible use of limited perspectives when deciding under uncertainty. **Level 2:** individual judgment.

**Not:** neutrality, debiasing, or certainty-seeking.

### Integration

Constructing a larger model that explains why multiple partial perspectives each noticed something important. **Level 3:** collective integration.

**Not:** compromise, averaging, false equivalence.

### Process design

Deliberate institutional structures that integrate partial perspectives, create feedback, and reduce unchecked dominance of any one view. **Level 4:** institutional process design.

**Not:** debiasing individuals, achieving objectivity, or unbiased institutions.

### Designed epistemology

Planning term: an organized practice for knowing together under finite perspective (science, peer review, design review, appeals, checklists). See [`process-design.md`](process-design.md). Avoid as jargon until earned in prose.

### Distributed cognition

Cognition spread across people, roles, methods, artifacts, and time—central to process design.

### Error correction

Designed response to partial sight across a system—not absence of bias in any one person.

### Neutrality (myth of)

The idea that a perspective-free view is achievable in an individual mind—rejected in this book.

### Objectivity (careful use)

Not a property of unbiased individuals. May describe **processes** that integrate multiple partial perspectives and correct over time—never without definition.

### Uncertainty

Condition of decision, not failure of character or effort.

## Terms to introduce carefully

| Term | Risk |
|------|------|
| Identity bias | Culture-war fuel |
| Algorithmic bias | Tech-bashing |
| Unconscious bias | Debiasing handbook register |
| Objectivity | Implies unbiased individuals unless defined as process outcome |
| Eliminate bias | Contradicts core thesis |
