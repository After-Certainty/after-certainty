Editorial pass for publication readiness. **Status:** entries with bibliography entries are verified for manuscript use; unresolved items need author confirmation before final promote.

## Citation philosophy (locked for this book)

| Cite | Do not cite |
|------|-------------|
| Named studies (Simons & Chabris, Tversky & Kahneman, etc.) | Composite illustrative scenes |
| Named historical events (Apollo 13, *Challenger*, Snow map) | Ordinary competence hypotheticals |
| Institutional programs (ASRS, morbidity conferences) | Generic “a nurse” / “a manager” scenes |
| Specific methodological claims (RCT, double-blind, replication crisis) | Metaphor-only claims (maps, sketches) |

Footnotes use full **Chicago Notes–Bibliography** first-cite form in chapter prose (`[^id]: Author, *Title* (Place: Publisher, Year).`), matching published house books such as *How Trust Forms*. Bibliography holds the same entries in list form.

## Unresolved citation questions (author review)

1. **Democratic design paragraph (Ch 12)** — Claims about separation of powers, appeal, opposition, and periodic elections are accurate as institutional-design generalizations but are not tied to a single source. **Recommendation:** add Federalist No. 51 and/or Dahl *On Democracy* if you want a footnote; otherwise treat as synthesis (no footnote), consistent with citation philosophy.

2. **Morbidity conference opening scene (Ch 11)** — Composite clinical scene, not one documented case. **Recommendation:** no footnote on the scene; bibliography lists practice context only if desired (Gawande).

3. **Galileo paragraph (Ch 10)** — Historically standard. **Recommendation:** no footnote unless you want Sidereus Nuncius (1610) for precision.

4. **Peer review as “deliberate delay” (Ch 10)** — Accurate institutional description. **Recommendation:** no footnote, or Spier/Burnham on peer review history if you want one anchor.

5. **Air traffic control / fifteenth-century bookkeeping (Ch 5)** — Bookkeeping footnoted; ATC used as modern parallel without separate cite. **Acceptable** as illustrative extension of structure theme.
