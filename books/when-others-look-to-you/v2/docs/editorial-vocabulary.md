# Editorial vocabulary — v2 pointers

## Full checklist (v1)

The enforced plain-language checklist with chapter-specific examples is:

**`../v1/docs/editorial-vocabulary.md`**

That file references **v1 chapter numbers and titles** (e.g. Harm, Legitimacy,
Scale). Use it for **rules**, not for chapter numbering.

---

## Rules that transfer directly to v2

- **Look / look to** — anchor verb for the opening definition and formation;
  use **follow** only where v1’s rule says (role/office authority), not to blur
  “looking vs obeying” in early chapters.
- **Valence** — in forming/renewing-shaped dynamics, avoid blamey or
  submission-loaded words unless the line is about harm or drift; see v1
  **Word valence** in `book-rules.md`.
- **System** — avoid *system* as a default synonym for group; prefer v1’s
  alternates (**group**, **layers**, **how the group operates**).
- **Structural** — keep analyzing structure; vary with plainer glosses when
  density stacks.

---

## What to ignore or translate from v1

- **Chapter-prefixed sections** (“Chapter 2 — Renewal and erosion”, “Chapter 7 —
  Scale”) — v2 chapters are pattern-named; apply the **spirit** of each rule to
  the closest v2 chapter (e.g. erosion language in Part III).
- **Circulation rich / constricted** — still useful **concepts** for feedback
  paths; v2 has no separate “circulation chapter” decision to track.
- **Echo / duplicate-content guardrails** between v1 Part II and Part III — map
  to v2 by **part** (e.g. don’t repeat the same long definitional block in
  multiple bridges) rather than by v1 chapter numbers.
