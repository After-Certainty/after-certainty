# **Bridge — From the Introduction to Forming**

The introduction already walked the full arc—from **Attention Finds a Focus**
through **Leadership Reproduces Itself** and back. **Part I** stays with the
first movement only—**Forming**.

These three chapters follow what happens when others begin to look to you.
They move through **Attention Finds a Focus**, then **Examples Accumulate**,
then **Leadership Coalesces**.

Each chapter builds on the last.
