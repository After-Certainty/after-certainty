# **Bridge — From Eroding to Repeating**

What has formed does not stay in one room. **Part IV**—**Repeating**—has a
single chapter. It names how behavior spreads: **Leadership Reproduces Itself**,
and what is copied becomes what happens next.

That brings the arc back to the beginning. When a new question appears,
**Attention Finds a Focus** again—and what people have learned
determines what they do with it.
