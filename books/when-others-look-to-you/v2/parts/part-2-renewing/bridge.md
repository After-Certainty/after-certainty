# **Bridge — From Forming to Renewing**

Once attention has a steady address, information and adjustment begin to
matter. **Part II**—**Renewing**—follows the loop that keeps a group tied to
what is actually happening—not only to what was decided last time.

**Dissent is Welcomed.** **Feedback Drives Change.** **Leaders Feel the
Consequences.** When these three hold together, the group can still
correct course while the cost of change is small.
