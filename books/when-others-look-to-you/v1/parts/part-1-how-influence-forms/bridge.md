# **Bridge — From Attention to Pattern**

Attention is not yet a habit. It is a moment.

People look to someone else to decide what to do next. The moment can be
small. It can pass without words. If it keeps happening, it leaves a pattern.

It starts with a glance. Over time, it can settle into a habit.

Leadership begins when **Attention Finds a Focus** around someone and direction
takes shape there. Repeated signals turn into expectations, then habits. What
gets rewarded, ignored, or corrected starts teaching everyone how decisions are
made here.

This bridge stays with formation: how attention becomes habit, how belief
firms up, and how early responses to risk and dissent show others what to try
next.

The point is to see what is forming before it sets.
