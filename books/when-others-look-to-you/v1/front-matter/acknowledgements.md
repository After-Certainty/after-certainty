# **Acknowledgements**

This book did not come from a single moment. It came from many small ones,
repeated over time.

**To my wife, Brittany**

Thank you for your constant encouragement and belief. You have not only
supported this work, you have lived it. You’ve taught me these patterns in
ways I could not have learned on my own, and you’ve given me the space to
try, fail, and try again.

**To my children**

You’ve helped me see what it means to feel the consequences of what I
decide—not as an idea on paper, but in how it lands on the people closest to
me. You respond to what I do far more than what I say. The examples add up in
small moments until they outweigh anything I could announce.

**To my parents**

You’ve shown me, over time, that plans can yield when life won’t fit the old
story. There is honesty in that. I learned more from what you practiced
than from anything taught step by step. What mattered to you kept showing up in
me anyway. Those lessons have stayed with me longer than anything that could have
been said directly.

**To Alistair Cockburn**

Thank you for teaching me how to see and speak in patterns. Your influence is
present throughout this book, not just in what is written, but in how it is
shaped.

**To Anderson Shen, Herb Collins, Jeremy Smith, Dee Grey, Daniel Bean, Tyler Chapman, and Justin Kenlon**

Your examples make this real. The ideas in this book are not abstract to me
because I’ve seen them lived out through you.

And to the many small, easy-to-miss moments when people suddenly knew where to
look, something happened, and someone learned from it.

This book is an attempt to notice those moments more clearly.
