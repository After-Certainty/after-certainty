## Global rules

- Do not change stable chapter IDs, routes, or filenames.
- Do not alter required verbatim lines; stop and report if tempted.

- Preserve ordinary-scene → widening → tension → compression → return cadence.
- Footnotes travel with claims; do not orphan citations.
