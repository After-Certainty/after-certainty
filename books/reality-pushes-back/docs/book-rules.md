# Reality Pushes Back — Book Rules

## Purpose

Architectural constraints for **Reality Pushes Back** (*What Happens When Our Models Meet the World*).

This is not a marketing brief. It preserves conceptual cohesion, stylistic consistency, and structural discipline across the manuscript.

## Book Scope and Structure

### Target length

- First-draft band: ~45–55k words for manuscript units (intro, twelve chapters, conclusion), plus pattern-language appendix
- ~3–4k words per chapter (Chapter 1 density is the working model)
- Structure per `index.md`: Introduction + four parts (three chapters each) + conclusion + Appendix A

### Markdown file structure

- One markdown file per chapter (and per bridge where present).
- Filenames must match the chapter slug in `index.md`.
- `index.md` is the hub; all units must be linked from it.
- Back matter files (conclusion, appendix) live under `back-matter/`.

### Front-matter depth

- The introduction establishes the master pattern through material resistance (the bent nail) and encounters the town through routines, not full character introductions.
- Copyright/legal sections are deferred until promotion to `books/`.

## Core invariant (non-negotiable)

Every useful model leaves something out. Reality answers through particulars—grain, wear, exceptions, dissent, lived consequence—that the model did not contain. Responsible attention preserves paths back from abstraction to the people, cases, and materials it represents.

Every chapter must map back to this claim. If a section cannot be tied to it, it does not belong.

Governing question for every chapter: **What part of reality answered back?**

## Thematic arc by part

- **Part I — Perception (*What We Leave Out*):** Compression, circulating understanding, and results that conceal the field that produced them.
- **Part II — Power (*What Attention Creates*):** Authority forming from attention, legitimacy from participation, responsibility from leverage.
- **Part III — Time (*What Decisions Become*):** Structures outliving reasons, speed relocating judgment, commitment leaving room for revision.
- **Part IV — Contact (*How Reality Returns*):** Distance concealing consequence, bad news reaching power, care preserving particulars.

Master pattern across the whole: **Reality Answers Back** (book title dramatizes it as *Reality Pushes Back*).

## Distinguishing structure

Each chapter follows:

**Material → Human → Civilization**

1. Cold open — a witness already doing something ordinary
2. Material resistance — wood, cloth, dough, steel, paper, plaster, or another physical reality answers
3. Human pattern — the same movement in a person, relationship, or local institution
4. Civilizational widening — craft, history, art, science, public life, organizations, technology, economics, or culture
5. Pattern naming — after the reader has felt the shape
6. Town return — the wider understanding changes how the opening scene is read
7. Unclosed ending — the pattern becomes visible without every consequence resolved

The rhythm guides chapters without making them mechanically identical.

## Tone and positioning

### Shared house style (upcoming nonfiction)

- Diagnostic, not prescriptive.
- Observational, not preachy.
- Clear, not academic.
- Serious, not dramatic.
- Calm, deliberate, grounded.

### This book must not be

- Small-town melodrama, conspiracy, spectacular crime, or catastrophe for momentum
- A catalog of abstract pattern definitions without material contact
- A hero's journey in which one person masters the pattern language
- An allegory that reduces people to the pattern of the chapter
- A moral sorting of “good” vs “bad” patterns (corrective patterns can distort; obscuring patterns can be necessary simplifications)

### This book must be

- Ordinary: stakes arise from accumulation, not spectacle
- Continuous: the same town across ~fifteen years; objects carry history
- Standalone-readable: each chapter unlocks without reconstructing earlier strands
- Precise about patterns and incomplete about people
- Material-first: readers handle resistance before institutions widen

### Plain-speak habits

- Short sentences by default; one clear claim per sentence unless parallelism is deliberate.
- Concrete verbs over abstract noun chains.
- Ground observable behavior before naming concepts.
- Avoid manifesto voice, management-blog tone, and culture-war framing.

## Narrative discipline (from Town Bible)

- Patterns emerge before they are named.
- Continuity deepens but never unlocks; recognition rewards but never burdens.
- Objects carry more continuity than exposition.
- Reintroduce through action, not biography.
- One chapter, one remembered prior cause (max).
- The narrator may know what happened, was said, was done, what an object bears, what an institution recorded—not the complete inner reason for hesitation, regret, or silence.
- The ending preserves attention, not resolution. The town may learn; it does not graduate from uncertainty.
- Town name remains deferred (“the town”) until an explicit naming decision.
- Approved cover art is locked as-is; do not modify, regenerate, or alter cover elements.

## Continuity anchors

- Chapter 6 decision quietly echoes in Chapters 8, 10, and 11.
- Nora: teacher → administrator → principal by Chapter 12.
- Daniel: attention before office; public role accumulates.
- Tessa: incomplete file, not secret answer; do not unlock a “true Tessa.”
- Recurring places: bakery, school, community center, hardware store, Town Hall, library, river/bridge.

## Repetition rules

- Reintroducing the invariant in varied form is allowed.
- Repeating the same examples, definitions without deepening, or scene templates without new nuance is not.
- Do not pause to update the reader on every character.

## Citation and glossary

### Citation (when prose is drafted)

- Prefer light citation; this book is narrative-philosophical, not heavily footnoted essay.
- Use Pandoc footnote syntax when a structural pivot needs a source.
- Never fabricate references.

### Glossary

- Pattern catalog lives in Appendix A rather than a reader glossary.
- Corpus glossary remains under `semantic/glossary/`.

## Drafting checks

Before marking a unit draft-complete, ask:

1. Does this reinforce the core invariant?
2. Did material resistance arrive before institutional widening?
3. Is the pattern named only after the reader has felt it?
4. Does the chapter stand alone without multi-strand unlock?
5. Are people larger than the pattern currently visible?
6. Does the ending leave room rather than graduate the town?
