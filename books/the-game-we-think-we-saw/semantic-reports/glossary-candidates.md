## Disambiguation notes

- `operational-finality` vs `legitimacy` / `authority` — finality is operable closure under disagreement, not belief that the call was just.
- `winning-as-excuse` vs `accountability` — winning offered as indemnity rather than one fact among others.
- `athletic-refusal` vs `agency` — book-specific enacted no against event/institution demand.
- `gesture-afterlife` vs `meaning` / `interpretation` — social reproduction of a moment after it leaves the field.
- `scoreboard-remainder` vs `compression` — remainder is what persists after a correct settlement; compression is the reduction that travels.
