# Part IV

## What the Result Becomes

The game ends, but the event keeps moving.

An absence becomes a story. A gesture becomes an image. An explanation becomes a headline. Meaning intended by the athlete enters institutions, arguments, and memories the athlete cannot control.

Public interpretation is not limitless, but neither is it obedient.

This final part follows the sporting moment beyond the field, where the result remains fixed while the story around it continues to change.
