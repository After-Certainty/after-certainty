## Evidence layer definitions

- **documented** — what happened (records, rulebooks, footage, transcripts, investigations)
- **contemporary** — how it was interpreted at the time
- **retrospective** — how meaning changed later
- **author analysis** — pattern claim belonging to this book’s argument

## Quality guide

- **primary** — official record, direct statement, footage, transcript, court/investigative document
- **secondary** — reputable contemporaneous reporting or scholarship summarizing primary materials
- **tertiary** — overview commentary distant from primary materials; insufficient alone for central claims

Chapters 4 and 7 require filled chronology dossiers before drafting. Cultural reputation is not a source.
