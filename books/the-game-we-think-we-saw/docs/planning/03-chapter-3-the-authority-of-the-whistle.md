## Chapter title

Chapter 3 — The Authority of the Whistle

## Governing question

Why should the result count?

## Purpose in the book’s progression

**Conceptual role: Procedural legitimacy.** Move from what happened and what caused it to why an official judgment converts dispute into the next play’s conditions.

## Ordinary opening image

A disputed play stopped by a whistle or review; the ball eventually returns to play while argument continues.

## Central mechanism

Authorized judgment, not omniscience. The whistle creates operational finality that may outrun factual certainty.

## Primary case or case structure

Rotating disputed-call examples across sports rather than one central personality.

## Supporting cases / subjects

- NFL catch rules and replay
- soccer VAR and offside
- baseball strike zones and automated challenges
- basketball contact
- clear-and-obvious standards
- replay speed and camera angle
- human judgment as local adaptation
- consistency versus isolated accuracy

## Key distinctions

- factual accuracy
- procedural legitimacy
- operational finality

**Key insight:** The machine settles the call only after people have settled what the machine should mean.

## Necessary complication or counterexample

Procedure is not automatically legitimate. Official systems can keep producing decisions after inconsistency, opacity, or insulation from correction has damaged trust.

## Research questions

- Which official rule-language passages best show authorized judgment vs omniscience?
- Which rotating examples illustrate accuracy / legitimacy / finality without drowning the chapter?
- How did leagues define “clear and obvious” in practice?

## Evidence risks

Lower than biography-heavy chapters, but rules language and review standards change; date every claim. Do not invent quotes from officials.

## Possible return image

The ruling is announced. The crowd continues disagreeing. The ball returns to play.

## Approved compression lines

- We believed disagreement persisted because we could not see enough. Replay taught us that visibility does not interpret itself.
- Authority earns trust not by eliminating judgment, but by making judgment inhabitable.
- Accuracy makes a judgment defensible. Consistency makes it inhabitable.
