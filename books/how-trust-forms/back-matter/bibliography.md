# **Bibliography**

This book draws primarily on sociology, philosophy, political science, and systems thinking—not the management and leadership trust literature. Works cited in the notes appear below in alphabetical order by author.

- Arendt, Hannah. *The Human Condition*. 2nd ed. Chicago: University of Chicago Press, 1998.

- Baier, Annette C. "Trust and Antitrust." In *Moral Prejudices: Essays on Ethics*. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1994.

- Berger, Peter L., and Thomas Luckmann. *The Social Construction of Reality: A Treatise in the Sociology of Knowledge*. New York: Anchor Books, 1966.

- Dewey, John. *Human Nature and Conduct: An Introduction to Social Psychology*. New York: Henry Holt, 1922.

- Dewey, John. *The Public and Its Problems*. Athens, OH: Ohio University Press, 1954. First published 1927.

- Fricker, Miranda. *Epistemic Injustice: Power and the Ethics of Knowing*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2007.

- Goffman, Erving. *The Presentation of Self in Everyday Life*. Garden City, NY: Doubleday, 1959.

- Hardin, Russell. *Trust and Trustworthiness*. New York: Russell Sage Foundation, 2002.

- Luhmann, Niklas. *Trust and Power: Two Works*. Translated by Howard Davis, John Raffan, and Kathryn Rooney. Chichester, UK: John Wiley & Sons, 1979.

- MacIntyre, Alasdair. *After Virtue: A Study in Moral Theory*. 3rd ed. Notre Dame, IN: University of Notre Dame Press, 2007.

- O'Neill, Onora. *A Question of Trust*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2002.

- Ostrom, Elinor. *Governing the Commons: The Evolution of Institutions for Collective Action*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1990.

- Putnam, Robert D. *Bowling Alone: The Collapse and Revival of American Community*. New York: Simon & Schuster, 2000.

- Scott, James C. *Seeing Like a State: How Certain Schemes to Improve the Human Condition Have Failed*. New Haven, CT: Yale University Press, 1998.

- Weber, Max. *Economy and Society: An Outline of Interpretive Sociology*. Edited by Guenther Roth and Claus Wittich. Berkeley: University of California Press, 1978.
