# **Appendix**

## **Core Principles**

This book explored a simple question:

**How do people become willing to participate with one another under uncertainty?**

The answer emerged through a series of connected observations about trust.

### 1. Trust Begins Before Verification

People cannot verify everything before acting. Every relationship, partnership, team, and community begins with some willingness to accept uncertainty.

### 2. Actions Reveal Beliefs

People learn about one another less through stated intentions than through observed behavior. Actions make beliefs visible.

### 3. Outcomes Teach Trust

Outcomes provide feedback. They reveal whether trust was justified, misplaced, strengthened, or weakened.

### 4. Trust Enables Participation

Trust allows people to cooperate before certainty arrives. It lowers the cost of vulnerability and makes collective action possible.

### 5. Participation Updates Beliefs

Participation creates experiences that observation alone cannot provide. Through participation, people revise their understanding of one another and of the world.

### 6. Trust Scales Through Abstraction

As societies grow, people cannot rely solely on personal relationships. Institutions, professions, standards, and processes allow trust to extend beyond direct experience.

### 7. Trust Converts Attention Into Legitimacy

Repeated trust encourages people to look to the same individuals for coordination and guidance. Over time, trust can become legitimacy.

### 8. Trust Slows Distortion

Trust cannot eliminate misunderstanding, but it encourages clarification, patience, and revision. Distrust accelerates distortion.

### 9. Trust Makes Cooperation Possible

Trust allows people to continue participating despite uncertainty. Cooperation emerges not because uncertainty disappears, but because people become willing to act together while it remains.

## **The Trust Cycle**

These principles form a cycle rather than a sequence.

**Beliefs influence actions.**

**Actions produce outcomes.**

**Outcomes shape trust.**

**Trust enables participation.**

**Participation updates beliefs.**

The cycle then begins again.

Trust does not emerge from certainty. Trust emerges when people continue participating long enough for experience to teach them what certainty never could.
