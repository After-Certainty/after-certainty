# Phase 6 Historical Plan — Chapters 5–10

## Chapter 6 — Writing as Moral Stabilizer

| Field | Plan |
|-------|------|
| **Primary anchor** | Ashoka's rock and pillar edicts (c. 268–232 BCE) |
| **Secondary** | Han provincial registers; Qumran scroll preservation |
| **Structural claim** | Writing stabilizes authoritative wording while multiplying dispute over meaning; moral order travels beyond presence |
| **Hammurabi** | One brief precursor sentence only; not opening or closing object |
| **Remove** | Monastic scriptorium (post-500 CE); Hammurabi opening/closing |
| **Sources** | Thapar, *Asoka and the Decline of the Mauryas*; Salomon, *Indian Epigraphy*; Roth (Hammurabi precursor only); Metzger, *The Canon of the New Testament* (canon) |

## Chapter 5 — The Axial Adaptation

| Field | Plan |
|-------|------|
| **Primary anchor** | Babylonian exile (586 BCE onward) — texts, Sabbath, prayer, dietary law, covenant without temple |
| **Comparison** | Warring States China — teachers, rites, cultivated conduct between competing states |
| **Structural claim** | Portable ethics under fragmentation; contested Axial family resemblance only |
| **Preserve** | Axial Age qualification; exile opening |
| **Remove/qualify** | Schematic section survey; unqualified inward-authority claim; Analects as sole Warring States history |
| **Sources** | Albertz; Levenson; Puett, *To Become a God* or Lewis, *Sanctioned Violence in Early China* |
