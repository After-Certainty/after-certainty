**Causal sequence:** Formation → Legitimization → Persistence. This subsumes the manuscript compression arc (instability → compression → certainty → inheritance → overextension).

Distinct from WOLTY-style behavioral patterns and from After Certainty's ten post-certainty epistemic-moral patterns. This book names deep civilizational motions, not small recurring behaviors.

## What each pattern explains

### 1. Instability Demands Compression

When coordination becomes fragile, societies narrow interpretation, behavior, and acceptable disagreement in order to stabilize action.

Covers: ritual, hierarchy, law, sacred authority, bureaucracy, wartime conformity, institutional process, modern proceduralism.

### 2. What Holds Becomes Moral

Practices first adopted for survival or coordination gradually become treated as moral truths rather than adaptive responses.

Covers: sacralization, inherited norms, institutional legitimacy, procedural morality, moral inheritance generally.

### 3. Tools Survive Their Purpose

Systems that stabilize one era persist into another long after the conditions that produced them have changed.

Covers: canon, bureaucracy, institutional inertia, inherited authority, legitimacy persistence; bridge to later portfolio books.

## Rules (non-negotiable)

1. **Prose first, name second** — The pattern must feel discovered, not announced.
2. **Introduce once, quietly** — Full trio in introduction prose; not academic framing.
3. **Bold inline compression only** — At most **one** bold surfacing per chapter when earned; book-wide target **≤5 isolated bold lines** after tighten pass (not every chapter needs one).
4. **Surfacing tiers (tighten pass)** — **Tier A:** isolated bold OK (Ch 6, conclusion). **Tier B:** embed only, no isolated bold (Ch 1, 2, 4, 7). **Tier C:** embed preferred (Ch 5, 8, 9, 10).
5. **Never** — Pattern callout boxes, "Pattern:" labels, numbered catalogues, diagrams in chapter prose, re-defining all three in every chapter, WOLTY-style density, per-chapter pattern headers visible to readers.
6. **Not in historical anchors** — No bold pattern names inside anchor paragraphs.
7. **Scarcity** — Three patterns total; do not add named patterns without revisiting this doc.

## Format

After a paragraph (or short sequence) that earns the insight:

```markdown
Under threat, societies narrow acceptable behavior before they clarify truth.

**Instability Demands Compression.**
```

Title case in bold surfacing. Sentence case acceptable in introduction list form.
