# Part III Bridge: What These Disciplines Share

Part III identifies the common logic beneath diverse methods. Across
domains and vocabularies, serious systems converge on the same
commitments: treat error as expected, keep action interruptible, protect
dissent that carries operational consequences, and evaluate success by
preserved corrigibility rather than short-term coherence.

The aim is not methodological purity, but transferable architecture.
Part III distills what those disciplines share beneath jargon and
industry.

Part IV begins where this convergence destabilizes: when disciplined
practice is preserved as language but abandoned as governing behavior.
