# Reader Guide: Chapter Contributions

Use this appendix for a one-sentence map of each chapter's contribution to the full argument.

## Chapter 1: When Knowing Is No Longer Enough

This chapter contributes the core diagnosis: knowledge can increase while corrigibility declines.

## Chapter 2: Confidence Outruns Correction

This chapter contributes the central mechanism by which confidence scales faster than correction.

## Chapter 3: The Collapse of Corrective Loops

This chapter contributes the loop-failure model showing how correction erodes before breakdown becomes visible.

## Chapter 4: Constraint as Discipline

This chapter contributes the first durable countermeasure: constraint as architecture for survivable error.

## Chapter 5: Learning Faster Than Prediction

This chapter contributes the temporal discipline: adaptation depends on feedback speed more than forecast precision.

## Chapter 6: Seeing the System You Are In

This chapter contributes the spatial discipline: serious learning must track whole-system effects, not local wins alone.

## Chapter 7: Being Wrong on Purpose

This chapter contributes structured falsification as an institutional practice rather than an individual trait.

## Chapter 8: When Failure Is Not an Option

This chapter contributes the high-reliability pattern: humility must be procedural, not merely cultural.

## Chapter 9: Error as a Design Input

This chapter contributes the unifying Part III claim that serious systems design for recoverability, not infallibility.

## Chapter 10: Action as Probe, Not Solution

This chapter contributes a probe logic of action that privileges learning quality over control narratives.

## Chapter 11: Authority That Preserves Correction

This chapter contributes an authority model where power is judged by its ability to keep correction alive.

## Chapter 12: When Methods Become Myths

This chapter contributes the first decay pattern: effective disciplines harden into doctrine through success and identity.

## Chapter 13: The Seduction of Coherence

This chapter contributes the epistemic failure pattern in which coherence suppresses disconfirming reality.

## Chapter 14: Success as the Most Dangerous Signal

This chapter contributes the governance warning that success can accelerate fragility unless discipline is renewed.

## Chapter 15: What Remains Possible

This chapter contributes a practical post-certainty stance for preserving correction under pressure and asymmetry.

## Chapter 16: Learning as a Way of Being

This chapter contributes the closing orientation: seriousness is sustained as posture, not completed as method.
