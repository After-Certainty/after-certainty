# Part V Bridge: After Certainty

Part V closes with a practical moral question: what remains possible when
certainty can no longer authorize action?

Rather than offering a final framework, these chapters articulate a
durable posture: responsibility without illusion, authority that stays
correctable, and learning treated as an ongoing way of being rather than
a stage before control.

The endpoint is not closure. It is disciplined continuation under
uncertainty, where judgment remains active even when confidence can no
longer be trusted as evidence.
