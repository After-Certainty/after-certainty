# Part II Bridge: Disciplines That Survived Reality

Part II shifts from diagnosis to operating discipline. If certainty cannot
be restored and correction cannot be assumed, what practices still work
under real pressure?

These chapters develop five durable responses: constraint that preserves
reversibility, feedback speed over forecast confidence, system-level
visibility beyond local metrics, structured falsification, and
high-reliability humility.

Taken together, they form a practical posture for acting responsibly
without requiring predictive control. Part II is where the book moves
from naming the problem to demonstrating what actually keeps systems
teachable.
