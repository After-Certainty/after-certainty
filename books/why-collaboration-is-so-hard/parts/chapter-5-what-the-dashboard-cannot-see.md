> **Stub — awaiting author draft.** Beat sheet only. Full planning card: [`../docs/outline.md`](../docs/outline.md).

## Opening image

Nonprofit metrics; board reporting; visibility through numbers.

## Stay in the tension

Clearer metrics calm the room and narrow the work at the same time. Site visits lose to dashboards.

## Widen

Outputs vs care; auditability cheaper than trust; optimization for what is counted; invisible field labor.

## Late pattern

What can be shown on a screen is never the whole collaboration—and treating it as such remakes the work.

## Return

The dashboard green; a field worker’s uncounted hour.

## Do not

Anti-metrics rant. Skip the board’s anxiety and the director’s bind.
