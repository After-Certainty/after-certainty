# Chapter 1 — The Show That Opens Friday

> **Stub — awaiting author draft.** Beat sheet only. Full planning card: [`../docs/outline.md`](../docs/outline.md).

## Opening image

Community theater; tech week; a show that must open Friday.

## Stay in the tension

A script everyone agreed to in spring is not what arrives on opening night. Load limits, line changes, call times—constraint as co-author.

## Widen

Shared authorship; surrender of control; ensembles, labs, mutual-aid kitchens, watershed councils. Work no individual authored in advance.

## Late pattern

Some work becomes possible only after everyone gives up the right for it to remain entirely theirs.

## Return

Curtain; applause for a show none of the makers singly owns.

## Do not

Rush from theater to organizational theory. Open with framework terms (*diffuse ownership*, etc.).
