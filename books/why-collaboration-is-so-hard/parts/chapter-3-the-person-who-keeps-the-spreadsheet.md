Associate professor; “temporary” spreadsheet; invisible labor of organizational memory.

## Stay in the tension

Intellectual debate happens in the room; the next real step depends on work no agenda names. “Sorry to bother you—quick question.”

## Widen

Handoff debt; accreditation and contracts; risk and reputation asymmetry; emotional labor that never appears on the roadmap.

## Late pattern

Every successful collaboration quietly asks someone to remember what everyone else forgot.

## Return

The spreadsheet still called temporary, still open after the meeting adjourns.

## Do not

Lead with a taxonomy of load types. Moralize the person who carries memory; diagnose the structure.
