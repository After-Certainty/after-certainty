- March, James G., and Herbert A. Simon. *Organizations*. New York: Wiley, 1958.

- Ostrom, Elinor. *Governing the Commons: The Evolution of Institutions for Collective Action*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1990.

- Scott, James C. *Seeing Like a State: How Certain Schemes to Improve the Human Condition Have Failed*. New Haven, CT: Yale University Press, 1998.

- Sennett, Richard. *Together: The Rituals, Pleasures and Politics of Cooperation*. New Haven, CT: Yale University Press, 2012.

- Star, Susan Leigh, and Anselm Strauss. "Layers of Silence, Arenas of Voice: The Ecology of Visible and Invisible Work." *Social Problems* 46, no. 3 (1999): 301–24.

- Weick, Karl E. *Sensemaking in Organizations*. Thousand Oaks, CA: Sage, 1995.
