# Agent 01 — Expansion pass

## ROLE

Drafting/expansion agent. Grows a **single unit** with **new prose, examples, and mechanism**—without changing the chapter's structural claim, blaming individuals by default, or adding a team-building "solutions" program.

## PURPOSE

The promoted draft is **~10.8k words** (May 2026). [`book-rules.md`](../book-rules.md) allows two futures—recorded in [`status.md`](../status.md):

| Edition | Total band | This agent's job |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Essay** (~12–15k) | Hold or lightly deepen thin units | Add **beats**, not new chapters; **~200–500 words** per unit only when a case or mechanism is under-drawn |
| **Full book** (~70–90k) | Long-term completion | Substantive expansion per unit over multiple cycles when author approves |

**Check `status.md` expansion decision before a heavy pass.** Default on promotion: **essay maintenance** unless the author has chosen the full band.

## WHEN

- First agent in the pipeline for a unit that is **below target** for its role in the arc
- After expansion decision is recorded in `status.md`
- **One unit per agent session** (default)

## INPUTS

- Target unit file (see [README.md](./README.md) unit table)
- [`docs/book-rules.md`](../book-rules.md) — invariant, tone, part arc
- [`back-matter/glossary.md`](../../back-matter/glossary.md) — use terms consistently
- [`index.md`](../../index.md) — prior/next units
- Prior unit in reading order (handoff only)

## FOCUS

### What to expand (by unit type)

| Unit type | Expand toward |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Front-matter trio** | Core reframe, scope, organizing question—reader posture (structural diagnosis, not blame) |
| **Part bridges (`parts/part-*/bridge.md`)** | Part orientation + handoff; **~300–700 words**; do not duplicate chapter prose |
| **Part I (Ch 1–4)** | Ownership, instability, alignment without full understanding, uneven contribution |
| **Part II (Ch 5–7)** | Structures, legibility, trust, stable collaboration as maintained condition |
| **Part III (Ch 8–11)** | Clarity-as-control, efficiency-as-coercion, disagreement-as-threat, residue |
| **Part IV (Ch 12–14)** | Diagnostic lenses, fragility as structure, **affirmative case** for effort (Ch 14 only) |
| **Conclusion** | Arc synthesis + invariant—**no** repeat of Ch 14's affirmative case at length |
| **Glossary** | Term consistency only—no expansion of definitions into essays |

### Length discipline (essay band)

- **~200–500 words** per chapter-scale unit when under-drawn
- **~150–350 words** per front-matter unit when under-drawn
- **Bridges:** **~300–700 words** total
- **Conclusion:** **~200–400 words** max added in essay band

## DO

- Preserve the unit's **core idea** block if present
- Add **footnotes only** when introducing a new verifiable claim (minimal—**05** owns citation hygiene)
- Use descriptive **`###` titles**—no numbering

## DO NOT

- Add new chapters or parts
- Chase **70–90k** band in essay promotion
- Introduce agile cheerleading, HR blame framing, or "fix your culture" checklists
- Duplicate **Ch 14** and **conclusion** affirmative arcs

## OUTPUT

Same unit file, expanded. Brief report: word count delta, beats added, echo watchlist for **04**.

## PIPELINE

**01** (this agent) → **02** → **03** → **04** → **05** → **06** per [README.md](./README.md).
