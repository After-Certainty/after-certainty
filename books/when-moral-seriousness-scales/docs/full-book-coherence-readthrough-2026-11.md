## Purpose

The merged Chapter 1 and Chapter 6 pilot made the central concepts legible through two constructed meal-program situations, but created a substantial difference in texture and length from the remaining outline-like chapters. This pass revises the intervening and closing chapters as a coherent manuscript rather than copying the pilot's scene-first formula ten times.

The two pilot scenes and their cited footnotes remain as the starting point. The invented people, settings, and illustrative extensions are disclosed in the author's note. This PR does not introduce documentary case studies, new factual source claims, or a publishing decision.

## Each chapter's distinct job

| Unit | Reader should gain | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — Moral Density | Competing costs visible at one door | Smallness is automatically benevolent |
| 2 — Reciprocal Constraint | Why proximity alone does not make others' limits consequential | False equivalence of recipient and volunteer leverage |
| 3 — Contestability | Distinguish being heard from having power to change a decision | Open-door rhetoric without recourse |
| 4 — Distance | See how valid metrics can miss excluded experiences | Treat data or bureaucracy as inherently deceptive |
| 5 — Asymmetry | Follow uneven authority, exposure, and burden of proof | Make executives into villains |
| 6 — Incentive Gradient | Understand how approval can outpace usable objections | Assume delay is always preferable |
| 7 — Acceleration Threshold | See how defensive imitation can normalize pressure | Claim escalation is inevitable or quantify an imaginary threshold |
| 8 — Judgment Under Pressure | Distinguish responsiveness from delay and ritual review | Make judgment synonymous with waiting |
| 9 — Rebuilding Density | Test limits of local discretion, review, and feedback | Turn this into a reform checklist or guarantee |
| 10 — Limited but Real | See what progress means without complete victory | End in a tidy resolution |

The preface preserves the author's personal motivation. The introduction establishes the question without summarizing every answer. Part bridges carry forward a specific unresolved tension. The conclusion revisits the examples and the originating question without asserting a sweeping historical narrative.
