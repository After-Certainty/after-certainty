# When Moral Seriousness Scales — Book Rules

## Structure

- 4 parts, 10 chapters, front matter and back matter per house layout
- One file per chapter under `parts/part-N-*/`
- Part bridges live beside chapters (`bridge.md`)

## Citation and bibliography

- Use Pandoc footnotes (`[^id]` with chapter-scoped IDs such as `[^c1-arendt-responsibility-and-judgment]`).
- Maintain `back-matter/bibliography.md`; every cited work in footnotes must appear there.
- Place a blank line before each `[^id]:` definition block (required for Pandoc/EPUB).

## Thematic arc

- **Part I — The Human-Scale Model:** Moral density, reciprocal constraint, contestability
- **Part II — What Scale Changes:** Distance, asymmetry, incentive gradient
- **Part III — The Pressure Point:** Acceleration threshold, judgment under pressure
- **Part IV — What Remains Possible:** Rebuilding density, limited but real
