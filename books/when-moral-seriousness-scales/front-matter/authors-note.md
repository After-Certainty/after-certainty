# Author’s Note

I am the author of this book.

For transparency in writing it, I made use of an AI language model as a
drafting and revision aid—much as one might use an editor, sounding
board, or analytical tool. The model did not supply beliefs, values, or
conclusions. It helped surface structure, clarify phrasing, and test
coherence.

All moral judgments, interpretations, and final decisions belong to me.

The neighborhood and regional meal-program scenes in Chapters 1 and 6 are
invented illustrations, not reports of real people or institutions. They
are meant to make competing obligations visible, not to serve as
historical or empirical evidence. Citations in those chapters provide
context for the ideas, not verification of the events described.
