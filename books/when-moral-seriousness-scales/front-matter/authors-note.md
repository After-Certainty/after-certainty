# Author’s Note

I am the author of this book.

For transparency in writing it, I made use of an AI language model as a drafting and revision aid—much as one might use an editor, sounding board, or analytical tool. The model helped surface structure, clarify phrasing, and test coherence. The interpretations and final editorial decisions remain my responsibility.

The neighborhood and regional meal programs—and Elena, Mara, Nina, Amir, and the related situations discussed across the chapters—are **invented illustrations**, not reports of identified people or institutions. They are intended to make competing obligations visible, not to establish empirical findings. Variations and possible institutional responses in later chapters are likewise hypothetical.

Sources cited in the chapters inform the conceptual discussion. They do not verify these invented events or demonstrate that any proposed process would produce the outcome described. The book is an exploration of judgment under structural pressure, not a report of a field study.
