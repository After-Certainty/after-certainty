# When Others Become Leaders — Themes and Patterns

Tracking sheet for recurring concepts across chapters. This file is for planning coherence, not for introducing a front-loaded framework in the manuscript.

## Person-by-person pattern matrix

Legend: **Strong / Moderate / Weak / Ambiguous / Contested**

### Jesus (The Table)

- Strong: circulation, multiplication, relational credibility, survival after absence
- Moderate: institution-building (through communities/practices)
- Ambiguous/contested: historical reconstruction precision across traditions

### Gandhi (Salt)

- Strong: example-based legitimacy, means-ends alignment, voluntary sacrifice/limits
- Moderate: circulation through movement practice
- Contested: moral posture toward harm in specific contexts; exclusions and contradictions

### Mandela (Victory)

- Strong: restraint, transferability of legitimacy, post-conflict relational credibility
- Moderate: institution-building
- Contested: effectiveness and persistence of reconciliation outcomes

### Martin Luther King Jr. (The March)

- Strong: circulation, multiplication, moral legitimacy within movement ecology
- Moderate: transferability through institutions/churches/organizers
- Contested: over-attribution to single figure in public memory

### Wangari Maathai (The Tree)

- Strong: multiplication via local acts, institution-building, means-ends coherence
- Moderate: relational credibility across civic domains
- Contested: narrative simplification that understates political opposition

### Václav Havel (The Sign)

- Strong: example-based legitimacy, transferability, truthful witness under cost
- Moderate: restraint and voluntary limits in office transition
- Contested: effectiveness constraints in post-dissident governance

### Jane Addams (The House)

- Strong: institution-building, circulation via place-making, multiplication through participation
- Moderate: relational credibility across class and community boundaries
- Contested: paternalism critiques and limitations of reform frameworks

### Fred Rogers (The Neighborhood)

- Strong: relational credibility, cultural reproduction, dignity practices
- Moderate: transferability through mediated norms
- Ambiguous/contested: policy-scale effectiveness beyond interpersonal-cultural domain

### Taylor Swift (The Bracelet)

- Strong: cultural reproduction, circulation through fan rituals, contemporary example effects
- Moderate: transferability via norms of recognition/generosity
- Ambiguous/contested: durability over time; distinction between influence and leadership

## Cross-chapter checks

- Avoid implying all nine figures are morally equivalent.
- Preserve major differences in context, sacrifice, risk, and consequence.
- Keep contested categories visible; do not smooth them for pattern symmetry.
- Ensure each chapter includes at least one tension that resists easy synthesis.
