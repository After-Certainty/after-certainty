# When Others Become Leaders — Chapter Architecture

Reusable chapter-planning template based on an eight-movement structure. Use as a guide, not a rigid formula.

## Usage principles

- Every chapter should feel native to its subject, context, and stakes.
- Keep movement order flexible when narrative logic requires deviation.
- Preserve discovery; avoid telegraphing the ending pattern too early.

## Optional extension beats (use selectively)

- **Bridge beat:** transition scene between associative domains.
- **Absence beat:** brief thought experiment removing the leader.
- **Reader reorientation beat:** one paragraph clarifying stakes when complexity is high.

## Coherence checks

- Does the chapter keep one guiding question alive?
- Does the central figure enter after inquiry formation?
- Are movement/community actors present, not erased?
- Are criticism and limitations integrated rather than tacked on?
- Does the ending transform the opening image?
