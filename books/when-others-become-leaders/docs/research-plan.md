# When Others Become Leaders — Research Plan

This is a structured roadmap for research. It is not full chapter research and does not claim source completeness.

## Research principles

- Verify memorable anecdotes before using them.
- Prefer primary or institutional sources for specific factual claims.
- Distinguish documented facts from interpretation in notes and prose.
- Avoid invented interiority.
- Quote sparingly; paraphrase accurately with source tracking.
- Track controversies and limitations honestly.
- Avoid reducing movements to one person.
- Maintain a claim-support ledger for each chapter’s major factual assertions.

## Source categories required per chapter

- Primary writings
- Speeches/sermons/public addresses
- Letters or direct correspondence where available
- Interviews/oral histories
- Archival and institutional records
- Reputable biographies
- Historical scholarship
- Critical scholarship
- Evidence of movement/community infrastructure around the central figure
- Sources that complicate heroic narratives

## Initial primary-source anchors by chapter

### Chapter 1 — The Table (Jesus)

- **Likely anchors:** Canonical Gospel meal narratives; Second Temple and Roman-period meal/hospitality scholarship; early community practice evidence.
- **Priority source types:** Primary textual traditions + historical-critical scholarship.
- **Complication track:** Historical method limits; plural interpretations across traditions.

### Chapter 2 — Salt (Gandhi)

- **Likely anchors:** Gandhi writings/speeches/letters around civil disobedience and Salt March; colonial records; contemporary press.
- **Priority source types:** Primary texts + South Asian historical scholarship + critical reassessments.
- **Complication track:** Caste, race, gender critiques; strategic and ethical tensions.

### Chapter 3 — Victory (Mandela)

- **Likely anchors:** Mandela speeches/writings; negotiation-era records; TRC documentation.
- **Priority source types:** Primary public record + reconciliation scholarship.
- **Complication track:** Limits of reconciliation, persistent inequality, contested legacy.

### Chapter 4 — The March (MLK Jr.)

- **Likely anchors:** King speeches/sermons/writings; movement archives (SCLC/SNCC/local organizations); oral histories.
- **Priority source types:** Movement-level primary archives + civil-rights scholarship.
- **Complication track:** Leadership concentration narratives vs distributed organizing realities.

### Chapter 5 — The Tree (Wangari Maathai)

- **Likely anchors:** Maathai writings/speeches/interviews; Green Belt Movement records; Kenyan political and environmental histories.
- **Priority source types:** Primary movement records + environmental/social scholarship.
- **Complication track:** State resistance, implementation challenges, narrative simplification risks.

### Chapter 6 — The Sign (Václav Havel)

- **Likely anchors:** *The Power of the Powerless*; dissident texts and interviews; Czechoslovak historical archives.
- **Priority source types:** Primary dissident writing + Eastern Europe scholarship.
- **Complication track:** Post-transition governance tensions; limits of symbolic politics.

### Chapter 7 — The House (Jane Addams)

- **Likely anchors:** Addams writings; Hull House records/reports; Chicago reform-era archives.
- **Priority source types:** Settlement-house primary documents + urban/social history scholarship.
- **Complication track:** Paternalism critiques, institutional limits, class/culture tensions.

### Chapter 8 — The Neighborhood (Fred Rogers)

- **Likely anchors:** Program archives/transcripts, interviews, production records, public testimony.
- **Priority source types:** Primary media artifacts + media/cultural scholarship.
- **Complication track:** Reach boundaries, institutional context, nostalgia distortion.

### Chapter 9 — The Bracelet (Taylor Swift)

- **Likely anchors:** Public speeches/interviews/posts, documentary materials, reporting on Eras Tour culture and labor/bonus practices, fan-community artifacts.
- **Priority source types:** Primary/public artifacts + reputable cultural scholarship/journalism.
- **Complication track:** Recency bias, parasocial projection, durability uncertainty for living figure.

## Chapter research packet template

For each chapter, maintain:

1. **Claim ledger:** claim -> evidence -> source quality -> open verification status.
2. **Movement map:** key surrounding actors, organizations, infrastructures.
3. **Controversy map:** major criticism clusters and strongest evidence.
4. **Chronology strip:** minimum timeline needed for reader orientation.
