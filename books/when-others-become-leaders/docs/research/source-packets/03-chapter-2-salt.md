## Movement map

- **Indian National Congress** — campaign authorization; provincial committees
- **Ashram core** — Sabarmati marchers (78); local satyagrahis along route
- **Women activists** — Kamaladevi Chattopadhyay, Sarojini Naidu, rural picketers (post-Dandi)
- **Colonial state** — Viceroy Irwin; police at Dharasana; salt laws and monopoly
- **Press** — Webb Miller (UP); global sympathy after Dharasana beatings
- **Dalit politics** — Ambedkar, Depressed Classes; separate electorates debate (1932)
- **Muslim League / communal politics** — parallel and competing nationalist streams

## Controversy map

- **Caste** — Gandhi’s varna views; Poona Pact criticized by Ambedkar as insufficient for Dalit self-representation
- **Race in South Africa** — early petitions for Indian rights within racial hierarchy (Desai & Vahed)
- **Gender** — limited initial roles for women; later mass participation; brahmacharya experiments (Guha)
- **Class and peasants** — whether salt satyagraha addressed rural land poverty or symbolic politics alone
- **Coercion within satyagraha** — social ostracism and moral pressure as non-physical force
- **Partition legacy** — nonviolence did not prevent 1947 violence; Gandhi’s assassination (1948)

## Chronology strip

- **1893–1914** — Gandhi in South Africa; satyagraha experiments
- **1915–1929** — Return to India; Non-Cooperation; constructive program
- **2 Mar 1930** — Letter to Irwin announcing salt breach
- **12 Mar–6 Apr 1930** — Salt March to Dandi
- **May 1930** — Dharasana raid and Miller dispatches
- **Mar 1931** — Gandhi–Irwin Pact
- **Sep 1932** — Poona Pact after fast
- **1947** — Independence and Partition
- **30 Jan 1948** — Gandhi assassinated
