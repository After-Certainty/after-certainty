## Movement map

- **Green Belt Movement (GBM)** — community nurseries, women’s tree-planting groups
- **National Council of Women of Kenya (NCWK)** — institutional host for GBM launch (1977)
- **Rural women’s networks** — firewood, water, soil erosion as entry issues
- **Kenyan environmental and civic allies** — lawyers, clergy, students in later protests
- **State and development actors** — Moi-era land grabs; UN Environment Programme (Maathai later role)
- **International recognition** — Nobel committee; global environmental NGOs

## Controversy map

- **Tree-count claims** — Nobel and GBM cite tens of millions; independent audit of survival rates varies
- **Gender and ethnicity** — movement centered Kikuyu rural women; national politics complicated alliances
- **State repression** — beatings, arrests, media smears under Moi (1980s–90s)
- **Maathai vs Moi** — personal courage admired; later parliamentary and ministerial roles mixed reviews
- **Neoliberal environmentalism** — payment-for-ecosystem framing vs grassroots sovereignty
- **Posthumous GBM governance** — institutional continuity after Maathai’s death (2011)

## Chronology strip

- **1940** — Maathai born, Nyeri district
- **1971** — First East African woman PhD (biology), University of Nairobi
- **1977** — GBM founded through NCWK
- **1989** — Uhuru Park “Freedom Corner” protest vs Times Tower complex
- **1990s** — Repeated arrests; Karura Forest campaign
- **1999** — Karura protest; forest protection win
- **2002** — Elected to parliament
- **2004** — Nobel Peace Prize
- **2011** — Maathai dies; GBM continues
