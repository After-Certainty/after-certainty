## Movement map

- **Won’t You Be My Neighbor? production** — Family Communications / Fred Rogers Productions, WQED Pittsburgh
- **PBS / public broadcasting ecosystem** — CPB, local stations, Senate funding debates (1969)
- **Cast and collaborators** — François Clemmons (Officer Clemmons), David Newell (Mr. McFeely), musicians, puppeteers
- **Child development advisors** — Margaret McFarland and consultants shaping scripts
- **Viewers and parents** — daily half-hour address to children at home; letters and personal replies (limited scale)
- **Posthumous cultural stewards** — documentaries (2018), archives, Fred Rogers Center

## Controversy map

- **Clemmons and Rogers** — foot-washing pool scene symbolic; Clemmons closeted for years; Rogers asked him not to publicize gay life (memoir)
- **Reach limits** — PBS audience skewed; not all children had access; race integration on set vs national inequality
- **Sanitized emotion** — critics of “feel-good” avoidance; Rogers’s anger discipline as theology
- **Nostalgia industry** — post-2018 documentary wave simplifies complex production choices
- **Paradox of mass intimacy** — one man addressing millions; cannot know individual children

## Chronology strip

- **1968** — *Mister Rogers’ Neighborhood* national PBS launch (predecessor 1963 local)
- **May 1969** — Senate testimony defending $20M PBS funding
- **1969** — Pool scene with Clemmons (feet soaking)
- **1981** — Jeff Erlanger visits Neighborhood of Make-Believe (wheelchair)
- **2001** — Final original episodes; program repeats
- **2003** — Rogers dies
- **2018** — *Won’t You Be My Neighbor?* documentary renews public attention
