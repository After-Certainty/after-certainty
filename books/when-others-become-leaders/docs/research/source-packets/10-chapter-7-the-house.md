## Movement map

- **Hull-House co-residents** — Ellen Gates Starr, Julia Lathrop, Florence Kelley, others
- **Neighborhood clubs** — immigrant youth, laborers, women’s groups meeting on site
- **Chicago reform networks** — settlement league, labor legislation campaigns, juvenile court movement
- **University of Chicago ties** — Dewey, sociology, social survey methods
- **Women’s suffrage and peace** — Addams as public intellectual; WILPF (1915)
- **State and municipal actors** — factory inspectors, city health boards influenced by Hull-House data

## Controversy map

- **Charity vs mutual aid** — middle-class residents “uplifting” immigrant neighbors; paternalism critiques
- **Race and exclusion** — settlement movement uneven on Black Chicago; Hull-House focused Near West Side European immigrants
- **Professionalization** — social work becomes credentialed field; grassroots knowledge filtered through experts
- **Pacifism** — Addams opposed WWI; vilified as unpatriotic; later partial rehabilitation
- **Institutional survival** — Hull-House Association lasted until 2012 bankruptcy; physical museum remains

## Chronology strip

- **1889** — Hull-House opens, 800 S. Halsted, Chicago
- **1893** — World’s Columbian Exposition; Hull-House labor and cultural programs expand
- **1895** — *Hull-House Maps and Papers* published (Kelley lead on labor maps)
- **1906** — Child labor and factory inspection reforms (Kelley, Addams advocacy)
- **1910** — Addams publishes *Twenty Years at Hull-House*
- **1915** — Addams leads Women’s Peace Party / WILPF
- **1931** — Nobel Peace Prize (Addams)
- **1935** — Addams dies
- **1960s–2012** — Hull-House organization evolves; closes social services 2012

## Research notes

- Settlement method: **residency + neighbor participation**, not one-way charity — central to leadership-transfer thesis.
- Kelley’s labor maps (h2) evidence data-driven reform emerging from house residents, not Addams alone.
- Jane Addams Hull-House Museum (UIC) for exhibit dates and archival corroboration beyond *Twenty Years*.
