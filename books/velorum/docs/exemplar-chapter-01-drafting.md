## What the exemplar chapter *does* (movable bones)

1. **Place-first braid** — Several paragraphs stack **where / what / who touches what** (harbor, streets, market, slope, monument) before the POV’s private thesis. Geography and trade carry social logic; mood is earned by detail, not motto.
2. **POV anchored in labor** — Rent, shifts, bodies using him; **then** body inventory and climb. Interior “meant for something more” lands **after** work and place have weight.
3. **One charged object** — The pamphlet threads **official story vs felt reality** without a lore lecture; the POV tests it against mud, ditch, ground—not debate.
4. **Set-piece motion** — Ascent uses **sound, wind, gravel, sightline** so the ridge reads on a map-reader skim; refrain (**Heroes. Curse. Never forgotten.**) gets **its own breath**—use this device rarely.
5. **Dialogue as engine** — Long exchange carries conflict, misalignment, and relationship; **Cael** names timber/stone/craft risk; **Riven** names consequence and crowd; theology stays **in mouths or cheap print**, not narrator oracle.
6. **Messenger / noise break** — Bran interrupts pressure, advances plot clock (chores, singer), and **keeps speech off a single axis** before the closing argument.
7. **Weighted pivot** — Before the largest “ask” in dialogue, a **tiny physical pause** (touch, breath, weight on stone) so the line doesn’t read as a casual idea. See **voice-spec → Plain dialogue** and precision edits on the ridge block.
8. **Exit on image + texture** — Chapter ends on **door/boot routine** echoed in body/sensation, **without** explaining the fear of being forgotten.

Not every chapter needs all eight; use the list to ask *what’s missing* when a draft feels thin.

## Drafting habits (distilled from the exemplar)

### Openings and flow

- **Vary entry points** in tight dialogue or action clusters: lead with **motion, sound, wind, trail, throat, gaze** sometimes—not every beat with `Riven…` / `Cael…` / `He…` at the sentence front.
- **Braid narration** in sustained paragraphs; reserve **single-sentence paragraphs** for true punches (refrain, interrupt, one-word answer, gross-out beat). See **voice-spec → Paragraph shape**.

### Speech

- **Spoken > poster-shaped.** If a line could live on a bookmark, roughen it: **contraction, hesitation, asymmetry**, or split into how a tired person actually says it—still **short**. Chapter 1’s pamphlet commerce lines are the calibration for “lived-in” vs thesis.
- **Stakes before ornament** in argument; **one** image per spoken beat unless someone is **performing** (crowd, ritual, magistrate).

### Lore and belief

- **Attribute claims:** pamphlet, old tale, “people say,” square song—not neutral narrator proving canon (**voice-spec → Clear agency**, **Pass A3** in chapter-edit-pass).

### Big lines (asks, confessions, proposals)

- **Land weight with 1–2 lines max** of silence, breath, or small body–environment contact **before** the line that turns the scene—**not** new exposition. Same standard as a **precision pass**, not a second speech.

### Closings

- **Last beat:** concrete image already in the chapter’s motif bank (boots, door, latch, grit, trail). Add **one** sensory or physical tail if the closing line risks reading as epigraph—**no** explanation of meaning.

## Anti-patterns the exemplar avoids

- **Staccato narration** where one clause could sit in the same paragraph as the next.  
- **Aphoristic dialogue** that sounds written for Twitter, not pressure.  
- **Floating “the town teaches”** without bodies and coins on the page.  
- **Same sentence opener** three times in a row in close argument (**Pass H** + opening-variation habit).  
- **Em-dash wallpaper** — Chapter 1 after punctuation discipline is the density bar (**voice-spec → Em dashes**).
- **Expansion gobbledygook** — invented infrastructure (*fish stairs*), market-label stacks (*bond singers*), quirky inventory used as generic "concrete" filler. Prefer **human friction** (exhausted workers, wrong orders, missing inventory, bad info, mistakes). See **[agents/revision/08-reality-agent.md](./agents/revision/08-reality-agent.md)** after expansion passes.
