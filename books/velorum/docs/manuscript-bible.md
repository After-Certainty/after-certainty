- **Age:** mid-20s (implied adult laborer)
- **Work:** docks, Mara's inn (sweep, haul, odd jobs)
- **Physical:** broad laborer's build; herring/tar smell recurring; worn pack strap; heel blister from ch. 3; thumb skinned on scree ch. 3

### Cael
- **Age:** mid-20s
- **Work:** maintenance/builder instincts; sweeps Mara's yard
- **Physical:** tall, dark coat, short hair; bad knee (mentioned ch. 2); sore knuckle from monument
- **Voice:** literal answers; planner; wrong sometimes (path read, oatcake stall, print count, seep timing)
- **Cognitive style:** systems, load, timing — mule scene is the template
- **Core want (Acts I–III):** prove the curse can *mean* something — purpose, not freedom (Riven) or protection (Lyra)
- **Arc filter:** learning that meaning cannot justify suffering; Halverin tempts meaning, not greed
- **Act III flaw (on-page):** believes carrying more responsibility prevents the next tragedy — **wrong** (ideological break ch. 19)
- **Act III blind spot:** assumes Riven will understand once the load proves itself (road habit; fails in square)
- **Humanity beats:** secret relief when work matters; small pleasure in being asked-for; grief when public proof collapses (ch. 17–19)

### Mara
- **Role:** Greyhaven innkeeper; board/scrub names; charges extra for everything
- **History:** Kept Riven's mother's kettle three winters; mother died in fever years ago
- **Consistency:** tired > angry; inventory mindset; flour on sleeve; no coffee

## Curse / bond rules (Act I — post-oath)

**Full authority:** [voice-spec.md → Shared burden guardrails](./voice-spec.md#shared-burden-guardrails-curse-language-default)

### What the bond IS (on-page Act I)
- **Oath at Ashring** (*I'm in* / *We hold*) triggers Velorum attention — not fully explained in prose
- **Boundary violation**, not telepathy: private thought/feeling can cross uninvited
- **Physical echo:** balance, pain, fatigue, motion can arrive early or in wrong body
- **Emotional bleed:** anger, fear, relief, disappointment cross before words

### Act I progression (ch. 5 → 6)
| Stage | Ch. | Symptoms |
|-------|-----|----------|
| Pre-oath pressure | 5 | Warm stone, late sound, ring drop, presence—**no bond bleed** until after hum |
| Oath | 5 | Rope test (nothing); *I'm in* ×2; hum → **shared inhale = instant**; wrist lock, count overlap, can't step back |
| Immediate after | 6 rim | Thought words (*nail*), memory flashes (dock, hinge) |
| Descent | 6 trail | Sync steps, shared falls, cabbage/rope tests |
| Camp | 6 | Unspoken *We hold* / *Yeah*; stew banter; fear of planning bleed |

### What bond is NOT (Act I)
- No clean mind-reading at will
- No voice of Velorum speaking in sentences
- No full body swap
- No cure/explanation delivered in dialogue

### Verification discipline (one proof each)
- **Shock:** nail thought (ch. 6)
- **Absurd test:** cabbage (ch. 6)
- **Cost:** relief→anger; can't stop planning miles (ch. 6 fire)
