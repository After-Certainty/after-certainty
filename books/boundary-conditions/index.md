# Boundary Conditions

## A Literary Organizational Fiction Novel

When a delegated-account flaw becomes Sev One, **Nate Mercer** is the person everyone routes toward—at work and, quietly, at home. These chapters trace discovery through coordination and realization, then boundaries and a return that tests whether the organization can learn without eating its most reliable person again.
