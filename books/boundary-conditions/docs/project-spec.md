# CORE PREMISE

A serious **authorization vulnerability** in delegated identity/account flows forces a large enterprise technology company into a Sev One incident. Different teams frame the same failure as synchronization, staging data, customer experience, compliance, or reputation—while the underlying issue is **authorization boundaries**, **auditable consent**, and **institutional accountability**.

**Nate Mercer**, senior staff/principal engineer, is calm, systems-oriented, and widely trusted. He naturally becomes **connective tissue** between Security, Product, Engineering, and leadership. Organizations—and his family—**route uncertainty toward the person most willing to absorb it**.

Nate’s arc is **not cynicism or disengagement**. It is **boundaries**, **stewardship**, and **sustainable leadership**—helping without becoming invisible infrastructure.

---

# THEMATIC CENTER

- Invisible coordination work and informal leadership
- Responsibility without authority
- Systems that depend on heroics instead of sustainable structures
- Institutional fear around documentation and accountability
- Distributed systems as metaphor for human organizations
- **Title — *Boundary Conditions*:** technical authorization edges; organizational “not our system”; emotional limits; familial caregiving routed to the reliable sibling

**Core realization:**

Systems redistribute responsibility toward the people most willing to absorb uncertainty.

---

# SETTING

A large modern enterprise technology company (no direct real-company modeling). Fragmented distributed systems, delegated identity flows, security/compliance pressure, architectural silos, competing incentives across Security, Product, Infrastructure, and Engineering.

# POV RULES

- **Default POV:** close third on **Nate Mercer**, one POV per chapter unless planning docs explicitly assign another host.
- Anchor Nate early (name, body, perception) within the first paragraph when practical.
- Technical explanation stays **scene-grounded** (screens, calls, whiteboards)—no lecture chapters.

---

# GENRE ANTI-PATTERNS

- Melodrama, cartoon villains, simplistic corporate satire
- Nate as flawless savior; endless heroics without cost
- Jargon stacks without human stakes
- Theme lectures detached from behavior and consequence
- “AI saves/dooms everything” as substitute for organizational choice

---

# ENDING REQUIREMENTS

- Exploit or accountability crisis **returns**; organization reaches for Nate again
- Nate **helps differently**: explain, advise, review, transfer knowledge—**does not** re-become total coordinating infrastructure
- Emotional resolution favors **distributed ownership** and **sustainable help**, not burnout martyrdom or cold withdrawal
