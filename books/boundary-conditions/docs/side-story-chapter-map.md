## Threading rhythm (workbook guidance)

Alternate **technical/system pressure** and **intimate human scenes** within each act—roughly one family or marriage beat per act in Acts II–IV, not only Ch 10–11.

Suggested pattern per act (5 chapters):

1. Incident/organizational pressure  
2. Organizational pressure  
3. Human scene (family or Hadley)  
4. Organizational convergence or conflict  
5. Realization or handoff beat  

Act I bent this rule because Nate was traveling (family scenes *are* the human scenes).
