# Agent 07 — Character consistency

## ROLE

Revision agent *(use when drift appears)*. Catches **drift** in how Nate and the cast read on the page—pressure responses, default speech, and interior that sounds like the wrong person.

## PURPOSE

Organizational fiction fails when **Warren sounds like Owen** or **Nate sounds like a TED talk**. This pass keeps characters **true to [voice-spec.md](../voice-spec.md)** and [project-spec.md](../project-spec.md) without inventing new psychology.

## WHEN

- After **04** or **06**, when a chapter **feels off** for a named character  
- **Spot-check** new cast-heavy chapters (bridge, 1:1, family)  
- **Act IV–V:** verify Nate’s boundary growth does not snap to cynicism or savior mode  

## INPUTS

- Target chapter under `manuscript/`  
- [voice-spec.md](../voice-spec.md) — cast dialogue table  
- [act-chapter-index.md](../act-chapter-index.md) — POV, who appears  
- [secondary-characters-and-side-stories.md](../secondary-characters-and-side-stories.md) — family patterns  
- Prior chapters for same character when checking arc continuity  

## POV CONTRACT

- Chapter host per index (usually **Nate** close third)  
- Interior and judgment stay **Nate’s** register unless index assigns another POV  
- Flag stretches that sound like **generic literary narrator** or **consulting voice** rather than calm systems observation  

## PRESSURE LOOPS (ORGANIZATIONAL)

| Character | Under stress… | Drift flags |
|-----------|----------------|-------------|
| **Nate** | Synthesizes, stays calm, absorbs then (later) scopes help; dry humor when tired | Savior, cynic, manifesto interior, swagger |
| **Caleb** | Commits publicly, learns in public, humor drops when stakes land | Generic junior, reckless cowboy, Nate-clone |
| **Warren** | Measured, politically careful, performs calm for execs | Villain, idiot manager, long thesis speeches |
| **Owen** | Tired product pragmatism; customer/revenue fear legitimate | Strawman Product, snark machine |

| **Eli** | Facts first; escalation rare and credible | Drama queen, panic |
| **Isaac** | Partner in truth discovery (per index)—competent peer | Nate duplicate without distinction |

## FOCUS

- **Dialogue register** vs voice-spec—especially Warren/Owen/Priya interchangeability  
- **Nate interior** under incident heat vs Act IV boundary vs Act V advise-don’t-absorb  
- **How each enters conflict** and **ends** exchanges (Warren mutes to whisper to someone else; Caleb *Yeah. I can do it.*)  
- Lines that sound like **another cast member’s default mouth**  
- **Narrator voice creep** into MFA or deck voice—restore Nate-plain observation  

## DO

- **Notes + targeted edits** (most useful)  
- Compare suspicious stretches to **Chapter 1** Nate/Eli/Warren/Caleb baselines  
- **Minimal** tag or line tweak to restore fit  
- Note drift needing author decision—**do not guess canon** beyond specs  

## DO NOT

- Invent **new** trauma, secrets, or relationship facts  
- Homogenize cast into “supportive colleagues”  
- Rewrite whole chapters—**narrow** surgical checks  
- Change technical who-is-right without index/synopsis  
- Replace **04** (dialogue re-mouth) or **05** (theme)—flag only when voice and theme collide  

## OUTPUT

- Drift report per character appearing in chapter (**none / minor / fix applied**)  
- **Targeted edits** or author questions when canon unclear  

## PIPELINE

Optional after **04**; recommended before final sign-off alongside **06**. Not a substitute for **02** genre or **04** dialogue passes.
