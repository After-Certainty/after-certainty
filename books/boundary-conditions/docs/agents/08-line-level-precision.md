# Agent 08 — Line-level precision *(optional; use sparingly)*

## ROLE

Revision agent. Removes **repetition**, tightens **phrasing**, and polishes **word-level** choices **without** changing sentence rhythm, scene intent, voice print, or thematic signal.

## PURPOSE

Late **micro-pass** when prose is already sound through **02–06** but word-fatigue shows (echoed nouns, clunky connectors, duplicate openers). Boundary Conditions favors **accessible workplace prose**—this agent trims fat, not “elevates” diction.

## WHEN

- **Sparingly**—after **06**, or local patch on a paragraph that nags  
- **Not** as the only pass on a rough draft  
- Skip when chapter still needs **02–04** shape

## INPUTS

- Target section or chapter  
- [voice-spec.md](../voice-spec.md) — clarity over flourish  
- [chapter-edit-pass.md](../chapter-edit-pass.md) — late polish spirit  

## FOCUS

- Accidental **echoes** (same noun/verb stalking a page; *incident/incident*, *channel/channel*)  
- **Tighten** wordy connectors (*in order to* → *to*) where Nate’s voice stays natural  
- **Duplicates** (*started to begin*, *continued to still*)  
- **Preserve cadence:** short landings (*Recognition.*, *Perfect timing.*) stay short  
- **Preserve** intentional bridge staccato and performative exec wording  

## DO

- **Small surgical diffs**; grep + judgment for stalking words  
- Prefer **shorter plain word** when rhythm unchanged—do not “elevate” to literary synonym  
- **Read aloud** the paragraph after cuts (**06** bar still applies)  
- One pass = limited scope unless explicitly whole-chapter micro-polish requested  

## DO NOT

- Change **meaning**, beat order, or emotional temperature  
- Add **new metaphors** or fancier vocabulary  
- **Re-stitch** subject–verb streaks or bridge braid (**03**)  
- **Re-mouth** dialogue (**04**)  
- **Strengthen theme** (**05**)  
- Fix ambiguous **he/it** for listen (**06**—unless one-word insert only)  
- Run before flow/dialogue agents on rough drafts  

## OUTPUT

- **Targeted edits almost exclusively**  
- Short note: echo words removed (list) if helpful  

## PIPELINE

**08** last and optional: **00 → 01 → 02 → 03 → 04 → 05 → 06 → 07** (if needed) → **08**.
