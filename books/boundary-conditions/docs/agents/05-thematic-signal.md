# Agent 05 — Thematic signal

## ROLE

Revision agent. Strengthens **Boundary Conditions** themes through **behavior, routing, and recurrence**—readers feel pressure; the narrator does not deliver a thesis.

## PURPOSE

After dialogue is stable (**04**), reinforce what the book is *about* without naming it. Themes live in **who gets the DM, who runs point, who signs, who goes silent**—not in labels like *invisible labor* or *boundary conditions* as essay vocabulary ([project-spec.md](../project-spec.md), [voice-spec.md](../voice-spec.md)).

## WHEN

- After **04** on chapters where theme should land (most Acts II–V; light touch in Act I discovery)  
- **Re-run** when a pass added blunt theme lines or abstract narrator commentary  
- **Act IV recalibration:** quieter **signal**—boundaries shown through **refusal, transfer, selective help**—not repeated “realization” speeches

## INPUTS

- Target chapter under `manuscript/`  
- [project-spec.md](../project-spec.md) — thematic center, ending requirements  
- [synopsis.md](../synopsis.md) — act spine  
- [act-chapter-index.md](../act-chapter-index.md) — chapter job  
- [side-story-chapter-map.md](../side-story-chapter-map.md) — family parallel when present  
- [recalibration.md](../recalibration.md) — Act IV tone  

## THEME MAP (SHOW, DON’T LABEL)

| Theme | Dramatize through… | Avoid naming in narration |
|--------|---------------------|---------------------------|
| **Reliable person / routing uncertainty** | Nate copied on threads, “can you sanity check,” Caleb handed point, Rachel scheduling for Mark | *reliable person*, *invisible labor* as slogan |

**Core realization (earn once per act, not every scene):**  
systems and families **route ambiguity** toward whoever absorbs it most willingly.

## FOCUS

### Method

- Reinforce through **patterns already in play**: bridges, incident channels, private VP thread, parking garage normal life, airport rain, father's paperwork, delegated-flow diagram  
- **Echo and vary** concrete recurrence (participant count jumping, template fields, clock in corner of monitor)—do not spam the same beat twice in one paragraph  
- Prefer **workplace objects and actions** (laptop rotated, mute button, timeline bot) over abstract nouns (*stewardship*, *alignment*, *culture*) in Nate’s narration  
- Abstract words in **dialogue** only when a character would perform them (exec, comms, Legal)

### Trim bluntness

- Lines that **name** the theme when cutting would leave the scene smarter  
- Nate interior that sounds like **consulting deck** or **leadership blog**—replace with observation (*recognition*, *quiet pressure*) already in voice-spec  
- **On-the-nose** dialogue already flagged in **04**—this pass catches **narration** theme labels

### Act-aware touch

| Act | Thematic emphasis |
|-----|-------------------|
| **I** | Fragmentation, competing frames, Nate pulled in despite leaving |
| **II** | Connective tissue; mitigation sprawl; family routes work to Nate |
| **III** | Authorization truth; institutional self-protection; reliable-person insight **earned** |
| **IV** | Boundaries at work/home; transfer; quieter stewardship ([recalibration.md](../recalibration.md)) |
| **V** | Pressure returns; Nate helps **differently**; distributed ownership |

## DO

- **Small, surgical** motif touches and trims  
- Strengthen **parallel** (org routing ↔ family routing) only when chapter map calls for it  
- Align with ending requirement: sustainable help, not martyrdom or withdrawal  

## DO NOT

- **Explain** themes in new narration or dialogue  
- Add exposition, lore, or “what this chapter means” beats  
- Turn subtext into text (*what this really means is…*, *the boundary conditions of leadership*)  
- Replace concrete workplace detail with **ornamental metaphor**  
- Invent new theme strands not in project-spec/synopsis  
- Use **02**’s job (scene pull, more dialogue)—this pass only **sharpens signal**  

## OUTPUT

- **Targeted edits** (typical): motif echo, blunt-line trim, one image swap  
- Brief report: which theme threads **strengthened** / **over-labeled** (trim list)  
- **Full chapter** only when prompt requests chapter-wide thematic polish
