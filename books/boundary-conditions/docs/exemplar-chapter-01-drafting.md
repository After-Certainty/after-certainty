## Scene bones (Chapter 1)

1. **Hook:** clock + ordinary departure plan interrupted by DM  
2. **Relationship:** Eli as credible escalator; Nate’s trust earned over twelve years  
3. **Technical stake:** delegated flows, repro twice, sequence walkthrough—plain language  
4. **Organizational turn:** Sev One, Warren, thirty-eight participants, competing mitigations  
5. **Delegation:** Caleb accepts point with commitment not bravado  
6. **Nate’s insight:** mitigations ≠ underlying authorization problem  
7. **Coda:** airport rain; channels multiply; leadership thread vs engineering thread  

## Habits to carry forward (organizational-fiction engagement)

- Open with **concrete time and place** before abstract theme  
- Use **one scene per major beat**; avoid outline bullets on the page  
- **Dialogue and meetings** carry conflict; narrator summarizes only when skipping time  
- Let **competence** show through calm action (Nate skim report twice, not “brilliant Nate”)  
- **Warren** pressured, not villainous; **Eli** credible; **Caleb** commitment without swagger  
- End chapter on **pull**—recognition, pressure, or open question (*same incident, different realities*)  

## Do not copy

- Outline-style bullet lists from workbook tabs  
- Explaining the title’s four meanings in narration  
- Sermons on “systems thinking” without a body in a chair
