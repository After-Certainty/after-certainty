# Boundary Conditions — Expansion Plan

**Purpose:** Grow the manuscript from **~31k** words (current chapter prose) toward the **80–100k** band in [project-spec.md](./project-spec.md) without changing plot spine or act structure.

**Anchor density:** [chapter-01-the-message.md](../manuscript/act-1-discovery/chapter-01-the-message.md) (engagement) and [chapter-02-incident-declared.md](../manuscript/act-1-discovery/chapter-02-incident-declared.md) (room-scale scenes). Acts IV–V stay **quieter** per [recalibration.md](./recalibration.md)—expand with **texture**, not Act I noise.

---

## Principles

1. **Add scenes, not summaries** — bridges, 1:1s, kitchen table, whiteboard rooms; cut list-y time jumps.
2. **One new pressure per expansion block** — don’t stack three plot events; deepen one beat.
3. **Protect Act IV tone** — shorter sentences OK; still scene-based (Daniel walk, office hours, email boundary).
4. **No new chapters** unless you later choose a split (e.g. Ch 9 Owen dinner); keep 25-chapter spine for now.
5. **After each chapter expansion:** run agents **01–06** (08 sparingly).
6. **Trim repetition** from [manuscript read](./status.md) — weather report, load-bearing wall, goggles sticker (max once per act after expansion).
