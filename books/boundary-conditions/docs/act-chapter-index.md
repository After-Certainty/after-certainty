## ACT II — COORDINATION (Chapters 6–11)

**Goal:** Nate as connective tissue; mitigation sprawl; family parallel emerges.

**POV:** Nate Mercer

### Chapter 7 — Connective Tissue

**POV:** Nate Mercer

**POV:** Nate Mercer  
**Beats:** Human scene—Daniel’s cognitive decline visible (passwords, paperwork, repetition, deflection); Nate begins appointment/logistics coordination; mirror of “reliable person” begins at home. **Cast:** Daniel, Hadley (glimpse optional). **Side story 2 (home seed).**

## ACT III — REALIZATION (Chapters 12–16)

**Goal:** Authorization truth; institutional self-protection; reliable-person insight; outburst makes exhaustion visible.

**Side stories:** 4 (peak), 7 (outburst), 2 (naming), 3 (Caleb shift).

### Chapter 12 — Authorization Truth

**POV:** Nate Mercer  
**Beats:** Nate and **Isaac Chen** with **Priya** converge: core issue is delegated authorization, auditable consent, write scope—not sync-only; engineering channel begins to name problem. **Cast:** Priya, Isaac, Eli. **Side story 1** (truth lands on boundary language).

### Chapter 13 — Paper Trail Fear

**POV:** Nate Mercer  
**Beats:** **Pablo Ortega**’s AI-assisted security analysis circulates; leadership anxiety shifts to documentation, discoverability, regulatory exposure; fear of durable narrative—not cartoon cover-up. **Cast:** Pablo, Warren, Legal/Security. **Side story 4 (peak).**

### Chapter 14 — Institutional Self-Protection

**POV:** Nate Mercer  
**Beats:** Long-term design meetings go circular; **Side story 7—Nate’s outburst** (earned, not abusive): local boundaries vs system-level expectations; room goes quiet; Caleb sees Nate differently; leadership uneasy; technical truth vs org self-protection in same week. **Cast:** Priya, Owen, Warren, Caleb, cross-team leads. **Side stories:** 1, 4, 7.

### Chapter 15 — Exhaustion at Home

**POV:** Nate Mercer  
**Beats:** After outburst—embarrassment and sympathy; family logistics peak; siblings assume Nate will keep handling everything; **Hadley** names unsustainable responsibility. **Cast:** Hadley, Daniel, Rachel, Mark. **Side story 2 (home exhaustion).**

### Chapter 16 — The Reliable Person

**POV:** Nate Mercer  
**Beats:** Nate articulates insight: systems route uncertainty to the most reliable person; mistakes dependence for leadership; **Caleb** begins to see cost of admiration (“I thought leadership meant carrying everything” / Nate’s reply). **Cast:** Caleb, Hadley (echo). **Side stories:** 2 (naming), 3.

**Goal:** Transfer, recalibration, sustainable limits. **Tone:** quieter than Acts I–III — see **[recalibration.md](./recalibration.md)**.
