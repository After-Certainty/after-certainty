# Part IV Bridge: When Disciplines Fail

Part IV takes the handoff from Part III by examining how serious
practices decay after convergence has been achieved.

Disciplines fail not only through neglect but through success: methods
harden into identity, coherence displaces inquiry, and positive outcomes
are misread as proof that correction is no longer needed.

These chapters map the transition from adaptive discipline to
institutional myth, showing how systems can lose humility while retaining
the language of learning.
