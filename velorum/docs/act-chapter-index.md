# ACT & CHAPTER OUTLINE — VELORUM

*~30 chapters, scalable to ~90k words*

## ACT I — THE CHOICE (Chapters 1–6)
**Goal:** Establish characters, Greyhaven, the legend, and the curse.

### Chapter 1 — The Monument
**POV:** Riven  
Riven studies the crumbling statue outside Greyhaven. We see his fear of being forgotten and his desire for meaning. Cael frames the legend as purpose.

### Chapter 2 — The Decision
**POV:** Cael  
Cael commits to seeking Velorum. He rationalizes the choice as necessary. Riven hesitates but agrees.

### Chapter 3 — Leaving Greyhaven
**POV:** Riven  
They leave town. Establish their dynamic: Cael grounded, Riven searching. First hints of tension in why they’re doing this.

### Chapter 4 — The Highlands
**POV:** Cael  
Harsh terrain. Increasing pressure. Velorum’s presence begins to be felt as environmental distortion.

### Chapter 5 — Velorum
**POV:** Riven  
They encounter Velorum (presence, not form). Riven feels unease. Cael accepts immediately.

### Chapter 6 — The Curse
**POV:** Cael  
The bond begins. They hear each other’s thoughts. Initial confusion -> rapid synchronization.

## ACT II — THE GIFT (Chapters 7–13)
**Goal:** Show power, introduce Halverin + Lyra, seed instability.

### Chapter 7 — Perfect Movement
**POV:** Riven  
They fight bandits with flawless coordination. Riven feels exhilaration—but also exposure.

### Chapter 8 — The Road to Kestrel Gate
**POV:** Cael  
Their reputation grows. Cael begins to believe this is right.

### Chapter 9 — The Magistrate
**POV:** Cael  
Introduction of Halverin. He defines the “path.” Cael aligns with him immediately.

### Chapter 10 — Lyra
**POV:** Riven  
Riven meets Lyra. First moment of something separate. Cael feels it instantly.

### Chapter 11 — No Privacy
**POV:** Riven  
Riven tries to hold onto something private. Fails. Emotional discomfort grows.

### Chapter 12 — The Attack on Kestrel Gate
**POV:** Cael  
Major action scene. Their coordination falters. They still win—but too late.

### Chapter 13 — Aftermath
**POV:** Riven  
Riven feels guilt twice (his + Cael’s). First real sense that something is wrong.

## ACT III — THE BREAK (Chapters 14–20)
**Goal:** Collapse Halverin, lose Lyra, force truth into the open.

### Chapter 14 — The Explanation
**POV:** Cael  
Halverin reframes the failure. Cael accepts it. Riven doesn’t fully.

### Chapter 15 — Trying to Fix It
**POV:** Cael  
Halverin prepares the public demonstration. Cael commits to regaining control.

### Chapter 16 — Lyra’s Warning
**POV:** Riven  
Lyra tells Riven the truth: this is loss, not refinement. He resists accepting it.

### Chapter 17 — The Demonstration
**POV:** Cael  
Public square scene begins. Pressure builds.

### Chapter 18 — Collapse in Public
**POV:** Riven  
The bond destabilizes violently. Reality distorts. Halverin fails visibly.

### Chapter 19 — The Silence After
**POV:** Cael  
Halverin loses authority. Cael loses structure. He doesn’t know what to believe.

### Chapter 20 — Lyra Leaves
**POV:** Riven  
Riven tries to connect with Lyra but can’t. She leaves. This is a quiet emotional breaking point.

## ACT IV — THE TRUTH (Chapters 21–26)
**Goal:** Close Halverin’s thread, seek Velorum, escalate curse, force final understanding.

### Chapter 21 — No Answers Left
**POV:** Cael  
**Halverin exit:** final scene with the magistrate—power broken, narrative exhausted, last ugly exchange or hollow apology—so his arc ends here (~chapter 21), before the Seeker plot takes over. Then: they realize no civic authority remains worth obeying; decision to find Velorum.

### Chapter 22 — The Seeker
**POV:** Riven  
They meet the Seeker. Minimal dialogue. The Seeker begins walking.

### Chapter 23 — Following
**POV:** Cael  
Cael sees direction again. Riven sees inevitability.

### Chapter 24 — Fractured Lands
**POV:** Riven  
Environment mirrors their condition. Riven tries to hold onto individuality—fails.

### Chapter 25 — Losing the Line
**POV:** Riven  
Riven can no longer clearly distinguish his thoughts. Identity erosion accelerates.

### Chapter 26 — Velorum Again
**POV:** Cael  
They reach Velorum. Truth revealed: no cure, only transformation into legend.

## ACT V — THE LEGEND (Chapters 27–30)
**Goal:** Final choice, sacrifice, resolution.

### Chapter 27 — The Choice
**POV:** Riven  
Riven processes the truth. Internal conflict peaks: love vs legacy vs self.

### Chapter 28 — The Threat to Greyhaven
**POV:** Cael  
Final crisis emerges. Cael sees it as purpose fulfilled.

### Chapter 29 — Surrender
**POV:** Riven  
Climactic decision. Riven gives in—not from belief, but inevitability.

### Chapter 30 — Velorum Holds
**POV:** Lyra  
Aftermath. They are unified, but no longer themselves. Statue scene. Final emotional resolution.
